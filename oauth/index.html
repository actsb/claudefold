Channel Uploader Tools

Yadam Uploader and Gaseongbi Uploader are private tools used only by the owner of two YouTube channels, Korean Musical Folktales and 가성비리빙연구소. They upload the owner's own videos, set titles, descriptions, subtitles, thumbnails and publish times, read comments and channel analytics, and store the owner's own video files in the owner's Google Drive.

The tools are not offered to the public. No other person signs in, and no data is sold or shared.
