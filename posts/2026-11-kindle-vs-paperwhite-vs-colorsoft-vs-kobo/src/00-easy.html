Which e-readers show ads, and what does removing them cost?

The base Kindle and base Paperwhite show lockscreen ads; the Colorsoft, the Paperwhite Signature Edition and the aluminum 32 GB Kindle do not; removing ads later costs about $20.

Amazon's help page for removing ads gives the route: Manage Your Content and Devices, pick the device, then under Special Offers choose Remove offers, and a pop-up shows the price before you pay; it states no amount. The-ebook-reader (November 3, 2026) puts the fee at $20 on the plastic models, and says the Signature Edition and Colorsoft models are ad-free by default; Tom's Guide says the same of the Colorsoft Signature. Good e-Reader gave $25 and said the Signature Edition is also sold with ads; Best Buy's listing of the 2026 Signature Edition describes it as ad-free, so we go with that. Amazon customer service stopped removing ads for free in September 2024. A free Lockscreen Ads setting filters unsuitable ads but does not remove them. Kobo has no lockscreen ads on the US models we found, though Good e-Reader reported a Kobo Plus promotion screen on older Kobos in other countries, in a post we could not date.

What do the Kindle's AI features do, and can you turn them off?

Ask this Book answers questions about the book you are reading, Recaps and Story So Far summarize it, and Amazon documents no off switch for any of them.

Amazon says Ask this Book "lets you ask questions about the book you're reading and receive spoiler-free answers", for thousands of US English-language titles (Amazon, updated July 2, 2026). An Amazon spokesperson told Reactor that the feature is always on and authors cannot opt titles out (Reactor, December 12, 2025). Story So Far needs Kindle software 5.19.4.0.1 and is US only (the-ebook-reader, June 9, 2026). Prof. H's lab checked six Kindle user guides (October 5, 2026) and found no setting to disable any of the three; the workarounds are not to open them, to pick "Reading so far" instead of "Full book", to use Clear History (Amazon clears it after 90 days idle) and, for Recaps, Airplane Mode, which the lab did not test. Two cautions. We could not confirm that Ask this Book is live on the 2026 e-ink Kindles yet: the November 10 Colorsoft review mentions no AI feature. And Kobo has shipped no reading AI that we could find as of mid-November 2026, though its Writing Life publishing platform uses AI to screen submissions (The Next Web, June 26, 2026).

Which is better for library books, Kindle or Kobo?

Kobo, if you want to borrow on the device itself; Kindle works with US libraries, but the loan starts in the Libby app on another device.

Libby says most library books can be read on a Kindle in the US only, via Shelf, Loans, Read With, Kindle, then finishing on Amazon (Libby help). Borrowing directly on the Kindle stopped working in May 2025, so you need a phone, tablet or computer (the-ebook-reader, May 21, 2025). Kobo's help page lists the Clara BW, Clara Colour and Libra Colour as Libby devices: scan a QR code with your phone, then borrow on the e-reader; audiobooks and magazines are not supported on Kobo. Kobo is also the easier place to read EPUBs you own. Kindle converts them through Send to Kindle, and Amazon removed its "Download and Transfer via USB" option on February 26, 2025. In November 2026 Kobo's 6.0 beta added multiple library cards, but on its Canadian site, and its Send to Kobo upload needs a Kobo Stack subscription that is not sold in the US (the-ebook-reader, November 5, 2026).

How do you set up an e-reader without the extras?

Check the listing for ads, choose how you will borrow books, and use the return window to test the screen edges and battery.

  1. Read the listing for "No Ads". Base Kindles and Paperwhites carry lockscreen ads; the aluminum 32 GB Kindle and the Signature Editions are sold without them.
  2. Pick your library route. On a Kobo, My Account, Settings, Libby, then scan the QR code with your phone and sign in with your library card number and PIN. On a Kindle, borrow in the Libby app and send the loan to your Kindle.
  3. Use "Reading so far", not "Full book", if you open Ask this Book, because Amazon's guide warns full-book answers may contain spoilers.
  4. Clear History in the Ask this Book panel now and then; Amazon clears it after 90 days idle.
  5. Open dark mode and look at the edges in a dim room in the first weeks. Light bleed is the 2026 defect owners report; return or exchange a bad unit.
  6. Read update notes before updating, on Wi-Fi with a full charge. Both brands had updates in 2026 that caused real trouble.

Quick answers

Kindle vs Paperwhite vs Colorsoft: which should I buy in 2026?

Most readers should buy the Paperwhite ($199.99 at Target, November 13, 2026). Pick the basic Kindle ($149.99) only to save money, and the Colorsoft ($289.99) only if colour matters; Tom's Guide and the-ebook-reader prefer the Paperwhite for text.

Why did Kindle prices go up in 2026?

Amazon raised prices by $40 to $50 on August 21, 2026, citing "significant increases in memory and storage component costs" (via Fortune and TechCrunch). The new lineup launched in October at the higher prices.

Paperwhite 2026 vs 2024: what changed?

The 2026 model is thinner and lighter, and the-ebook-reader found the screen slightly better but no faster. TechRadar notes the 2024 models cost the same as the 2026 ones, so there is no reason to buy last year's. Wireless charging is gone on the Signature Edition.

Is the Kindle without ads worth it, and what does removal cost?

One tester puts removal at about $20 on the plastic models and says Amazon's pop-up shows the exact price; Amazon's help page states no amount. If ads would bother you, the aluminum 32 GB Kindle, the Signature Edition and every Colorsoft come without them.

What is Kindle+, and is it Kindle Unlimited?

Yes: Amazon renamed Kindle Unlimited to Kindle+ on October 22, 2026. It costs $11.99 a month and is not included with Prime (Amazon, October 22, 2026).

What does Ask this Book do, and can I turn it off?

It answers questions about the book you are reading. Amazon says it is always on, and Prof. H's lab found no off switch in six Kindle user guides (October 5, 2026); picking "Reading so far" and clearing history are the workarounds.

Do Kindle recaps spoil the book?

Amazon designs Recaps and Story So Far to avoid spoilers beyond your place, but its guide warns that "Full book" answers in Ask this Book may contain spoilers. Story So Far needs software 5.19.4.0.1 and is US only.

Kindle vs Kobo for library books?

Kobo borrows library books on the device through Libby, in the US. A Kindle needs the Libby app on another device to start the loan, and Libby audiobooks and magazines do not work on Kobo.

Kobo Clara Colour vs Kindle Colorsoft: which is better?

The Kobo Clara Colour is $179.99 at Kobo and Target; the Colorsoft is $289.99. Tom's Guide called the Clara Colour the best Colorsoft alternative (April 13, 2026), while Engadget found Kobo's store and software weaker than Amazon's.

Is a new Kobo e-reader coming in 2026?

None has been announced or filed as of November 13, 2026. Kobo launched Kobo Stack ($2.99 a month in the US) and an OS 6 beta on November 11, which looks like what its CEO hinted at.
