What do long-term owners actually say?

Fit and battery decide whether owners keep a pair far more than sound does; the pairs they keep are the ones that stay in the ear and pair without drama.

  • Loved: a fit you forget about. Fit is what happy owners mention first in forums and long-term tests — and it's the thing you can fix with the tips in the box.
  • Loved: the noise cancelling on a plane. Bose and Sony owners describe the engine hum simply disappearing.
  • Loved: iPhone owners love the AirPods' instant pairing and Find My; Galaxy and Pixel owners say the same about their own brand's buds.
  • Complained: one earbud stops charging. Clean the case contacts monthly, and buy from Amazon itself (not a marketplace seller) so the warranty is simple.
  • Complained: they fall out on runs. Try every tip size, do the fit test, and if it still happens buy ear hooks (Powerbeats) or open-ear (Shokz).
  • Complained: the battery fades after about two years — on every brand. Batteries in earbuds can't be replaced, so buy on sale and plan on it.

Six words on the box, in one line each

  • ANC — active noise cancelling: microphones listen to the noise around you and the earbuds cancel it. Great for planes and trains; less magic for voices.
  • Transparency — the opposite: lets the outside in so you can hear a barista or a bike bell without taking them out.
  • Multipoint — connected to your laptop and your phone at the same time. If you take calls at a desk, you want it.
  • Codec (AAC, LDAC, aptX) — how music is squeezed over Bluetooth. Only matters if you pay for hi-res streaming and have an Android phone.
  • IP rating — sweat and rain resistance. IPX4 is fine for the gym; IP55 and up for rain and dust.
  • Open-fit vs. in-ear — open-fit (AirPods 5) rests in the ear with no tip; in-ear seals the canal for better bass and noise cancelling; open-ear (Shokz) sits outside it entirely.

Quick answers

Do AirPods work with Android?

As ordinary Bluetooth earbuds, yes. You lose the instant pairing, the settings, Find My and most of the tricks. On Android, buy the Sony, the Pixel Buds or the Galaxy Buds instead.

Should I wait for the AirPods 5?

If you want an open fit with real noise cancelling for $129 (Apple's list price), yes — they ship September 18. If you want the best AirPods, the Pro 3 (list $249, often under $200 on sale) is the better buy.

Are earbuds under $100 actually good?

Yes. The Soundcore Liberty 4 NC and the EarFun Air Pro 4+ give you good noise cancelling, 8 hours and an app. What you give up is call quality and the last 20% of sound and ANC.

How do I stop them falling out?

Try every tip size (most people need a different size in each ear), run the fit test in the app, and twist them in slightly. If they still move, you want ear hooks (Powerbeats Pro 2) or an open-ear design (Shokz).

How do I avoid fake AirPods on Amazon?

Buy only when the listing says "Ships from Amazon, Sold by Amazon" (or Apple). Check the serial number at Apple's coverage page when they arrive. A Pro 3 price far below the usual sale band is the tell.

When is the best time to buy?

Prime Big Deal Days (October 6–7) and Black Friday. The AirPods Pro 3 have sold for 20–30% below list in past sales, and Sony, Bose and Samsung typically drop $50–$80. Anything near 30% off the Pro 3 is a buy-now price.
