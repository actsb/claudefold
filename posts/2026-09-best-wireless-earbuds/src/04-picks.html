The 12 best wireless earbuds of 2026

Ranked by use case, because the best earbuds for a Galaxy owner who runs trails are the wrong earbuds for an iPhone owner who flies weekly. Every pick lists the manufacturer's price and the sale price we've seen this year; buy at the latter. Battery figures are with ANC on.

Best overall Sony WF-1000XM6

IMAGE SLOT — Amazon SiteStripe → Image → Sony WF-1000XM6, then delete this box.
List price: $329.99
Seen on sale: $279–$299
ANC tier: 1 (88% average reduction, SoundGuys)
Battery: 8h claimed, 9h41m measured; 3-min charge ≈ 45–60 min
Codecs: AAC, LDAC, LC3 (LE Audio)
Extras: multipoint, Speak-to-Chat, Adaptive Sound Control, 360 Reality Audio, AI beamforming mics
Works fully on: iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac
Water resistance: IPX4

The XM6 is what happens when a company fixes exactly the things reviewers complained about. The XM5's slippery fit and middling call quality are gone: the buds are shaped to lock in, the microphone array is the biggest step forward of any 2026 flagship, and the noise cancelling — measured at an 88% average reduction across the spectrum — lands within a hair of Bose while sounding, in What Hi-Fi?'s words, "wonderfully natural, musical." SoundGuys ran the battery to 9 hours 41 minutes with ANC on against Sony's 8-hour claim. And every one of those features works identically whether your phone is an iPhone, a Galaxy or a Pixel, which is the reason it beats the AirPods Pro 3 for the top spot: it is the best pair for the most people.

The honest caveats: at $329.99 it is the second-most expensive pair here and rarely drops below $279; the case is bulky; Sony ships fewer ear-tip sizes than Apple; and the touch controls still register the occasional adjustment as a tap. If you own an XM5, SoundGuys' advice is right — wait for a sale, the upgrades are focused rather than dramatic.

Pros

  • Tier-1 ANC, best-in-class sound balance
  • 9+ measured hours with ANC
  • Big mic upgrade; excellent calls
  • Every feature on every platform
  • Deepest app in the category

Cons

  • $329.99 list; slow to discount
  • Bulky case
  • Limited ear-tip options
  • Touch-control mis-taps

Most common owner complaint: accidental touch-control taps. Who should skip it: iPhone owners who will never leave Apple (the Pro 3 costs $130 less on sale and integrates better) and XM5 owners.

Best for iPhone Apple AirPods Pro 3

IMAGE SLOT — Amazon SiteStripe → Image → Apple AirPods Pro 3.
List price: $249
Seen on sale: $199 (September); $169 record low
ANC tier: 1 (Apple: 2× the Pro 2)
Battery: 8h with ANC
Health: heart-rate sensing; hearing test + hearing-aid mode (FDA-cleared)
Fit: 5 sizes of foam-infused tips; IP57
Extras: Live Translation, Adaptive Audio, Conversation Awareness, Find My
Works fully on: iPhone (basic audio elsewhere)

MKBHD called them "the first AirPods that have ever stayed in my ears," and that redesigned fit — smaller buds, a new angle, foam-infused tips in five sizes — is the headline. Everything else follows from the seal it creates: noise cancelling that Apple rates at twice the Pro 2 and the labs place in the top tier, deeper bass, eight hours with ANC on, and an IP57 rating that makes them proper gym earbuds. The heart-rate sensor works with Apple Fitness; the hearing test and hearing-aid mode (for mild-to-moderate loss, US-cleared) are features no rival offers; and on an iPhone the pairing, switching, transparency and Find My experience is still the benchmark. Long-term reviewers (AppleInsider at three months, several six-month YouTube reviews) say they got better with updates — including the static/crackle some early owners reported.

At $249 they are a strong buy; at the $199 Amazon has charged for most of September, and the $169 they hit in June, they are the obvious one for anyone with an iPhone. Their only real limits are on Android, where you get no app and none of the features above, and the absence of true multipoint (they auto-switch between your Apple devices instead).

Best noise cancelling Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds (2nd Gen)

IMAGE SLOT — Amazon SiteStripe → Image → Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds (2nd Gen).
List price: $299
Seen on sale: $249
ANC tier: 1 — the deepest measured
Battery: 6h with ANC
New in Gen 2: wireless charging, better mics, Bluetooth 5.4 + LE Audio, refined ANC
Extras: Immersive Audio (head-tracked), multipoint, CustomTune fit calibration
Works fully on: all platforms
Water resistance: IPX4

If the reason you are buying earbuds is to make an aircraft cabin, a subway car or an open-plan office disappear, buy these. Every lab we read still puts Bose's noise cancelling first, and the second generation — "boring updates aren't necessarily bad ones," as SoundGuys put it — added the things the first should have had: wireless charging, a much better microphone array, Bluetooth 5.4 with LE Audio, and software tweaks that make the ANC behave more gracefully with wind and voices. CustomTune plays a chirp at each power-on to calibrate the sound and ANC to your ear, and the bulb-and-fin fit is among the most comfortable for larger ears. The costs of that silence are a six-hour battery, a large case, and a sound signature that is pleasant rather than revealing — good, not Sony-good.

Best open-fit Apple AirPods 5

IMAGE SLOT — Amazon SiteStripe → Image → Apple AirPods 5.
Price: $129; $149 with Wireless Charging Case (adds swipe volume, +1h battery)
Availability: pre-orders open; ships September 18, 2026
ANC: standard on both models; up to 50% more noise removed than AirPods 4 with ANC (Apple)
Battery: 4h ANC on / 6h off ($129); 5h / 7h ($149); 20–22h with case
Chip / rating: H2, IP57, 4.3 g per bud
Extras: Adaptive Audio, Conversation Awareness, Live Translation, Find My

The AirPods 5 exist for the large minority of people who cannot stand a silicone tip in their ear canal, and for them there has never been a better option: an open, tip-free fit that most ears tolerate all day, with real noise cancelling for the first time at this price, Apple's transparency and Adaptive Audio, and the same H2 chip and IP57 rating as the Pro line. The trade-offs are the ones physics dictates for any open design: no seal means less bass and less ANC depth than the Pro 3, and four hours with ANC on ($129) or five ($149) is the shortest runtime in this guide. Spend the extra $20 for the wireless case — the swipe volume control and the extra hour are worth it. And if you can tolerate tips, the Pro 3 at $199 is the better earbud by a wide margin.

ANC tier: 3 (good hybrid ANC for the money)
Battery: 8h with ANC; wireless charging case

SoundGuys' repeat pick under $100 "wins or ties in nearly every category" of the budget field, and the reason is a feature list that reads like a 2023 flagship: hybrid ANC that genuinely cuts a commute, LDAC hi-res, wireless charging, multipoint, eight hours with ANC, and Soundcore's excellent app with a personalized-EQ hearing test. Best for sound quality Technics EAH-AZ100 (and Sennheiser Momentum True Wireless 4)

IMAGE SLOT — Amazon SiteStripe → Image → Technics EAH-AZ100.
Technics AZ100: $299; 7.5h ANC, 25h total; magnetic-fluid driver; 3-device multipoint
Sennheiser MTW4: $299.95; 7.5h, 30h total; aptX Lossless
ANC tier: 2 (Technics) / 3 (Sennheiser)
Weak spot (both): call quality

These two are for people who listen more than they talk. The AZ100 is "engagingly musical" with excellent controls and comfort for most ears (What Hi-Fi?, TechRadar), and its three-device multipoint is unique; its fit is mediocre for some ears and its call quality is, in SoundGuys' words, "surprisingly poor." The Momentum True Wireless 4 is the pair several reviewers call the best-sounding earbuds they've heard, with aptX Lossless on supported Android phones — paired with average ANC and lacklustre mics. If you want 90% of this sound with far better calls and ANC, the Sony XM6 is the sensible answer; if the last 10% matters to you, you already know it.

Who should skip them: anyone who takes calls on them.

Best for workouts Beats Powerbeats Pro 2

Ear hooks are the only design that never moves during burpees, and the Powerbeats Pro 2 pair them with Apple's H2 chip — so on an iPhone they pair, switch and locate like AirPods — while also shipping a proper Android app, which AirPods don't. The heart-rate sensor is the real thing: DC Rainmaker, who tests these against chest straps, came away impressed. Eight hours with ANC, a custom dual-layer driver and a big improvement in sound over the original round it out. The case is enormous and several reviewers hit pairing glitches on iOS at launch, most since patched.

Who should skip it: anyone who wants a pocketable case.

Best open-ear Shokz OpenFit 2+

IMAGE SLOT — Amazon SiteStripe → Image → Shokz OpenFit 2+ (ASIN B0DYCJSZLS).
List price: $199.95 (OpenFit 2 without Dolby/wireless charging: $179.95, seen at $139.95)
Battery: 11h per charge, 48h with case
Design: air-conduction open-ear, ear hook, IP55
ANC: none (by design); multipoint; Dolby Audio (2+)

Open-ear earbuds sit in front of the canal rather than in it, so you hear cars, coworkers and kids while you listen — and unlike bone conduction, the OpenFit 2's dual drivers sound like real earbuds, with bass. TechRadar's verdict on the 2+ was "better than Bose" at a fraction of the Ultra Open's $299, and 11 hours per charge is the longest runtime in this guide. They leak sound at high volume, so they are wrong for a quiet office, and there is no isolation at all. The step-down OpenFit 2 at $139.95 gives up Dolby and wireless charging and nothing else that matters; the Soundcore AeroFit 2 Pro ($179.99) is the value alternative.

Who should skip it: commuters and open-office workers who need isolation.

  • Sony WF-1000XM5 — now discounted below $200 in sales; 85% of the XM6 for far less, if you don't mind the older fit and mics. See on Amazon
  • AirPods 4 with ANC — only at a clearance price under $120 now that the AirPods 5 replace them with better ANC for $129/$149.
  • Skip the "75-hour battery, 2026 new, ENC noise cancelling" earbuds that sit atop Amazon's search results at $25–$40: the battery number is the case, "ENC" is a call-mic feature (not ANC), and the reviews go from five stars in week one to "left bud dead, seller gone" by month four. Spend $20 more on the P31i or JLab.
  • Skip AirPods from any seller other than Amazon.com, Apple, Best Buy, Target or Walmart — counterfeits with cloned serial numbers are the #1 AirPods complaint; see the checks in the tips section.
