## Buying-guide fundamentals
- LiDAR standard above ~$300; camera hybrids for obstacle recognition.
- Self-empty dock premium $150–$200; bags $20–$40/yr; filters $15–$30 per set every 6–12 months; 3-year consumables $100–$200 (self-empty).
- Value peak $300–$1,000 in 2026.

## Owner complaint themes (qualitative tally)
Hair tangles; getting stuck (chairs, cords, rugs, thresholds); dock clogs; mop odor; navigation quirks / keep-out zone violations; battery below claims; app/Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz; customer support (Ecovacs most cited; iRobot in transition).
