When the floor gets cold, an older dog gets stiffer, and a bed becomes the cheapest thing you can do about it. The Furhaven orthopedic sofa bed in Large (36 x 27 inches, rated up to 55 lb) cost $39.99 to $56.99 at Target and PetSmart on October 19, 2026, against $224.10 for a Casper Large at Target the same day. It is the value pick for small and medium dogs, not a Big Barker substitute: the foam softens under heavy dogs and the zipper is the weak point. We read owner reports at retailers other than Amazon, vets and tests to show who should buy it, who should not, and where it is cheapest.

What they praise

  • A good price for a washable bed: one Target reviewer (Apr 27, 2025) said two beds each lasted about a year indoors and a third a year on a deck.
  • The cover washes easily: a Target owner (May 14, 2024) sun-dries the insert to freshen it, and a 2022 Woot forum owner with beds for two small dogs reported no loss of loft and covers that washed well.

What they complain about

  • Zippers: three of the twenty newest Target reviews we could read mention one (1 star on Oct 19, 2025: broke after the first wash; Aug 9, 2025: broke when washed).
  • Flat or thin: a 1-star on Sep 5, 2025 said the bed arrived very flat and never fluffed out; a Chewy summary says the egg-crate foam is “highly compressible” (undated). The long-term test below saw the same softening.
  • Runs small: a 4-star Target review (Jun 3, 2024) said the Large felt like a Medium, and the sleep surface is smaller than the outside size, about 28 x 23 inches on the Large by one summary (UNVERIFIED).

The questions people ask before buying

  • Will it flatten? Under a 65 to 70 lb dog, yes, noticeably within a year (PerfectDogBed one-year test, Dec 2, 2025, an affiliate blog). A 95 lb Rottweiler owner commented on that page (Mar 22, 2024) that it went through the foam in months, though we cannot verify the comment.
  • Is it waterproof? No. The liner is not waterproof (Target spec, Oct 19, 2026), so add a waterproof pad for an incontinent dog.
  • Will it smell? One Walmart summary mentions a chemical odor that lasted months on a single unit; we found no such report at Target. Let it air out.

How do you use the Furhaven bed right?

Give it a few days to expand before you judge it, and wash only the cover.

  1. Measure first. Furhaven says to measure the dog lying in its usual sleep position and to size up if you are between sizes.
  2. Unbox it early. It ships compressed, and some owners report days to weeks before it fully expands.
  3. Put it somewhere warm and not slippery. VCA Hospitals (updated Jun 19, 2026) says cold, damp conditions can worsen stiffness, and that slippery floors make rising harder, so put a non-skid rug or mat under the bed.
  4. Wash the cover cold and gently. Furhaven says to unzip it, remove the foam, wash cold with unscented detergent, no bleach, and air dry or tumble on low (care page, Jan 26, 2024).
  5. Spot-clean the foam. A damp cloth and a little detergent, and let it dry fully; Furhaven says not to machine wash it.
  6. Check it every six months. If the dog sinks to the floor, size up or replace it.

What do the experts say?

Vets and testers agree a supportive bed helps comfort, but none of them say a bed treats arthritis, and none tested the Sofa style we recommend.

  • Consumer Reports (January 2022): describes egg-crate and memory foam as supportive for tender joints, notes memory foam tends to trap heat while egg crate stays cooler, and suggests a machine-washable, removable cover.
  • Consumer Reports (updated June 3, 2025): called the Furhaven Deluxe Chaise Lounge “the clear favorite” in its evaluations. That is a different Furhaven style, not the Sofa bed.
  • VCA Hospitals (updated June 19, 2026): suggests an orthopedic or memory-foam bed to reduce pressure on sore joints, keeping the dog warm and dry, and non-skid surfaces on slippery floors.
  • American Kennel Club (updated March 4, 2026): says orthopedic beds benefit many dogs, not only seniors, that large dogs need room to stretch, and that a removable washable cover is ideal.
  • Vetstreet (updated May 24, 2024): picked the Furhaven NAP Deluxe as best for large breeds and Big Barker as best overall, with price as Big Barker's main drawback; its method is research and testing, not a lab.

Why buy it on Amazon, and when isn't that the cheapest?

Amazon's strength is the size and colour range on one listing; for the lowest price, check Target, Chewy and Walmart for the exact style first.

  • Same bed, different styles. The Quilted Large was $39.99 at Target on Oct 19, 2026, while the thicker Plush & Suede was $54.99 to $56.99. Compare the same style before you compare prices.
  • Look at who sells it. Furhaven's warranty for orders from other retailers goes back to the original seller (FAQ, Oct 19, 2026), and Petco listings say unauthorized resellers may not be covered.
  • Know the returns. Target offers free 30-day returns on beds it ships; the listing you pick shows its window. A bulky compressed-foam bed is easy to send back only if you keep the box.
  • Skip look-alikes. Walmart marketplace and Chewy sell similar foam sofas from other brands at $37 to $75, but we found no owner evidence for most of them and no Temu or AliExpress listings we could check.

What's the catch?

The foam is soft: it suits light dogs, not heavy ones.

Egg-crate foam is comfortable and cheap, but it compresses, and Furhaven offers no shape warranty. The Better Business Bureau lists the company at C+ with 11 complaints in three years (checked Oct 19, 2026), mostly about slow warranty replies in the snippets we saw.

  • Dogs over 55 lb flatten it fastest: buy a Jumbo, or spend more on a Big Barker.
  • The zipper is the weak part: wash gently, and expect to replace the cover or the bed after a year or two of heavy use.
  • The liner is not waterproof: add a waterproof pad if your dog has accidents.

Quick answers

What size Furhaven dog bed should I buy?

Measure your dog lying in the usual sleep position and size up if you are between sizes (Furhaven FAQ, read Oct 19, 2026). The Large is 36 x 27 inches and its size guide says up to 55 lb, although one Target spec line also says Medium (26 to 50 lb). Dogs over 55 lb belong in a Jumbo or Jumbo Plus.

Does the Furhaven foam flatten?

Yes, under heavier dogs. A one-year blog test (published Dec 2, 2025) with 65 to 70 lb dogs found the foam noticeably thinner and the bolsters flattened, and expected one to two years of heavy use. We found no measured data, only owner reports, so treat it as a one-to-two-year bed for big dogs and longer for small ones.

Is the Furhaven bed real memory foam?

Not the Sofa style we recommend. It uses an egg-crate base, which Consumer Reports (Jan 2022) describes as supportive and cooler than memory foam. Furhaven also sells memory-foam and cooling-gel versions, which cost more.

Can I put the Furhaven cover in the washing machine?

Yes, the cover, not the foam. Furhaven says to unzip it, wash cold with unscented detergent and no bleach, and air dry or tumble low (care page, Jan 26, 2024). Wash gently: some Target reviewers report a zipper breaking at the first wash.

Is the Furhaven bed chew-proof?

No. Furhaven's FAQ says it does not make chew-resistant beds, and PetSmart's listing says the bed is not suitable for excessive teething or destructive chewing. A puppy or a digger will get through the cover.

How long does the bed take to puff up?

It ships compressed, and a 1-star Target review (Sep 5, 2025) said it arrived flat and never fully fluffed, while Chewy reviewers describe days to weeks. Give it several days and a shake before you decide to return it.

Is it good for an arthritic dog?

It can add padding and warmth on a cold floor, which VCA Hospitals (updated Jun 19, 2026) says helps stiff joints. No source we read says a bed treats arthritis, so keep your vet in the plan.

Furhaven vs Big Barker: which should I buy?

Furhaven for dogs under 55 lb or a tight budget. Big Barker for dogs over 50 lb: its Large is 48 x 30 x 7 inches and it carries a 10-year shape warranty, but it costs roughly five times as much. We found no independent test of the two side by side.

What is the Furhaven warranty and return policy?

Furhaven lists a 60-day Worry-Free replacement for accidents and a 90-day defect guarantee, and orders from other retailers go back to the original seller (FAQ, Oct 19, 2026). Target offers free 30-day returns on beds it ships.

Is there a cheaper way to get the same bed?

Sometimes. Chewy and Walmart showed some Large styles in the mid-$30s to $50s in search snippets we could not date, and Target ran the Quilted Large at $39.99 on Oct 19, 2026. Check the exact style first, because the thin ones are not the same build as the Plush and Suede.
