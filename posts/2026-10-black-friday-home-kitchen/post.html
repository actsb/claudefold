Why is the Bissell Little Green our top pick?

It is the product with the best paper trail of a real Black Friday-season cut, it solves a problem every household has, and the worst thing owners say about it is that it is loud.

Spot cleaners are a good Black Friday buy because the discount is large against a modest list price and the machine earns its keep on the first spill. Walmart listed the Little Green 1400B at $78.97 on November 3, 2025, according to a Slickdeals thread, and a Walmart+ early-access price of $78 (was $89) ran that month, per TechRadar. A Walmart brand page showed it at $95.99, down from $124.99, on a date we could not confirm. Put together, under $90 is a price we have seen, and $79 is one worth waiting for.

The reviewers are not starry-eyed. Reviewed.com found it easy to use and strong on tough stains, but messy and poor on large areas, and hair can clog the hose. Walmart's page showed 4.4 out of 5 from about 1,600 reviews (snapshot, undated). Our full Bissell Little Green review covers the 1400B in depth. The short version: buy it for the couch, the stairs and the car seat, not for the living room carpet.

What do unhappy owners say?

  • It is loud, and the hose is short.
  • It leaves fabric damp; owners tell each other to run it dry and open a window.
  • One eBay buyer said it mostly left the carpet wet. Other models (the HydroSteam) draw reports of lost steam after months, but those are a different machine.

Is Black Friday really the cheapest time?

For appliances and vacuums it is a reliably good time, but several of this list's lowest prices came outside Black Friday week.

Adobe Analytics, as reported, found appliances averaged 20.2% off on Black Friday 2025 and furniture 18.6%, against 2.1% and 1.2% on November 1 (secondary report; we did not retrieve Adobe's original). Home Depot's list on NBC Select showed 32% off Ninja kitchen appliances and 39% off Shark vacuums, with the caveat that not every item in a brand is cut. Costco's Ninja Crispi was about 22% off (Eat This, November 2025).

The same year, three products on this page had their lows earlier. A Levoit Core 300-P was about $49 at Home Depot on November 6 and 26 (Slickdeals; prices varied from $24 to $99 by store). A KitchenAid Artisan was $242.29 at Lowe's on October 6. Costco's Tramontina set was $189.99 in October, and a commenter said it was $30 more than the year before. Consumer Reports adds that holiday kitchen deals can rival Black Friday prices. So the rule is a number, not a date: buy when the price reaches your target, in October or November.

What do the experts say?

Tested picks and shopping advice agree on three things: write a list, check the price history, and buy products that were good at full price.

How to shop the sale

  • The FTC advises making a list and a budget, comparing prices and being wary of high percent-off claims because the original price may be inflated. It also says doorbusters may be excluded from extra percent-off codes and recommends paying by credit card for dispute protection (FTC, November 2019).
  • Google says its Shopping price insights label a price as low, typical or high, show up to 90 days of history, and offer price-tracking alerts; it also says the insights do not predict future prices (Google blog).
  • Consumer Reports flags deals mainly on products it already recommends and notes that holiday kitchen deals can rival Black Friday prices (CR holiday coverage, 2025 season).

What the tests say about the products

  • Air fryers: America's Test Kitchen's winner is the Instant Vortex Plus 6-Quart (January 2026 update); RTINGS names the Cosori TurboBlaze best overall (2026).
  • Stand mixers: America's Test Kitchen's mid-price pick is the KitchenAid Classic 4.5 qt, and it says the Artisan Mini lacks torque for stiff dough (updated September 2025).
  • Stick vacuums: RTINGS names the Dyson V12 Detect Slim its best stick vacuum (2026). A Consumer Reports page says CR does not currently recommend any cordless stick vacuum because of battery reliability; we could not confirm that page's date, so check it before buying.
  • Cookware: America's Test Kitchen's co-winners are the All-Clad D3 10-piece and Made In 10-piece sets (2026), and it notes that you can skip sets and buy only the pans you need. Consumer Reports lists November and December as the best months to buy cookware.
  • Mops, pillows, heated throws: America's Test Kitchen's winning spin mop is the O-Cedar EasyWring. Consumer Reports' April 2026 pillow picks include the machine-washable Coop Sleep Goods Original.

How do you check a deal before you buy?

Spend five minutes comparing the model number, the price history and two other stores; that is the whole defense against a fake sale.

The checks come from FTC and Google shopping guidance; the habit people skip is matching the exact model number.
  1. Write your number before the sale. Use the target column above.
  2. Match the model number. A Levoit Core 300-P is not the 300S-P, and a deal on a Duo Plus is not a deal on a Duo.
  3. Look at the history. Google's price insights show the last 90 days and a low, typical or high label.
  4. Open two other stores. The brand's own store and Walmart often match each other.
  5. Read the return window. The extension to January is common, but Home Depot's stays at its normal 90 days.

Quick answers

When is Black Friday 2026?

Friday, November 27, 2026, the day after Thanksgiving; Cyber Monday is November 30. Retailers had not announced their Black Friday plans on October 9. In 2025 Walmart announced on October 27 and Best Buy on October 20.

What is the best home and kitchen deal to wait for?

Our top pick is the Bissell Little Green, with a Walmart price of $78.97 in November 2025. Under $90 is our target.

How deep do Black Friday appliance discounts go?

Adobe reported appliances averaging 20.2% off on Black Friday 2025, and named brands such as Ninja and Shark went 30% to 40% off at Home Depot (NBC Select). Dyson and KitchenAid are stingier.

Is it cheaper to buy before Black Friday?

Sometimes. In 2025 a Levoit Core 300-P, a KitchenAid Artisan and a Tramontina cookware set were all cheapest in October or early November at some stores.

Should I buy a cordless stick vacuum on Black Friday?

Only as a second vacuum. Consumer Reports says it does not currently recommend cordless sticks because of battery reliability, and owners report batteries dying near two years.

Is the Bedsure heated throw safe?

The CPSC recalled Bedsure electric heating blankets and pads, including model BS-HB5060, on March 9, 2023. Check the model number on the label against the recall before use.

Does a store price-match during Black Friday?

Often not. In 2025 Best Buy's holiday match excluded Black Friday and Cyber Monday event prices, Target matched only Amazon and Walmart, and Costco does not match but adjusts its own price within 30 days.

How long do I have to return a Black Friday purchase?

In 2025 Walmart returns ran to January 31 and Best Buy's to January 15; Home Depot kept its normal 90 days. The 2026 terms were not published when we checked.

Is a cookware set worth buying?

Sometimes. America's Test Kitchen suggests skipping sets and buying only the pans you need. If you want a full set, a $190 tri-ply stainless set was seen at Costco in October 2025.

How do I know a "was" price is real?

Match the exact model number and check Google's 90-day price history. The FTC warns that a large percent-off may sit on an inflated original price.
