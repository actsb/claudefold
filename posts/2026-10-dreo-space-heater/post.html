How do you use the Dreo space heater right?

Plug it straight into the wall and keep three feet clear: that is the habit that matters most.

Steps from the Dreo support page for the DR-HSH004 (Oct 15, 2026) and CPSC (Jan 2026); the step people skip is the cleaning.
  1. Use a wall outlet. CPSC says never plug a space heater into an extension cord or power strip, and Dreo's support page advises against cords too.
  2. Clear three feet. CPSC advises keeping heaters at least three feet from drapes, furniture and bedding.
  3. Set the thermostat near 68°F. The range is 41 to 95°F; the heater then cycles instead of running flat out.
  4. Use Eco mode for long sessions. Per Dreo, Eco stops heating (and oscillation) once the set temperature is reached.
  5. Turn it off when you sleep or leave. CPSC and NFPA both say so; the timer helps.
  6. Clean it each season. Unplug, let it cool, wipe with a dry cloth and brush dust off the air inlet. Dreo says no detergents.

What do the experts say?

Experts agree on the rules (wall outlet, three feet, tip-over shutoff) more than on any one brand.

  • CPSC (January 2026): says to plug heaters directly into a wall outlet, never an extension cord or power strip, to keep them at least three feet from anything combustible, and reports an average of about 1,600 fires and 70 deaths a year from portable heaters (2020 to 2022).
  • Consumer Reports (updated October 2025): tests how fast heaters warm a 204-square-foot room, notes a thermostat keeps the set temperature, and says oscillating heaters tend to be the noisiest.
  • U.S. Energy Information Administration (July 2026 data, preliminary): puts the average U.S. residential price at 18.31 cents per kWh, so 1,500 watts costs about 27.5 cents an hour at full power, or about $2.20 for eight hours (our arithmetic).

  • Tom's Guide (date not shown): is quoted in a search snippet as measuring 45.5 dB on high for the Vornado VH200, the quietest it tested, with no oscillation.

Quick answers

How much does it cost to run a 1500W heater?

About 27.5 cents an hour at full power, using the EIA's July 2026 average of 18.31 cents per kWh (preliminary). Eight hours at full power is about $2.20. The thermostat cycles the heater, so real use is lower.

Can I plug it into an extension cord?

No. CPSC (Jan 2026) says to plug electric heaters directly into a wall outlet, never an extension cord or power strip, and Dreo's support page advises against cords too.

How big a room does it heat?

Plan on about 150 to 200 square feet. Dreo's listings claim up to about 200; commercial sizing guides use 150. One reviewer said a heater advertised for 1,000 square feet struggled at 500.

Is it safe to leave on overnight?

CPSC and NFPA both say to turn space heaters off when you sleep or leave. Use the timer and keep it three feet from bedding.

Does the Atom One oscillate?

Dreo lists 70° oscillation for the Atom One (DR-HSH004) on dreo.com, Oct 15, 2026. A long-term blog says the HSH006 tower does not, so check the model number.

What does ECO mode do?

Per Dreo's support page, ECO stops heating once the set temperature is reached and restarts when the room cools. Oscillation stops in that state too.

What does the FF code mean?

Dreo's support page says heating stops and FF shows if the ambient temperature is above 50°C (122°F). Unplug it and restart after it cools.

How long is the warranty?

Dreo's terms say 12 months (read Oct 15, 2026). A 30-month extension appears only in some retailer listings, so we do not promise it.

Dreo, Vornado or Dyson?

Dreo for price and a remote, Vornado VH200 for quiet, Dyson only if you also want the purifier and fan. We found no test that compared all three directly.

Can I clean it with a damp cloth or spray?

No. Dreo says unplug it, let it cool and wipe with a dry soft cloth, with no detergents and no water near the motor, element or switches.
