Three reasons it's a best seller — and one catch

  • 1. Sip or chug, without swapping lids. Straw at the desk, gulp after the run. The patented spout is the whole product, and it's why owners say "I drink more water now" more than anything else.
  • 2. It doesn't leak in a bag. Push the button, flip the lid; close it, slide the loop over, and it's locked. Laptops everywhere are grateful.
  • 3. Cold for 24 hours and it survives concrete. Vacuum-insulated steel; owners report drops onto sidewalks with a dent and no loss of insulation.
  • The catch: the straw and the silicone gasket collect gunk if you don't take them apart — that's the mold complaint, and it's real. The stainless body is hand-wash only. And it is not for hot drinks; coffee people want a Stanley or a Yeti.

How to use it like a pro

  1. Pick the size by where it lives. 24 oz fits cup holders and lunch bags; 32 oz for the desk and the gym; 40 oz only if you never need a cup holder — or get the FreeSip Sway with the handle.
  2. Sunday ritual, two minutes: pull the gasket out with its little tab, lid and straw on the top rack of the dishwasher, body rinsed by hand. Mold never gets a chance.
  3. Lock it every time it goes in a bag. Loop over the button. Every leak review starts with "I forgot to lock it."
  4. Ice first, then water — the wide mouth takes big cubes, and 24-hour cold means the ice is still there at bedtime.
  5. Buy the colour you love when it hits ~$24–$28. Waiting for a deeper cut usually means watching it sell out.

Quick answers

Is it dishwasher safe?

Lid and straw, yes — top rack. The steel body is hand-wash.

Can I put coffee in it?

No. Owala says it's not for hot liquids. Stanley and Yeti are the hot-drink bottles.

Does it leak?

Not when the loop is locked over the button. Unlocked in a bag, it can.

Which size should I get?

24 oz for cup holders and kids; 32 oz for the desk and the gym; 40 oz only without a car — or the Sway with a handle.

How do I stop the mold?

Pull the gasket out with the tab, dishwash lid and straw, soak in vinegar if there's build-up. Two minutes a week.

FreeSip vs Sway vs Twist?

Same spout. The Sway adds a bucket handle (30 or 40 oz); the Twist swaps the flip lid for a twist cap.
