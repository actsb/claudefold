What exactly did the 2023 recall cover?

A controller that could malfunction, in blankets and pads sold in late 2022, including the 50×60 model number BS-HB5060.

Per the CPSC notice dated March 9, 2023, as relayed by news outlets and state agencies in search summaries, Bedshe International recalled about 350,000 Bedsure electric heating blankets and pads because the controller could malfunction, creating fire and burn hazards. Is a heated throw cheaper than a space heater?

For warming one person, yes by a wide margin; for warming a room, no, because they do different jobs.

The 2026 average US residential electricity price is about 17.9 cents per kilowatt-hour, per figures from the Energy Information Administration relayed in search summaries (check the latest in the EIA Electric Power Monthly, Table 5.6.A). A 1500-watt space heater draws 1.5 kilowatt-hours an hour, so it costs about 27 cents for each hour on high; an eight-hour evening is roughly $2.15. A throw drawing 50 to 100 watts (a typical range from third-party pages, not a Bedsure spec, so check your label) costs about a cent or two an hour. The throw wins when you sit still and the thermostat can stay low. A space heater wins when you need the air in the room warmer, for several people or for a pet. Many households use both, and neither replaces insulation.

How do you use the Bedsure heated throw right?

Check the label, connect the cords in the right order, and switch it off and unplug it when you are done.

  1. Read the model number first. It is on the back label, the side of the controller, or in your Amazon order history. Compare it with the CPSC recall list (the notice includes BS-HB5060) and use the refund route if it matches.
  2. Lay it flat and keep it clear. ESFI says not to fold or tuck a heated blanket while it is on, or to pile things on it.
  3. Connect the control cord to the blanket, then plug into the wall. Bedsure's guide ties the F2 error to doing it the other way round.
  4. Press Preheat, then lower the level. Preheat warms fast; owners report levels 3 or 4 are enough for a sofa.
  5. Know your auto-off. The listing for the Preheat throw we checked says 8 hours; other Bedsure throws stop at 3.
  6. Wash with the cords off. Unplug from the wall, detach the control cord, gentle cold wash; do not wring or iron. Your manual rules.

Quick answers

Is the Bedsure heated throw safe?

It carries an ETL mark per the listing, but Bedsure had a CPSC recall in March 2023 covering about 350,000 heated blankets and pads, including model BS-HB5060. Check your model number and use it as directed.

Which models were recalled?

Search summaries of the CPSC notice list blankets BS-HB5060, BS-HB6284, BS-HB7284 and BS-LV0A, and pads BS-HP2422, BS-HP1224 and BS-HP2433. Read the notice itself for the full list and the refund steps.

How big is the throw?

50×60 inches, which is lap-and-shoulders size. It will not cover a queen bed.

How much does it cost to run?

Can I leave it on overnight?

Some listings say 8 hours auto-off, but ESFI-style safety advice is to switch heated blankets off before sleep and never leave them on unattended.

Can I wash it?

What do the error codes mean?

Bedsure's guide describes F1, F2 and F3 as connection or cord problems and a blinking red light as no power or a broken wire. Reseat the cords and, if it persists, contact Bedsure or use Amazon's return window.
