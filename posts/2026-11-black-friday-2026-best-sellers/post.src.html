What is selling the most on Black Friday 2026?

Game consoles, Apple's small gadgets, half-price kitchen machines and a handful of viral toys: that is what Adobe's Thanksgiving data, Circana's console tracking, Walmart's merchandising chief and NBC Select's reader purchases all point to on November 26 and 27, 2026.

Full Black Friday sales figures are not out yet; Adobe usually publishes them the next day. What we have is Thanksgiving Day, which set a record. Adobe counted $7.1 billion in US online spending on November 26, up 10.4% from 2025 and above its own $6.9 billion forecast (Seeking Alpha, November 27). Between November 1 and 22 Americans spent $82.5 billion online, up 9.1%. Adobe forecasts $12.9 billion for Black Friday itself and $15.1 billion for Cyber Monday (Adobe's 2026 holiday forecast, September 28, 2026), and the NRF expected 182.7 million people to shop over the Thanksgiving weekend, 126 million of them on Black Friday.

Gaming was the breakout. Against October's daily average, Adobe saw console sales rise 1,058% on Thanksgiving, video games 817% and gaming accessories 595% (USA Today, November 27). Circana's tracking puts the Nintendo Switch 2 first among US consoles for October and for 2026 so far, with the PS5 second (VGChartz).

In stores, Walmart's US chief merchandising officer told Fox Business on Black Friday morning that NeeDoh sensory toys were more than 80% sold through by midday, and named Pokémon cards, TVs, home goods, slippers and pajamas as strong sellers. Online, NBC Select's list of what its readers bought most on Black Friday 2026 is led by the AirPods Pro 3 and AirTag, followed by the Cuisinart ClearView air fryer, the Owala FreeSip, the Roomba 105, the Nespresso Vertuo Pop and the Oral-B iO3. Is Black Friday really cheaper, or should you wait for Cyber Monday?

Black Friday is the better day for TVs, toys, appliances, apparel and furniture; Cyber Monday (November 30) is expected to be better for electronics and computers.

That is Adobe's 2026 holiday forecast, which also expects discounts to peak during Cyber Week at up to 30% for electronics, 29% for toys and 23% for TVs and computers, after 14% in early November and 21% just before Thanksgiving. Is Black Friday really cheaper than the rest of the year? Not always: the UK consumer group Which? tracked Black Friday prices in 2025 and found 83% of products were the same price or cheaper at other times, and 35% got cheaper afterward. UK prices are not US prices, but the lesson travels: compare with the lowest price of the past year, not with the "regular" price on the sign. How do you tell if a Black Friday deal is real?

Match the exact model number, compare with the item's lowest price of the past year, and ignore the crossed-out "regular" price.

Quick answers

When does Black Friday 2026 end?

Black Friday is Friday, November 27, 2026, and its one-day prices end at 11:59 p.m. local time, but most of the sales in this guide run through Cyber Monday, November 30. Some last longer: Nintendo's Switch 2 bundle price runs to December 5, and Meta's glasses sale to November 30.

What is selling the most on Black Friday 2026?

Game consoles led on Thanksgiving, with sales up 1,058% against October (Adobe), and the Nintendo Switch 2 is the best-selling console of 2026 (Circana). The AirPods Pro 3 and AirTag top NBC Select readers' purchases, and at Walmart NeeDoh toys were 80% sold through by midday on Black Friday.

Is Black Friday really cheaper?

Often, but not always. Which? found in 2025 that 83% of Black Friday products in the UK were the same price or cheaper at other times. Compare with the lowest price of the past year: AirPods Pro 3 at $179.99 and AirTag at $18 pass that test this year; the Switch 2 bundle does not beat 2025.

Is Cyber Monday better than Black Friday?

For electronics and computers, probably: Adobe expects them to be cheapest on Cyber Monday, November 30, 2026. TVs, toys, appliances, apparel and furniture are expected to be cheapest on Black Friday.

Are Black Friday TVs lower quality?

Some doorbuster TVs are store-specific versions with their own model numbers, and Consumer Reports has found mixed results with these models. Match the full model number with the one reviewers tested before you buy.

Is the Nintendo Switch 2 cheaper on Black Friday 2026?

The Mario Kart World bundle is $499.99, $50 off its new $549.99 list price, from November 22 to December 5. That is the same price stores charged on Black Friday 2025, so it is a fair price, not a new low.

Is there a PS5 Black Friday deal in 2026?

Not on the console: Sony and the big stores are not discounting the PS5 or PS5 Pro this year. The discounts are on accessories, such as $100 off PlayStation VR2 and $20 off DualSense controllers.

What is the best AirPods Black Friday deal?

AirPods Pro 3 at $179.99 at Target, $70 under the $249.99 regular price. Walmart had them at $199, and the base AirPods at $129.

When is Cyber Monday 2026?

Monday, November 30, 2026. Adobe forecasts $15.1 billion in US online spending that day, the biggest of the season.

What should you not buy on Black Friday?

Anything whose deal price only matches last year's, like this year's Switch 2 bundle, and resale-priced collectibles like a $116 Pokémon Elite Trainer Box. Laptops and other electronics may also be cheaper on Cyber Monday.
