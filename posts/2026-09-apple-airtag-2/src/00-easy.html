Vera's 10-second verdict

Why is the AirTag 2 so popular?

Apple quietly replaced the 2021 AirTag in January 2026 and changed exactly three things people actually complained about. The speaker is about 50% louder (one lab measured roughly 85 dB against 66 dB for the original — the difference between "is that my phone?" and "it's under the couch cushion, second from the left"). The new Ultra Wideband chip means the on-screen arrows lock on from up to 50% farther, and reviewers found the real-world jump bigger than Apple's claim. And for the first time you can point your Apple Watch at it — useful when the thing you've lost is the phone's usual neighbour.

Then Apple kept the price where it was: $29 for one and $99 for four (Apple's list prices at launch, January 2026). Since summer the 4-pack has been discounted again and again, usually by somewhere between 10% and 20%. A tracker that cheap per tag ends up on everything — keys, bags, the kid's backpack.

In real life: four moments

Three reasons it's our top pick for iPhone — and one catch

  • 1. It's the loudest it has ever been. The old AirTag's chirp got lost in a handbag. The new one you can hear from the next room. This alone is the reason owners upgraded.
  • 2. The arrows work from across the parking lot. Precision Finding is the party trick — your iPhone screen shows an arrow and a distance — and it now locks on from much farther away. Tom's Guide called it the most findable tracker it has tested.
  • 3. Hundreds of millions of iPhones are looking for it. There's no GPS inside. Any passing iPhone quietly relays its location to you. In a city, that means your suitcase updates every few minutes without anyone doing anything.
  • The catch: still no hole for a keyring, so budget $5–$15 for a holder. Still a coin battery — about a year, or 6–9 months if you play the sound a lot. And it is iPhone-only. Not "works better on iPhone." Only.

How to use it like a pro

  1. Checked bag? Open Find My → the AirTag → Share Item Location. You get a link that shows the airline where your bag actually is. It expires after 7 days.
  2. Name them honestly. "Keys", "Wallet", "Blue suitcase", "Emma's backpack". The Play Sound button is faster when you're not guessing.
  3. Turn on Notify When Left Behind for the wallet and the keys. Your phone taps you on the shoulder as you walk out of the café.
  4. Buy Energizer or Panasonic CR2032s, not Duracell — the bitterant coating on some Duracell cells can trigger false "low battery" alerts.
  5. Don't use it on the dog. There's no live GPS; updates depend on passing iPhones. For a pet, buy a GPS collar. For a cat that hides in the house, an AirTag on the collar is genuinely useful.

Quick answers

Do I need a new iPhone?

Any iPhone on iOS 26 or later. The on-screen arrows need an iPhone with Ultra Wideband — that's every iPhone 11 and newer except the SE.

Does it work with Android?

No. It won't pair. Android users should buy the Chipolo Pop (Google Find Hub) or the Samsung SmartTag2 (Galaxy phones).

How long does the battery last?

Apple says more than a year on a replaceable CR2032. Heavy users who play the sound a lot report six to nine months.

Is it waterproof?

IP67: rain, splashes and a brief dunk are fine. Not a swim.

Should I upgrade from the original AirTag?

Yes if you keep losing things in noisy places or want Apple Watch finding. Otherwise the original, often under $20 on sale, does the same job on the same network.

Can I use my old holders?

Yes — same size, same shape. Apple confirms every existing accessory fits.
