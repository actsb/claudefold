What do owners say after a month with smart glasses?

Battery life and privacy defaults are the complaints; the camera and the hands-free answers are what owners call addictive.

  • Loved: translation on holiday. Pointing at a menu and hearing it in English is the moment owners describe most.
  • Loved: hands-free photos of kids and dogs, and video calls from your own point of view.
  • Loved: the private cinema. Xreal and Viture owners say a 120-inch screen in an economy seat is worth it on its own.
  • Complained: the battery dies by mid-afternoon, and the always-listening AI drains it in half an hour. Carry the case.
  • Complained: being asked to take them off — gyms, courts, cinemas, a friend's living room. Camera-free glasses avoid it.

Six words on the box, in one line each

  • Camera glasses — a camera, mics, open-ear speakers and an AI you talk to. No screen; the answers are spoken. (Ray-Ban Meta, Oakley Meta, Rokid Style.)
  • Display glasses — the same, plus a small text screen in the lens for directions, captions and messages. Not for movies. (Meta Display, Rokid Glasses, Even G2.)
  • XR glasses — a big virtual screen from your phone, laptop or Steam Deck over a USB-C cable. No camera, no AI. (Xreal, Viture.)
  • Field of view — how big the virtual screen looks. 20° is a readout; 50°+ is a cinema.
  • Neural Band — Meta's wristband that reads your finger movements so you can scroll without touching anything.
  • Rx — prescription lenses. Ray-Ban Meta supports the widest range; the Meta Display only −4 to +4; XR glasses use snap-in inserts.

Quick answers

Should I wait for Meta Connect on September 23–24?

If you want Meta camera glasses, yes. The Gen 3 is expected, and the Gen 2 will be discounted either way. The Rokid, Even, Xreal and Viture pairs aren't due a refresh — buy those whenever.

Do they work with an iPhone?

The camera and display glasses all do, through their apps. XR glasses need a phone with video out over USB-C — iPhone 15 and later work; older Lightning iPhones don't.

Is it legal to record people?

In public in the US, generally yes; a recording light shows when you're filming. Indoors, ask. Courts, gyms, cinemas, casinos, clinics and exam halls ban them, and New York banned them from every courtroom this year.

Can I get prescription lenses?

Yes for every pair here, from the maker or a retrofit service. Budget $45–$210 for retrofit lenses, more at an optician. The Meta Display only fits −4 to +4.

Are the $20–$40 "AI glasses" on Amazon any good?

No. Reviewers found weak cameras, apps that stop working and no answer on where your footage goes. The floor for a real pair is the Rokid AI Glasses Style at $299 or the Echo Frames on sale.

How long does the battery really last?

Four to eight hours of normal use on camera glasses; about 30 minutes if the AI is watching continuously. The charging case adds a day or two. Display glasses last about six hours; the Even G2, with no camera, lasts days.
