14 questions people ask before buying smart glasses

Is it legal to record people with smart glasses?

In public places in the US, generally yes for video; audio is different — about a dozen states (California, Florida, Illinois, Washington among them) require everyone's consent to record a conversation. Private venues set their own rules, and a growing number ban camera glasses outright. The practical rule: don't record where people expect privacy, and tell people when you are.

Do smart glasses work with an iPhone?

Meta, Rokid, Even and Echo Frames: yes, through their apps, on iPhone and Android. XR glasses (Xreal, Viture): only USB-C iPhones — 15 and later — because they need DisplayPort output.

Can I get prescription lenses?

Yes on everything here except the cheapest no-name pairs, with limits: the Meta Ray-Ban Display supports −4.00 to +4.00 only; Ray-Ban Meta, Rokid and Even support broad ranges; XR glasses use snap-in inserts. Budget $45–$210 for a retrofit service or $200–$400 at an optician on top of the frames.

Can you see the display outdoors?

The Meta Ray-Ban Display is readable in direct sunlight (up to 5,000 nits); Rokid's and Even's green displays are readable outdoors but dimmer; XR glasses need their light shield or Viture's dimming lenses in bright light.

How long does the battery last, really?

Eight hours of light use on Gen 2 Ray-Bans and HSTN, nine on the Vanguard, six on the Display, about two days on the Even G2. Video and Live AI cut any of them to an hour or two. XR glasses have no battery and drain the device instead.

What's the difference between the Meta Ray-Ban Display and Xreal glasses?

The Display is a 20° heads-up readout for text, maps and captions with a camera and AI; Xreal is a 57° 1080p screen for video and apps with no AI or camera. One is a smartwatch on your face, the other is a TV. Nobody sells both in one pair.

Do the glasses work without the phone?

Meta and Rokid glasses can take photos and play music from onboard storage, but AI, translation and calls need the phone's connection. Even G2 needs the phone for most features. XR glasses need a device by definition.

Will I be asked to take them off?

Yes, if you wear camera glasses into a gym, courtroom, cinema, clinic, exam hall or casino, and increasingly in workplaces. Carry the case; or buy camera-free glasses if that's your week.

Is Meta watching what I record?

By default, voice recordings are stored (up to a year) and captures used with Meta AI can be used for training; a 2026 lawsuit alleges contractors reviewed owners' footage. You can disable the wake word, delete voice history and change data-sharing toggles — see the day-one checklist above. Rokid uses OpenAI/Google under their policies; Even's G2 has no camera.

Are the cheap "ChatGPT AI glasses" on Amazon any good?

No. Reviewers found 2K-at-best cameras, apps that stop working, no updates and no clear data policy. The Rokid AI Glasses Style ($269–$299) and Echo Frames ($120–$170 on sale) are the real floor.

Should I wait for Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3 or the Google glasses?

For Meta camera glasses, yes — Connect is September 23–24 and the Gen 2 will be discounted either way. For Android owners who want Gemini, the fall Android XR launch is worth waiting for unless you want the Rokid Glasses today. Display, Rokid, Even, Xreal and Viture aren't due refreshes.

Do they work with hearing aids or over prescription sunglasses?

Open-ear speakers sit above the ear, so they coexist with behind-the-ear hearing aids better than any earbud does, and several hearing-aid wearers in the Meta community threads say the Ray-Ban Meta is the first "headphone" they can actually use. The Nuance Audio glasses from EssilorLuxottica are a different product entirely: FDA-cleared over-the-counter hearing glasses with no camera, for mild to moderate loss. As for wearing smart glasses over your own sunglasses, no — every pair here is a frame, so you replace the frame and order lenses (clear, sun or transition) inside it.

What about warranty and returns?

Meta gives one year in the US and the Ray-Ban stores accept returns for 30 days if the lenses are stock; prescription lenses usually make the pair non-returnable, which is the single most common regret in the returns threads, so try a stock pair first. Amazon's 30-day window applies to the Xreal, Viture, Rokid and Even pairs sold there. Rokid's warranty runs through the seller, not Rokid US, and owners report two- to four-week turnarounds; Even Realities handles its own service from the US and was the best-rated after-sales experience in the threads we read.

Could I really use these to cheat on a test?

The exam video shows it works on printed problems in a quiet room — which is exactly why exam boards, schools and universities banned them this year and why Korea brought its first prosecution. Don't. The same capability, pointed at a menu in Lisbon, is the legitimate reason to buy them.

Final verdict

Decide the kind first. For camera-and-AI glasses, the Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2 is the finished product — buy it after Meta Connect on September 24, or the Rokid AI Glasses Style for $100 less and no Meta account. For a screen in your eye, the Meta Ray-Ban Display is the most polished and the Rokid Glasses are the value, with a display in both eyes and your choice of ChatGPT or Gemini; the Even Realities G2 is the one you can wear anywhere because it has no camera. For a private cinema, the Xreal One Pro for features and the Viture Luma Pro for the picture. For sport, the Oakley Meta Vanguard. Under $200, the Echo Frames on sale, and nothing else.

Whatever you buy: change the privacy defaults on day one, learn where you'll be asked to remove them, keep the case in your pocket, and treat the AI as a very good assistant for signs, objects and languages rather than a replacement for your phone. That — and a realistic battery expectation — is the entire difference between the owners who wrote the five-star reviews and the ones who returned them in week two.
