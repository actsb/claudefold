Is the Rabbitgoo No-Pull Harness worth it? (the walk)

For most dogs, yes: it is the $20–$26 harness that fits on the first try, with a front ring that turns a lunge into a sideways step. For a strong, reactive or escape-artist dog, spend more.

There is a harness in the background of every dog-park photo in America, and it is usually this one. Two leash rings (front for pulling days, back for good days), four adjustment sliders so the neck and the chest are set separately, a padded plate, reflective piping for the 5 a.m. crowd, and a handle on the back for curbs, vet scales and the moment a squirrel enters the chat. It goes on over the head with two buckle clicks, which is why owners of wriggly dogs love it, and it costs about what a bag of decent treats costs. A 2026 review that ran it daily on a 90-pound German Shepherd for six months reported minimal fraying and intact buckles, and "noticeably better stitching than the knockoffs" — the knockoffs being the point of this whole guide.

Fitting it in ninety seconds

  1. Measure before you order. Rabbitgoo's own guide says to measure the lower neck and the widest part of the chest just behind the front legs, and not to choose by weight, breed or age. Pick the size that puts your dog's chest number in the middle of the range, not at an edge. A 50-pound Jindo lands in Large.
  2. Loosen everything first. Unbuckle both sides and open all four sliders. Neck loop over the head, logo on top, bottom strap between the front legs, two clicks.
  3. Adjust chest, then neck, both sides equally. Lift the strap into an arch through the slider instead of pulling it flat; adjusting only one side tilts the whole harness.
  4. Two fingers, not a fist. Two fingers should slide flat under every strap. A hand means the dog can back out; nothing means the armpits will rub.
  5. Front ring on pulling days, back ring on good ones. The chest ring turns a forward lunge into a turn toward you; the back ring is for jogging and dogs that already walk on a loose leash. Hand wash, air dry — the reflective piping doesn't survive a machine.

The escape test, for Jindos, huskies and shibas

Spitz-type dogs back out of harnesses the way other dogs shake off water: reverse, drop the head, "Superman" the front legs, gone. Any two-strap harness leaves a loop to reverse through if it's loose, so do the test before every walk for the first week: hold the leash, step back, apply steady pressure. If the harness slides toward the ears, tighten the girth strap and seat it behind the widest part of the ribs, not up in the armpits. The trainer who tested the Rabbitgoo for months at Rebarkable called it cheap, adjustable and perfectly usable for average dogs, and specifically not the harness for strong, reactive or escape-prone ones. We agree, which is why the pricier alternative below is a three-strap design: a belly strap behind the ribcage closes the loop, and there is nothing left to reverse out of.

Not it? Two alternatives

  • Cheaper: the Voyager Step-In Air harness (about $10–$15) — the small-dog favourite, mesh, step-in, no front clip. Fine for a dog that doesn't pull; easier to back out of.
  • Pricier: the Rabbitgoo Escape Proof three-strap harness (about $25–$30) for the reversers, or the 2 Hounds Freedom No-Pull ($35–$45, made in the USA), the harness trainers name when a dog pulls like a sled team. Pair either with a fixed 5- or 6-foot leash: vets, the AKC and Consumer Reports all steer away from retractables, and a 2024 veterinary study counted more than 420,000 US emergency-room visits for leash-related injuries.

Is the KONG Classic still the best $12 in dog ownership? (enrichment)

Yes. It is the one toy vets and trainers name unprompted, it is made in Colorado from natural rubber, and stuffed and frozen it replaces a dog walker for twenty minutes at a time.

A red rubber snowman with a hole at each end, unchanged since 1976, made in Golden, Colorado, and — a 2026 detail that matters — made in the USA, so it sat out the tariff price hikes that hit the Chinese-made harnesses, bowls and beds around it. Empty, it's a doorstop with an erratic bounce. Stuffed, it's the cheapest behaviour tool in the house: crate training, the departure cue for a dog with separation anxiety, a rainy afternoon, a dinner that takes twenty minutes instead of forty seconds. Canine Journal's line is the consensus: "veterinarians and trainers worldwide recommend the KONG Classic," with two honest cons — it is not for aggressive chewers, and it smells if you don't wash it. It has never been recalled in fifty years; the only KONG recall on record was a 2016 batch of treats, not the toy.

Classic or Extreme, and which size

The red Classic is the medium-density rubber for normal chewers, puppies and seniors; the black Extreme is the firmer compound for dogs that destroy rubber. The size chart is by weight: Small to 20 lb, Medium 15–35, Large 30–65, X-Large 60–90, XX-Large above that. Noeul, at 50 pounds, is a Large. Two rules from the people who sell thousands of these: if your dog can clamp down on the whole toy, it's too small and the rubber will fail; and between sizes, go up, never down, because a too-small KONG is a choking hazard, not a toy. KONG's guarantee (replacement or refund for US buyers from authorized sellers) excludes "excessive chewing," so a chewed-through Classic is not a defect claim. It is a memo to buy the Extreme.

How to stuff it so it actually works

Start easy: loose kibble that falls out, so the dog learns the game in a minute. Then plug the small hole with a smear of peanut butter (check the label: no xylitol, which is toxic to dogs), fill with kibble and something wet to bind it — water, pumpkin, applesauce, plain yogurt — and cap the big opening. Freeze it overnight and the ten-minute toy becomes a thirty-minute toy. Count the filling toward the day's calories; a KONG is a bowl, not a bonus. Wash it every couple of weeks: KONG says the Classic is top-rack dishwasher safe, or soak it an hour in warm soapy water and run a bottle brush through it. The "my dog ignores it" reviews almost always describe an empty KONG on a floor.

Not it? Two alternatives

  • Cheaper per toy: the KONG Classic 2-pack, because the trick to a KONG household is always having one frozen and one in the dishwasher.
  • Pricier: the West Paw Toppl (about $16–$25) — a wider opening that is easier to fill and clean, and a favourite of trainers for frozen meals; or the KONG Extreme for the dog that split a Classic.

Which dog poop bags are worth paying for? (the consumable)

Earth Rated's 270-count unscented rolls, on Subscribe & Save: they won the 2026 lab test, open with frozen fingers, and cost about $2 a month. Amazon Basics is the cheaper bag that also passed.

Here is the math nobody puts on the box: two bags a day is about 730 a year, which makes this the most-used product a dog owner buys and the one that absolutely cannot fail at the moment of use. Earth Rated's standard bags are 9 by 13 inches, 65% recycled plastic, on rolls of fifteen that fit every dispenser ever made, with a perforated starter tab and a "3 bags left" sticker that has saved more walks than any app. CNN Underscored tested eight brands in 2026 and picked it best overall: no rips in the durability tests, a 5-pound weight held, and one of only two bags to survive their asphalt-scoop test. The other survivor was Amazon Basics, which is why it's the alternative below and not a footnote. Wirecutter's guide has the same winner and notes the bags open easily "even with dry frozen fingers" from minus ten to a hundred degrees.

The glove flip, and the winter-glove problem

Technique, because there is one: rub the bag between thumb and fingers to split the seam (or use the perforated tab), hand all the way inside like a glove, grab, invert the bag over your hand with the other hand, twist the neck, overhand knot. In January the plastic stiffens and gloved hands can't tie, which is the entire reason handle bags exist — Pogi's and Earth Rated both make them, and they tie one-handed. Disposal reality, from Rover and Whole Dog Journal: the trash is the honest default. "Biodegradable" and "compostable" claims got twenty bag marketers a warning letter from the FTC in 2015, landfills don't compost anything, and most US composting facilities refuse dog waste. Earth Rated itself settled an $825,000 class action in 2024–2025 over its old "certified compostable" line, which it no longer sells in the US; the standard recycled-plastic bags recommended here were not part of the case.

  • Pricier: Pogi's bags with easy-tie handles (300 for about $14) for gloved winters and one-handed tying; they come folded in a box rather than on rolls, so they won't fit a roll dispenser.

  • Price. On the exact items here: Earth Rated's 270-count was about $13.99 regular and $9–$10 on a coupon at Amazon, $14.99 at Chewy ($14.24 on Autoship) and $24 from a Walmart marketplace seller; the KONG Classic Large was $9.96 at Chewy and from $7.96 at Walmart, so the KONG is the one item to buy wherever it's cheapest that week.
  • Speed. Prime members get free same-day on $25-plus orders in most metros; Chewy is one to three days with a $49 free-shipping floor ($79 a year for Chewy+ removes it); Walmart and Target ship free at $35; Petco and PetSmart do same-day through DoorDash at $39 and $49. Temu's "local warehouse" listings arrive in three to seven days at best; anything still shipped from China takes seven to fifteen business days.
  • Returns. This is where Amazon loses. Thirty days, and pet food, edible items and medications can't go back at all. Chewy gives 365 days with free return shipping and routinely tells you to keep the item or donate it. Walmart is 90 days for items Walmart sells (30 for marketplace sellers); Petco and PetSmart 60 days in store, which for a harness means you can try it on the dog and hand it back. For a fit-dependent item that's a real argument for Chewy or a store — unless you measured, which is the point of the fitting section above.
  • Re-orders. Subscribe & Save takes 5% off and 15% when five or more items ship together in a month, which is the cheapest poop-bag route in the country. Chewy's Autoship gives 35% off the first order (capped at $20) and 5% after on select brands — better for a first bag of premium food, worse for bags forever. Petco and PetSmart copy Chewy's 35%-then-5% structure.
  • Authenticity. Amazon says it seized 15 million counterfeits in 2025 and verified 2.7 billion units through Transparency codes, and it still sells through a marketplace, so read the "Sold by" line: rabbitgoo, KONG or Amazon.com, Earth Rated. Chewy, Petco, PetSmart, Target and Costco are first-party by default. Walmart's marketplace was the subject of a CNBC investigation in September 2025 (sellers using stolen business identities, lab-confirmed fakes), so buy pet gear there only when it says "Sold and shipped by Walmart."
  • Temu, honestly. Until May 2025, Temu's trick was the de minimis rule: parcels under $800 entered the US duty-free direct from China. That ended on May 2, 2025 for China and Hong Kong (for every country on August 29, 2025); Temu briefly showed import charges of around 145% in April 2025, then moved US sales to "local" sellers and warehouses, lost about half its US daily users by that summer and cut its US ad spending by up to 95%. It has recovered some of the audience in 2026. What it has not done is stock a genuine Rabbitgoo, KONG, Earth Rated or Chuckit!: the listings are look-alikes copying the product photos, with no brand, no guarantee and no safety testing behind them. The Toy Association's December 2025 testing found 89% of Temu and Shein toys failed at least one safety check. Returns are 90 days with one free label per order and $7.99 for each additional return. Fine for a spare travel bowl. Not for the thing your dog's leash attaches to.

Quick answers

What size Rabbitgoo harness for a 50-pound dog?

Usually Large, but measure: the widest part of the chest just behind the front legs, and the lower neck. Choose the size whose range puts the chest number in the middle. Deep-chested, narrow-waisted dogs should check the girth strap sits behind the ribs, not in the armpits.

Front clip or back clip?

Front (chest) ring for a dog that pulls or lunges: it turns the pull into a sideways step toward you. Back ring for relaxed walks, jogging and dogs that already walk on a loose leash. Many owners use the front ring for the first ten minutes and switch.

Can a Jindo or husky slip a Rabbitgoo harness?

A loose one, yes — any two-strap harness. Tighten to two fingers at all four sliders, seat the girth strap behind the widest ribs, and do the step-back test before walks. Habitual escape artists should move to a three-strap harness with a belly strap.

Is the KONG Classic or Extreme right for my dog?

Classic (red) for normal chewers, puppies and seniors; Extreme (black) if your dog has destroyed rubber toys before. Same sizes, same stuffing; the difference is rubber density. Size by weight (Large is 30–65 lb) and go up if in doubt.

What can I put in a KONG?

Kibble, xylitol-free peanut butter, plain yogurt, canned pumpkin, mashed banana, cottage cheese, soaked kibble or wet food. Freeze it for a longer session and count it as part of the day's food. No cooked bones, no grapes, no onion, nothing sweetened with xylitol.

Is a KONG dishwasher safe?

Yes, top rack, according to KONG. Or soak it an hour in warm soapy water and use a bottle brush. Wash every couple of weeks; the smell complaints come from KONGs that never get washed.

Are Earth Rated bags compostable?

The standard bags are not; they are 65% recycled plastic and go in the trash, which is also where "biodegradable" bags end up. Earth Rated's old compostable line was the subject of an $825,000 class-action settlement and is no longer sold in the US. Most US composting facilities refuse dog waste anyway.

How many poop bags do I need a year?

About two a day for one dog, so roughly 60 a month and 730 a year. A 270-count pack lasts four to four and a half months; the 900-count bundles last more than a year.

Is it cheaper to buy dog supplies on Amazon or Chewy?

Amazon, by about 6% on average across pet supplies in Profitero's 2025 study, with Chewy about 1% higher. Chewy wins on returns (365 days) and on the first Autoship order of food (35% off, up to $20). For bags and gear, Amazon with Subscribe & Save is usually the cheaper route.

Is Temu safe for dog products?

It sells no genuine Rabbitgoo, KONG, Earth Rated or Chuckit! products, only look-alikes with no brand or safety testing behind them, and its 2025 safety-test record on toys was poor. Use it for disposable extras like a spare travel bowl, not for a harness, a chew toy or anything a leash attaches to.
