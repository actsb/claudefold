What is six months of Google AI Pro really worth?

$119.94 if you would otherwise pay monthly, about $100 at the annual rate, roughly $30 if a cheaper plan would do, and nothing if you would never have paid for it.

Google's footnote says the "actual value is $119.94 based on monthly auto-renewal rate", which is six payments of $19.99. Google's plans page (read November 18, 2026) lists what that buys: 5 TB of storage across Gmail, Drive and Photos, the Pro-class Gemini model, Gemini inside Gmail and Docs, 1,000 Flow credits and a limited Veo trial. Storage rose from 2 TB to 5 TB in April 2026 with no price change (Android Central and Tom's Guide, April 2026), so older deal posts that say "2 TB" are out of date. The annual plan is $199.99, so six months at that rate is about $100.

What it is worth to you depends on which of these people you are:

  • You already pay for AI Pro. Close to the full $119.94. Owners report that a paid Google One plan is cancelled when the trial starts, with a pro-rata refund (Google's help page, as summarized in owner threads), so you lose nothing, but note the date the trial ends.
  • You pay for storage only. Google's AI Plus plan is $4.99 a month for 400 GB and the Flash model; if that covers you, the trial saves about $30, and the rest is a six-month look at the Pro model. Prof. H's lab worked through that choice in is Google AI Plus worth it after November 13: since that date AI Plus no longer includes the Pro model, which makes the gap $180 a year.
  • You would never pay for an AI plan. Zero, and a small risk. A card is required at sign-up, and the bill starts on its own.

Two limits make the trial worth a little less than a paid plan. 9to5Google reported on October 16, 2026 that free AI Pro trials, Pixel ones included, do not get YouTube Premium Lite or the 10% Google Store credit that paid members get, and cannot be switched to paid early from inside the app (9to5Google). And Google's offer documentation allows one trial at a time: redeeming a new one revokes the one you have, so a Pixel trial and a Galaxy trial do not stack. The Galaxy S26 FE terms go further and exclude anyone who is already "a discounted Pixel bundle subscriber". If you are weighing the plan against other AI subscriptions, Prof. H's lab keeps the numbers in its real monthly cost of AI subscriptions (Prof. H runs that site; this one earns commissions, that one does not).

Is a carrier deal better than buying unlocked?

Only if you were going to switch or add a line anyway. How do you claim the AI Pro months without paying later?

Read the footnote before you buy, check your Google One plan, redeem after activation, and set a reminder for month five.

  1. Read the redeem-by date on the maker's page, not the banner. The Pixel 10 Pro banner outlived its October 31 deadline.
  2. Check how you pay for Google One now. The terms exclude plans billed through a third party (a carrier, for example), add-ons, a higher tier, and family members who are not the plan manager.
  3. Do not cancel or swap the order once the trial has started. Owners lost their existing plans that way in 2025.
  4. Activate the phone, then redeem in the Google One or Gemini app. If no offer appears, owners report that "Check for offers" in Google One or reinstalling the app helped.
  5. Set a reminder for month five. Decide whether to keep AI Pro, drop to AI Plus at $4.99, or cancel. Google says cancelling during the trial keeps access to its end.
  6. Keep the phone if you keep the plan. Google's and Samsung's terms say returning the device may cancel the subscription.

Quick answers

Which Pixel phones include free Google AI Pro in 2026?

The Pixel 11 Pro, Pro XL and Pro Fold include six months, redeemable until October 31, 2027 (Google's Pixel help page, read November 18, 2026). The base Pixel 11 includes none, and the Pixel 10a gets three months of 100 GB storage instead.

Does the Galaxy S26 Ultra come with Google AI Pro?

Not in the US. Samsung's S26 Ultra offers page (November 18, 2026) lists two months of Adobe Lightroom and six months of SiriusXM, but no Google AI Pro, Gemini or Perplexity plan. The Z Flip8, Z Fold8 line and S26 FE include six months of AI Pro.

Does a Pixel 10 Pro bought now still get a year of AI Pro?

No. Google's footnote says the offer had to be redeemed by October 31, 2026, even though the Google Store page still shows the "year on us ($239 value)" banner.

How much is Google AI Pro after the free trial?

$19.99 a month or $199.99 a year in the US (Google's plans page, November 18, 2026). The trial renews automatically at the monthly price unless you cancel; 30 months after a six-month trial is $599.70.

Can I stack a Pixel and a Samsung AI Pro trial?

Not at the same time. Google's offer documentation allows one trial at a time, and redeeming a new one revokes the current one; the S26 FE terms also exclude anyone already on a discounted Pixel bundle.

What happens to my photos when the AI Pro trial ends?

Your files stay, but if you are over your plan's storage limit you cannot add more, and photo backup and Gmail can stop working until you free space or pay (Google's help page, as owners summarized it).

Pixel 11 vs Pixel 11 Pro at Black Friday: which should I buy?

The Pixel 11 Pro, if both are $699 as Google announced for November 19: the Pro is the higher model and adds six months of AI Pro. Buy the Pixel 11 only if it is clearly cheaper.

Is Galaxy AI going to cost money?

Samsung says its basic Galaxy AI features are free and that future enhanced features may be paid. A $4.99-a-month tier was reported as in testing on November 11, 2026, but Samsung has not announced it.

Is the Galaxy S26 Ultra cheaper on Black Friday?

Yes: $1,049.99 on Samsung.com from November 16 to 26, 2026, and $949.99 with the PAYPAL100 code on orders over $1,000 through November 30. Best Buy also sold it at $949.99 on November 7.

Is a carrier's free phone deal better than buying unlocked?

Only if you were switching anyway. The phone is paid off by bill credits over 24 to 36 months, often on a new line, and the remaining credits stop if you leave or pay it off early.
