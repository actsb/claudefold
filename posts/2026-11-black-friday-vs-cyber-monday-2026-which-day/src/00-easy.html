Vera's 10-second verdict

Which is cheaper, Black Friday or Cyber Monday?

It depends on the category: Black Friday for appliances, vacuums, TVs and toys; Cyber Monday for electronics and computers; and for most of the rest the prices are the same on both days.

Adobe, which measures online sales at large US retailers, forecast on September 28, 2026 that "the best deals for TVs, toys, apparel, appliances and furniture" will land on Black Friday, with appliances at 18% off and TVs at 23%, and that electronics (30%) and computers (23%) will be deepest on Cyber Monday. Its 2025 numbers looked much the same: on Cyber Monday 2025 electronics peaked at 31% off listed price and appliances at 19% (Adobe, December 2, 2025). Adobe is a vendor that sells to retailers, so we treat its numbers as direction, not as gospel.

The more useful finding for a shopper is what happened between the two days. Digital Commerce 360, reporting Adobe's data on November 29, 2025, said discounts peaked on Black Friday "and extended throughout the weekend." Brand stores ran one long sale: Roborock's US sale ran November 20 to December 1, 2025 (Wccftech), and Dreame's store had the X50 Ultra at one price from November 20 to December 1. The AirPods Pro 3 held the same low across both days (9to5Toys, December 10, 2025). When the weekend did beat Friday, it was because of stacked offers, not a new sticker price.

Why is the KitchenAid Artisan our top pick?

Because it is the one product where the dated record shows the Black Friday weekend beating Black Friday itself, and it is already on a real sale today.

On Sunday, November 30, 2025, Target took an extra 15% off appliances in the cart for signed-in shoppers, on top of its Black Friday price, which brought the Artisan 5-quart to $297.49 (Slickdeals thread, November 30, 2025). Commenters in the same thread stacked Target's card discount on top. One buyer went to Kohl's instead and paid $350 less $150 back in Kohl's Cash and rewards (December 2, 2025). An older NerdWallet price study of ten products found the KitchenAid mixer cheaper on Cyber Monday than on Black Friday in 2023 (as republished by the Independent Tribune). Three data points do not make a law, but they all point the same way: for a mixer, keep watching through Sunday.

Today, November 16, 2026, Target lists the Artisan KSM150 at $349.99, down from $499.99, which is exactly our good line. KitchenAid's own store had $499.99 on the same model in Onyx Black, with $349.99 on a few colours, and Walmart showed $369.99 for the white one in a search snippet. Kohl's listed it at $499.99 and marked it not eligible for coupons, though Kohl's Cash still earns $15 per $50 spent from November 22 to 30 (Kohl's, October 30, 2026). If you can wait eleven days, wait. If you need it for Thanksgiving baking, $349.99 is fair, and Target's holiday price match pays back its own drops until December 24.

What do unhappy owners say?

  • The gear is built to fail first. Repair shops explain that the nylon worm gear strips under overload on purpose, so the motor survives; it is a cheap part, but the mixer is out of action until it is replaced (Mr. Mixer, undated).
  • It walks on the counter with stiff dough. A Slickdeals owner said the tilt-head "will move around on the counter" with heavy dough (July 2024). Bread bakers should look at a bowl-lift model.
  • Grease can seep with age. The same repair guides describe separated grease leaking from older mixers, which a service fixes.

What never (or barely) goes on sale?

Apple's own store, the Litter-Robot, cheap staples like the KONG, and premium brands with summer or refurbished sales instead of Black Friday cuts.

  • Apple's own store. Apple's 2025 Black Friday event gave a $50 gift card with AirPods Pro 3 at the full $249 (MacRumors, November 21, 2025). The discounts are at Target, Best Buy, Walmart and Costco.
  • The Litter-Robot 4. $699 at Whisker and PetSmart before, during and after the sale in 2025 and 2026, apart from Whisker's $50 holiday cut in 2025. Bundles get the money off.
  • A $14 dog toy. The KONG Classic Large is $13.99 at Target and PetSmart (November 16, 2026); a 2025 Black Friday cut to $11.96 was laughed at in a Slickdeals thread.
  • Le Creuset. Its deepest cuts come in its summer Factory-to-Table sale, which ran through July 31, 2026 (Chowhound).
  • Vitamix. The best prices are on certified reconditioned units, and a Vitamix-focused blog says the company has pricing agreements with its retailers.

The pattern in all five: the maker controls the price, so the deal, if there is one, is a gift card, a bundle or a refurbished unit. A bundle is worth it only if you wanted the extras anyway.

What do the experts and the data say?

The data agree on the category split, and every price study we found says a large share of "deals" were matched or beaten at another time.

On which day

  • Adobe Digital Insights (September 28, 2026): $275.1 billion of US online holiday spending forecast for November 1 to December 31; Cyber Monday the biggest day at $15.1 billion, Black Friday $12.9 billion and growing fastest. Discounts up to 14% in early November and up to 21% just before Thanksgiving.
  • DealNews (updated November 21, 2025): Black Friday has traditionally had better deals on big-ticket items like TVs and appliances, and fewer than a third of its Cyber Week deals were posted on the Monday itself. The same page also lists kitchen items as better on Cyber Monday, so even deal sites disagree.
  • Consumer Reports, via KRGV (November 26, 2025): Black Friday is the best time to get a TV, and prices may go up after Cyber Monday.
  • NerdWallet's 2023 price check (as republished): of ten products tracked at three big retailers, Black Friday had the best price on six; four had hit their low on Prime Day. Old, but the only US study of its kind we found.

On whether the deal is real

  • Visualping (updated November 28, 2025): 33% of Black Friday prices it tracked from October 1 to December 1, 2025 were higher than the lowest price since October 1; 33% in home and appliances, 50% for microwaves. Its store links point to Canadian sites, so read it as North American, not purely US.
  • Which? (UK, 2025): 83% of 175 Black Friday deals were the same price or cheaper at other times in the year before. A UK study, but the same tactics are used here.
  • NRF (October 2026 survey, as reported): 64% of shoppers say they are likely to wait until Thanksgiving weekend to do most of their shopping, up from 51% five years ago. Waiting is popular; it is not always cheaper.

How do you plan Cyber Week so the day doesn't matter?

Set a price for each item, buy early where the store will pay back a later drop, and check the model number every time.

  1. Write your number now. Use the good and great lines in our cards and on Price Check. A number you set today beats a banner you see on Friday.
  2. Match the day to the category. Appliances and vacuums on Black Friday weekend; earbuds and laptops on Cyber Monday; pets and car gear whenever the number comes up.
  3. Buy early where you are protected. Target's holiday price match covers its own drops until December 24; Best Buy matches its own lower price for the season; Costco.com adjusts for 30 days.
  4. Treat Walmart differently. Walmart.com does not adjust a price after you buy, so buy there only at the low.
  5. Match the model number. A Shark IZ840HRW is not an IZ840H, and a Little Green 3369 is not a 1400B. Retailer-only versions often have fewer parts.
  6. Keep every receipt. Holiday return windows run to January 15 at Best Buy and January 24 at Target for most electronics.

Quick answers

Is Black Friday or Cyber Monday better in 2026?

For appliances, vacuums, TVs and toys, Black Friday (November 27); for electronics and computers, Cyber Monday (November 30). That is Adobe's September 2026 forecast, and its 2025 results looked the same. For most other products the price was the same on both days in 2025.

When is Cyber Monday 2026?

Monday, November 30, 2026. Best Buy runs Cyber Sunday and Monday on November 29–30, Kohl's Cyber Deals the same two days, and Walmart's Cyber Monday event runs online from November 30 to December 2.

Is Black Friday or Cyber Monday better for a KitchenAid mixer?

Watch both. In 2025 Target's lowest price on the Artisan 5-quart came on Sunday, November 30, with an extra 15% in the cart ($297.49, per Slickdeals). On November 16, 2026 Target had it at $349.99, our good line; under $300 is our great line.

Is Black Friday or Cyber Monday better for a robot vacuum?

Either, because the makers run one sale across both. Roborock's US sale starts November 19, 2026, and Dreame's Black Friday prices were already live on November 16. Compare against last year: Dreame had the X50 Ultra at $799.99 in 2025, and $999.99 on November 16, 2026.

Are pet supplies cheaper on Black Friday or Cyber Monday?

Neither by much. Chewy's deals usually run from the day before Thanksgiving through Cyber Monday, and in 2025 they were mostly gift cards with a minimum spend. Autoship's 5% stacks on sale prices, so a stock-up during the window is sensible; a Litter-Robot is not worth timing.

Should I buy AirPods now or wait for Cyber Monday?

Now, if you find $180 or less. Target listed the AirPods Pro 3 at $179.99 on November 16, 2026, $40 below the 2025 holiday low of $219.99, and Target's holiday price match pays back its own lower price until December 24.

What should you not buy on Black Friday?

Anything without a price you set in advance, retailer-only model numbers you cannot compare, and things that never really go on sale (the Litter-Robot, Apple at Apple's store, a $14 dog toy). Be wary of microwaves: Visualping found half the microwave "deals" it tracked in 2025 were higher than an earlier price.

Do prices go up after Cyber Monday?

Often, says Consumer Reports (November 2025). But not always: Costco cut a Litter-Robot bundle on December 9, 2025, Chewy ran a December gift-card offer, and refurbished robot vacuums and dash cams went lower in December. Adobe expects 10–15% discounts through December.

Which stores refund the difference if the price drops after I buy?

Target (its own price, November 1 to December 24), Best Buy (its own price, holiday season, marketplace items excluded) and Costco.com (30 days). Walmart.com does not adjust a price after purchase. Check each store's page; terms change.

How do I know a Black Friday deal is real?

Compare the price with the lowest price since October, not with the "was" price, and match the model number. Our Price Check page lists dated prices at stores other than Amazon for every product we recommend, with a good and a great line.

The bottom line: watch the mixer through Sunday, and set a number for the rest

The day matters for two kinds of product: appliances lean to Black Friday weekend, and earbuds and electronics lean to Cyber Monday. For everything else, the number matters more than the date. Our top pick, the KitchenAid Artisan, is at $349.99 at Target today; if you can wait, last year's Sunday stack took it under $300. The AirPods Pro 3 are already a buy at $180 or less. The Little Green is worth waiting for under $90. And the Litter-Robot is $699 on any day of the year, so buy it when you need it.
