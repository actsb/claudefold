What works on each doorbell if you never pay?

eufy, Reolink, Aqara and Tapo save video locally with no plan; Nest keeps three hours of events; Ring keeps nothing, but its live view, two-way talk and motion alerts stay free.

Ring: a live camera and an alert, no history

Ring's Live View help page says "Live View is available to Ring app users and Shared Users without a subscription," and two-way talk works during Live View; it also says reviewing recorded video requires a Ring subscription. Ring's own blog (July 16, 2026) lists motion alerts and motion zones as free, and video history, snapshot capture and person detection as plan features. A free 30-day trial starts when you set up the doorbell. Ring renamed its plans in 2026: Ring Solo is $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year for one device, Ring Multi is $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year for every device at a location, and Ring Pro is $19.99 a month or $199.99 a year with Video Descriptions, Familiar Faces (beta) and 24/7 professional monitoring (ring.com/plans, November 11, 2026). Many pages still call them Basic and Standard; those names are gone.

eufy E340: 8 GB inside the doorbell

eufy's product page says the E340 has 8 GB of eMMC storage built in and that 90 days of video can be stored, with no subscription for notifications. Person, package and face recognition are on the device. Cloud backup is optional at $3.99 a month or $39.99 a year for one device (eufy, November 11, 2026). The storage is soldered in, not a card, so if it fails you cannot swap it: one forum owner's storage stuck at "full" and needed a full reset (eufy Community, October 2024). eufy can also save to a HomeBase if you have one.

Reolink PoE: a card, a recorder or Home Assistant

Reolink's product page says the Video Doorbell PoE records 24/7, on motion or on a schedule to a microSD card of up to 256 GB, a Reolink NVR, a Home Hub or an FTP server, and its FAQ says "you don't need any subscription". Reolink's comparison table lists ONVIF, RTSP and a direct Home Assistant link for the wired WiFi and PoE models, and the Home Assistant integration page says it keeps working with the internet blocked. The newer battery "Video Doorbell (2nd Gen)" is different: Reolink says Home Assistant needs its Home Hub for that one.

Aqara G410: a card indoors, or Apple's cloud for $0.99

Aqara says the G410 takes a microSD card of up to 512 GB, and it sits in the indoor chime, so a thief cannot take it with the doorbell (MacRumors). Continuous recording works only when it is wired. For Apple Home, Apple's support page (September 16, 2026) says the 50 GB iCloud+ plan at $0.99 a month covers HomeKit Secure Video for one camera, and the video does not count against your storage. Aqara says HomeKit video is capped at 1200p. Without HomeKit, MacRumors says, you can only use local storage unless you pay for Aqara's HomeGuardian.

Tapo D225 and Nest: a card, or three hours

The Tapo D225 records to a microSD card of up to 512 GB, with free person, pet, vehicle and package alerts; Tapo Care cloud is $3.49 a month or $34.99 a year (Tapo, November 11, 2026). The Google Store says the Nest Doorbell (battery) "comes with 3 hours of included event history"; longer history moves to Google Home Premium, which Google says replaced Nest Aware, at $10 a month or $100 a year (Standard).

How do you set up a doorbell so it never needs a plan?

Check the transformer before you buy, put the storage in on day one, and shrink the motion zone so the battery lasts.

  1. Read the transformer label by the panel or the old chime. eufy asks for 16 to 24 V AC and 30 VA for wired mode; owners with 8 to 10 VA report drain, missed rings and no pre-roll. A battery doorbell avoids the question.
  2. Choose the storage. A high-endurance microSD (128 to 256 GB) for Reolink, Aqara or Tapo; eufy's 8 GB is inside.
  3. Format the card in the app and play back a test clip on day one. Reolink lists unformatted cards as the usual cause of "no SD card".
  4. Cut the street out of the motion zone. Street traffic is the drain owners name most; button-only mode is the last resort.
  5. Turn off the features you do not want. On Ring, Search Party is per camera under Control Center.
  6. Check after each firmware update that the chime rings and clips save; owners on eufy and Aqara report updates that broke both.

What is the catch?

This guide compares documentation and owner reports, not hands-on tests, and local storage moves the risk from a monthly fee to a card or chip you have to check.

We read the makers' plan, support and product pages, published reviews and forum threads; we did not install these doorbells. Plans change: Ring renamed and repriced its plans in 2026, and Google folded Nest Aware into Google Home Premium. Local storage has its own failure modes: a full or unformatted card, built-in storage stuck at "full", or a card stolen with an outdoor unit. And none of the no-plan options keep a copy off-site, so a doorbell pulled off the wall takes its eufy or Tapo clips with it.

  • Battery claims of five to ten months are best cases; plan on weeks to a few months at a busy door.
  • An old mechanical chime may not ring with any of them; check each maker's chime page first.
  • Our verdicts depend on past discounts; a sale announced next week can change them.

Quick answers

Does a Ring doorbell work without a subscription?

Partly. Ring says Live View and two-way talk work without a plan, and motion alerts are free, but reviewing recorded video, snapshots and person detection need a plan (ring.com, November 11, 2026).

How much are Ring plans in 2026?

Ring Solo is $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year for one device, Ring Multi $9.99 or $99.99 for a location, and Ring Pro $19.99 or $199.99 with the AI features (ring.com/plans, November 11, 2026).

What happened to Ring Basic and Ring Protect?

Ring renamed its plans in 2026: the single-device plan is now Ring Solo and the whole-home plan Ring Multi, with Ring Pro on top. Some Ring pages still say Ring Protect.

Which video doorbell has no monthly fee?

The eufy E340 saves about 90 days of clips in 8 GB built in, and the Reolink, Aqara G410 and Tapo D225 save to a microSD card. None needs a plan to record.

Where does the eufy E340 store video, and is it encrypted?

On 8 GB of storage inside the doorbell. In January 2025 New York's attorney general settled with eufy's distributors for $450,000 over streams that were not always encrypted, and required encryption at rest and in transit.

Aqara G410 vs G410 Select: what is the difference?

MacRumors reported the Select is the same doorbell with a three-month HomeGuardian trial instead of one month. The G400 is a different, wired-only model that is not a hub.

Which doorbell works with Home Assistant locally?

The Reolink Video Doorbell PoE and WiFi: Reolink lists ONVIF, RTSP and a direct Home Assistant link, and the integration works with the internet blocked. The battery 2nd Gen needs Reolink's hub.

How do I turn off Ring Search Party?

In the Ring app, open the menu, then Control Center, then Search Party, and turn off Search for Lost Pets and Natural Hazards for each camera (Ring help; Engadget, February 2026).

Is my Ring doorbell recalled?

Only the 2020 Video Doorbell (2nd Generation), model 5UM5E5, recalled in November 2020 over the wrong screws. The 2026 Battery Doorbell 2K, also sold as 2nd Gen, is not recalled (CPSC, November 11, 2026).

What does a video doorbell cost over three years?

From $119.99 for the eufy E340 with no plan to $189.96 for a $39.99 Ring with Ring Solo, $499.99 for a Nest with Premium Standard and $639.96 for Ring with Ring Pro (store and plan pages, November 11, 2026).
