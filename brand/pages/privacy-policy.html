Verdict Picks ("we", "us") is hosted on Google's Blogger platform. This page explains what data is collected when you visit, who collects it, and the choices you have. It supplements the cookie notice Google displays to visitors from the European Union.

1. Data collected by Google (Blogger)

Blogger, operated by Google LLC, collects standard server data (IP address, browser type, pages visited, referring page, timestamps) and sets cookies needed to run the site and to measure traffic. How Google uses data from sites that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

2. Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. When it does:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's certified consent message before personalized ads are shown.

3. Amazon Associates links

Product links on this site go to Amazon.com through the Amazon Associates program. When you click one, Amazon sets a cookie that lets it attribute a purchase to this site for up to 24 hours. Amazon's use of your data is governed by the Amazon Privacy Notice. We receive aggregate reports (clicks, orders, commissions); we do not receive your name, address, or payment information.

4. Embedded YouTube videos

Our articles embed YouTube videos. When a video loads or plays, YouTube (Google) may set cookies and collect viewing data as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Comments

If you comment, Blogger stores the comment with your Google profile name and the time. Comments are moderated. You can delete your own comments at any time.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not run our own tracking scripts beyond the Google services named above.
  • We do not collect email addresses unless you write to us.

7. Cookies — your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work, though Amazon will not be able to attribute a purchase to us. EU/UK visitors see Google's cookie notice on first visit; the choices you make there apply to Google's cookies.

8. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
