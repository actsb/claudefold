Verdict Picks exists because buying anything on Amazon has become a research project.

Search for a robot vacuum and you get 40 brands, suction numbers that seem to double every year, and thousands of five-star reviews sitting next to thousands of one-star reviews for the same product. Somewhere in there is the right answer for your home. Finding it takes hours most people don't have.

So we do the hours. For every category we cover, we:

  • Map the whole market — every brand that matters in the US, what each one is actually good at, and what it charges for it.
  • Cross-check the spec sheets against independent lab tests from outlets that measure things (RTINGS, Vacuum Wars, Tom's Guide, TechGearLab, Consumer Reports and others), because a marketing number is not a measurement.
  • Read the owner reviews — especially the bad ones. The one-, two- and three-star reviews on Amazon and other retailers, and long-term owner threads where we can reach them, are where the real ownership story lives: the hair tangles, the app bugs, the dock that clogs, the support ticket that went nowhere.
  • End with a verdict. Not a list of twenty links — a specific recommendation for each kind of household and budget, plus the products we think you should skip.

What we do and do not do

  • We research; we do not lab-test. Our guides are built from published tests, manuals, owner reviews and price reports. Unless a guide says otherwise, we have not handled the product ourselves, and we say when we could not open a source.
  • Illustrations are ours. The product art, comparison charts and the host, Vera, are original illustrations made for this site. They are not photographs of the product, and Vera is not a real person.
  • AI-assisted, human-checked. We use AI tools to help draft and check guides, and the Verdict Picks editors review them before they are published. Numbers are attributed and dated, and anything we could not confirm is marked unverified.
  • Prices. We report prices with their source and date; the price on the Amazon button is the one that applies.

Who we are

Verdict Picks is an independent publication written for shoppers in the United States. We are not owned by, sponsored by, or affiliated with any manufacturer. Brands do not pay for placement, and they cannot buy a ranking.
