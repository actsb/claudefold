## X / Threads (day 1)
1. About 1.58 million rechargeable hand warmers were recalled in the US in 2026. Before you charge yours: flip it over. If the bottom says UT3053, UT3056, ZLS-118(S/D), H01, H01(PD) or H163650, stop and claim the refund. Steps: https://acts39.blogspot.com/2026/11/rechargeable-hand-warmer-recalls-2026.html (Amazon links in the guide are paid links.)
