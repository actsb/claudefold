## 1. Safety recall: the single most important finding
Source: CPSC recall notice (cpsc.gov/Recalls/2023/Bedshe-International-Recalls-Bedsure-Electric-Heating-Blankets-and-Pads-Due-to-Fire-and-Thermal-Burn-Hazards-Recall-Alert), as relayed via search summaries and KGW, WTOP, RD.com, RTTNews, NY DHSES. Recall dated March 9, 2023 (per search summaries). CPSC page itself not opened.
- Hazard: controller can malfunction, posing fire and thermal burn hazards. About 350,000 units.
- 137 reports of malfunction (fires, burning, melting, overheating), 17 with minor injuries, one second-degree burn (per search summary of CPSC).
- Recalled blanket models listed: BS-HB5060 (the 50x60 throw, sherpa and/or flannel), BS-HB6284, BS-HB7284, BS-LV0A; plus heating pads BS-HP2422, BS-HP1224, BS-HP2433.
- Sales window: Amazon and BedsureHome.com, August to November 2022 in one summary (KHOU/Kitchn-type result) and August to December 2022 in another. Discrepancy: UNVERIFIED which is right; check CPSC.
- Remedy: stop using, full refund via 855-888-9966, safety@bedsurehome.com, safety.bedsurehome.com. Model number is on a back label, the side of the controller, or Amazon Order History.
- Affiliate implication: the recall covers the 2022 BS-HB5060 production. Whether current listings (e.g. 2023+ ETL-labelled versions) use a different model number or controller is UNVERIFIED. Tell readers to check the model number on the label and not to buy or use a unit that matches the recalled list. Lawyer-marketing pages (yourlawyer.com, johnsonbecker.com, schmidtlaw.com) exist; they are advertising, not evidence.

## 4. Complaints and failure modes
- Dead heating zone after months: a search summary quotes an Amazon reviewer giving four stars: "The heating has gone out in the middle but the outsides still heats fairly well. After 10 months of use I can't complain." Which listing and date UNKNOWN; wording UNVERIFIED.
- Lukewarm / no heat: summary of Amazon/Walmart snippets cites "very disappointed that it doesn't heat better than just a lukewarm" and "did not get warm after the first few hours". Reviewer, listing and date UNKNOWN; UNVERIFIED.
- Controller/error codes (Bedsure troubleshooting guide, bedsurehome.com/blogs/products; portablepowerguides.com Bedsure error-code page; date UNKNOWN):
  - F1: poor connection or damaged cord or controller problem. F2: usually cord connected to wall before blanket; connector not fully seated. F3: connection problem, often loose cable at the blanket.
  - Blinking red: no/insufficient power, or, per the Bedsure/third-party guides, throw disconnected or internal wire open-circuit (broken). Controller self-protection can trigger if wrapped up or near heat; cool 20-30 minutes then retry.
  - Third-party guides (portablepowerguides, EIN Presswire press release, Puffy blog) are generic and not owner testimony.
- Customer service (Trustpilot, bedsurehome.com; page "Bedsure Home Reviews", TrustScore 4 of 5 on main site per snippet; Australian site 3.2 of 5 from 43 reviews; dates UNKNOWN): one reviewer reported a heated sherpa fleece with non-working temperature control, a 25-day runaround and a 25% refund offer; another said replacement promises lapsed and cost them the Amazon refund window; one said they could feel wires in a heated mattress pad (different product). Individual claims, unverified, and not all about the throw.
- Pilling: Bedsure blog says pilling shows early in washes; no owner-attributed pilling data found. UNVERIFIED for this product.
- Burning smell is a generic replace-your-blanket sign (heavenlyhealth.com), not a Bedsure owner report.
