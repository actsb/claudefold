### 1.11 Tire-pressure guidance (for the "how to use" section)
- NHTSA page: https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle-safety/tires. The direct fetch returned 403. The points below come from search snippets of NHTSA pages (SNIPPET):
  - Check all tires, including the spare, at least monthly, when the tires are "cold" (the car not driven for at least three hours).
  - The proper pressure is on the Tire and Loading Information Label on the driver's side door edge or in the owner's manual. The number on the tire sidewall is the tire maximum, not the vehicle recommendation.
  - Do not adjust warm tires to the placard figure, which leads to underinflation. Pressure rises about 4 to 6 psi as tires warm.
  - TPMS is not a substitute for regular maintenance.
- TPMS threshold, 25 percent below placard: NHTSA final rule https://www.nhtsa.gov/sites/nhtsa.gov/files/tpmsfinalrule.pdf (403 on fetch; the snippet-based summary cites FMVSS 138). Secondary confirmation is Continental Tire: https://continentaltire.com/learn/tpms-warning-light-what-it-means-and-what-do. Example: a 32 psi placard triggers at about 24 psi or lower. Quote the 25 percent rule only after you open the NHTSA rule yourself.
- Tire Industry Association / Rubber Manufacturers Association: no pages were returned. UNVERIFIED. Tire Rack's tech page https://www.tirerack.com/tires/tiretech/techpage.jsp?techid=8 appeared in results (not opened).

## 5. Notes for the writer
- Ranking logic supported by independent data: Milwaukee M18 is the speed and truck-tire pick (needs a battery and is the bulkiest and priciest). DeWalt 20V is the versatile pick for tool owners (no independent timings found). Fanttik X8 Apex and AstroAI are compact top-off tools that stalled on a big truck tire with their small batteries. Ryobi works for low-cost tool owners but is loud and slow on flat tires, and the P737D is discontinued.
- Safe accuracy wording: Car and Driver says no tested gauge was perfect. Recommend verifying with a separate handheld gauge.
- Unfilled gaps: Wirecutter, Popular Mechanics and PCMag text, Tom's Guide details, Edmunds, Tested, RTINGS, TIA/RMA. Do not assert their picks.
