## 2. Warranty and returns
- Dreo US warranty: 12-month limited warranty from purchase date, covering original defects in materials and workmanship. Without proof of purchase it starts 2 months after the manufacture date. Service does not extend the term. Source: https://www.dreo.com/pages/warranty-terms-conditions (fetched 2026-10-15). The site has a "Warranty Activation" link, but the terms page says nothing about an extension.
- A "12 months, extendable to 30 months on registration" line appears only in third-party Newegg listings (https://www.newegg.com/p/0JH-01C1-001B1, search snippet 2026-10-15). A Slickdeals forum user (April 2025) said Dreo told them US warranties will be non-extendable 12 months (via search summary, https://slickdeals.net/f/18247573-..., unverified). Recommendation: write "12 months", and say the extension is not in Dreo's current terms.
- dreo.com returns: 30-day money-back guarantee for any reason. The product must be undamaged and in original packaging, with all accessories. The buyer pays shipping and there is no restocking fee. Purchases from other retailers go through that retailer. Source: https://www.dreo.com/policies/refund-policy (fetched 2026-10-15).
- Costco: DR-HSH015 page says Costco.com purchases can be returned to any warehouse under the satisfaction guarantee (https://www.costco.com/dreo-1500w-portable-3d-automatic-oscillating-ceramic-ptc-heater.product.4000375480.html, 2026-10-15).
- Typical windows (third-party summary, not the retailers' own pages; https://returnpolicyinfo.com/, search summary 2026-10-15): Walmart 90 days for most items, 30 days for electronics, per one source. Target 90 days. Home Depot 90 days, 30 days for major appliances. Lowe's 90 days. Best Buy 15 days, 30 for members. A small heater's category treatment is unconfirmed.
