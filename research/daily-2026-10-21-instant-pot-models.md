# Research brief 2026-10-21: Instant Pot Duo vs Duo Plus vs Rio (6-qt), Pro for context
Three agents (owners; video+experts; price+channels). Reddit, Walmart, Costco, Home Depot, Kohl's, Wirecutter, Serious Eats pages were not reachable; items from snippets are marked (snippet). No Amazon data used.

## Specs (instantpot.com product/collection pages, seen 2026-10-21)
- Duo 6QT 112-0170-01: $109.99 list, 1000 W, 7 functions (pressure, slow, sauté, steam, rice, yogurt, keep warm), 13 one-touch programs, 18/8 stainless tri-ply pot, 1-yr warranty, steam rack.
- Duo Plus 6QT V4 112-0169-01 (Whisper Quiet): $139.99, 1000 W, 9 functions (adds sous vide, cake), "25 recipe presets", WhisperQuiet steam release, big LCD with progress bar, steam rack with handles and two extra sealing rings. Older V3 112-0156-01 had Cake/Egg/Sterilize, 15 programs. Button list varies by version.
- Rio 6QT black 112-0312-01: $109.99, 1000 W, 7 functions, 13 programs, stainless anti-spin pot, one ring + trivet, time-only LCD, high pressure only (Pressure Cooking Today Rio review, 2023-07-09, upd 2024-06-01), no sous vide.
- Pro 6QT 112-0123-01: $169.99, 1200 W, 10 functions, 28 programs + 5 favorites, insulated pot handles.
- Variants: Rio Chef Series (Walmart, 112-2000-01) = ceramic-coated aluminum pot; Rio Gourmet (Costco, 112-2213-01); Target-exclusive 9-in-1 bundle 112-2356-01 (not same as Duo Plus V4; 15 programs, 2 rings, silicone egg pan).
- Warranty: 1-year limited, original owner, authorized retailers only (instantpot.com/pages/product-warranties, eff. April 1 2026). Registration optional.
- Parts: sealing ring $9.99, 2-pack $14.99, 6QT stainless pot $39.99 (instantpot.com accessories, Oct 2026). Manuals: replace ring every 12–18 months.
- Water test: 3 cups water, sealing, Pressure Cook 5 min, quick release (Instant Pot test-run page, summary). Do not fill past 2/3; 1/2 for rice/beans. Minimum liquid 6 qt: 1–1½ cups (sources conflict).
- Canning: NCHFP (UGA) 2019 "we do not support the use of the USDA canning processes in the electric multi-cooker appliances"; Penn State Extension updated Aug 25 2025: not recommended.

## Experts
- America's Test Kitchen multicookers review, May 14 2025: winner Pro 8Qt; Duo 6-qt slow-cooked poorly (stew pink after 8 h); Rio 6-qt didn't slow-cook well, sauté seemed underpowered, pot lacks handles; Duo Plus not tested.
- Food Network (updated Feb 21 2025): best overall Duo; Pro most presets.
- Tom's Guide "best Instant Pots 2026" (snippet): Rio best overall; Duo Plus most versatile; Duo Plus review: performed well except rice program; suggests upgrading Rio -> Duo Plus for sous vide/cake.
- Consumer Reports (Jan 1 2026): no Duo/Duo Plus/Rio verdict.
- TechGearLab Duo Plus V4 review 2022-10-12: V4 LCD, WhisperQuiet valve, anti-spin; sauté scorched pot.
- ATK "What to do when your Instant Pot says burn" 2022-04-15.
- Wirecutter current pick UNVERIFIED.
