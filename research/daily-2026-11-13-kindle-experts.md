## 1. Expert reviews (2026 hardware only unless noted)

Format: outlet, date, device, URL. Then what the page says.

### 1.1 Tom's Guide, Kindle Paperwhite Signature Edition (2026)
- Date 2026-10-28. https://www.tomsguide.com/computing/e-readers/kindle-paperwhite-signature-edition-2026-review (reviewer Tony Ware not confirmed; page read)
- Verdict (verbatim): "arguably the best iteration of the company's flagship e-reader—even if it comes with a steep price."
- Tested: two weeks; battery at 70% after two weeks of use (about 5.5 more weeks projected by the reviewer); Amazon claims 12 weeks, reviewer says he "haven't had the e-reader long enough to test" that claim. Display unchanged: 7 in, 300 ppi. Says the display "isn't anti-glare as advertised" but minimizes glare.
- Cons: price $249 (vs $189 prior Signature at launch), pogo-pin charging replaces Qi, dock is $59.
- Note: the text says "32GB of RAM" (error; it is storage).

### 1.2 Tom's Guide, Kindle Colorsoft Signature Edition (2026)
- Date 2026-10-31. https://www.tomsguide.com/computing/e-readers/kindle-colorsoft-signature-edition-2026-review
- Verdict: "arguably the best color e-reader right now, especially for Kindle users, though its steep price might deter some."
- Tested: battery 95% to 68% in two weeks at about an hour a day, adaptive brightness; resolution 300 ppi black and white, 150 ppi colour; "I still prefer the Kindle Paperwhite Signature Edition for its clearer and sharper black-and-white display."
- Cons: $319 ($40 up), proprietary charging, colour at half resolution, darker panel than Paperwhite.
- Pointer: recommends basic Kindle at $149 or $199 Paperwhite if price matters.

### 1.3 Tom's Guide, Kindle Colorsoft (labelled 2026), Kobo comparison
- Date 2026-06-19 (stale prices, see section 0). https://tomsguide.com/computing/e-readers/kindle-colorsoft-2026-review
- Verdict: "fantastic e-reader"; Colorsoft is "$90 pricier than the closest Kobo alternative, an increase of 56%".
- Tested: Kindle "displayed the reading material a touch faster than the Kobo" on simultaneous power-on; battery "up to 8 weeks" claim, reviewer's unit at 37% (heavy use); IPX8 on both.
- Kobo points: Kobo lets you change tap zones; Kindle can't. Kindle Unlimited $11.99 vs Kobo Plus $7.99/$9.99.

### 1.4 Tom's Guide, Kobo Clara Colour review
- Date 2026-04-13 (before May price rise). https://www.tomsguide.com/computing/e-readers/kobo-clara-colour-review

- Findings: warm light, IPX8, native Libby, 49-hour battery per reviewer's headline spec; downside smaller Kobo library; for BorrowBox (UK) needs manual USB loading. Prices quoted are $159 Clara Colour, $139 Clara BW, $229 Libra Colour (all now higher).

## 3. Official manuals and help pages
1. **Remove lockscreen ads (Amazon help, read 2026-11-13).** https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GJHYWUURJ5A6YMBU ("Remove Ads from Your Kindle E-Reader or Tablet"). Steps verbatim: "Go to the Manage Your Devices page. Select Devices, and then select your Kindle e-reader or tablet. Under Special Offers, click Remove offers. A pop-up window then displays the price to remove ads. Select End Offers and Pay the Fee to proceed." Also: "Your 1-Click payment method is charged for a one-time payment". Page links Manage Your Content and Devices. No dollar amount is stated. Background page: https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GFNWCZJAM3SBQQZD ("you can unsubscribe by making a one-time payment to cover the difference between the device price with and without lockscreen ads"; ads on Kindle e-readers show "as a screensaver ... and on your home screen marked as 'Sponsored'"). Neither page names 2026 models, no date on page.
2. **Airplane mode.** Official page found: https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=G5HYWUVWRXFSCH7E (Wi-Fi troubleshooting; its first quick action is "Turn off Airplane mode"). Per SNIPPET of Amazon safety page for 10th gen, Airplane Mode is under Settings. There is no 2026-model manual or any Amazon page saying Airplane mode prevents Ask this Book: UNVERIFIED. Tom's Guide (1.1) confirms the pull-down menu has an "airplane mode" toggle on the 2026 Signature.
3. **Ask this Book / Story So Far / Recaps.** Amazon source: https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/books-and-authors/kindle-recaps-feature-ebook-series-refreshers (page updated 2026-ish; read). Says: "'Ask this Book' lets you ask questions about the book you're reading and receive spoiler-free answers." and "available to U.S. customers across thousands of best-selling English-language eBooks, whether purchased or borrowed." It is a news post, not a settings manual. **No Amazon help page with an off switch was found.** Reactor (2025-12-12) quotes Amazon: always on, no opt-out for publishers. Whether the feature is on the 2026 e-readers (not just apps) is UNVERIFIED in this pass; the aboutamazon lineup post (https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/new-kindle-lineup-2026, 2026-10-01) text read did not mention Ask this Book (matches on "AI" were navigation links only).
4. **Kobo Libby / OverDrive.** https://help.kobo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017677053-Borrow-eBooks-from-the-public-library-using-your-Kobo-eReader (read). Lists Libby built-in models including "Kobo Libra colour", "Kobo Clara Colour and Kobo Clara BW". Says: "all you need is a library card and a Wi Fi connection to borrow eBooks"; Libby available in "Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia and New Zealand". Companion page https://help.kobo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4477058367895-About-the-Libby-app : after software 6.0 or later "users will need to re-add their library card"; sign in via QR code, URL and code, or "Sign in with Libby"; then Discover to search and borrow. Page date not shown. Country list in the first page's header says only Canada, but the body lists US: Kobo's page is internally inconsistent; the guide should say "built in for US libraries per Kobo's own text".
   Note on Libby on Kindle: Libby sends books to Kindle in the US (Tom's Guide, SNIPPET from YouTube comments only, not an official source). UNVERIFIED for 2026 models.
