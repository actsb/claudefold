## Corrections to the blueprint
1. "Live view works without a plan" is now **confirmed on ring.com** (Live View support article). Person detection needs a plan (Ring blog).
2. **HKSV for one camera needs only iCloud+ 50GB ($0.99/mo)**, not 200GB (Apple, Sept 16, 2026).
3. eufy cloud: **$3.99/mo Basic, $13.99/mo Plus** (eufy.com), so 3-year eufy-with-cloud = $143.64 on Basic monthly or $119.97 annual.
4. "Reolink Video Doorbell 2nd Gen" is the **battery** model; the wired WiFi/PoE are still first-generation. Microsd max: 256GB (PoE/WiFi) vs 512GB (Battery 2nd Gen).
5. G410 Select = G410 + 3-month HomeGuardian (vs 1 month), $135 vs $130 at launch (MacRumors).
