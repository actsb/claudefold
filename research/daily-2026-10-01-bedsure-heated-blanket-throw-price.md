## 0. Must-disclose: 2023 CPSC recall
- Bedshe International (Bedsure) recalled about 350,000 electric heating blankets and pads on 2023-03-09 for a controller malfunction (fire and burn hazard). Reported by the CPSC notice and NY DHSES (via search snippet).
- Models: blankets BS-HB5060 (this is the 50x60 throw size), BS-HB6284, BS-HB7284, BS-LV0A; pads BS-HP2422, BS-HP1224, BS-HP2433.
- Reported: 137 incident reports, 17 burn injuries. Sold on Amazon.com and BedsureHome.com, Aug-Nov 2022. Remedy: stop use, full refund.
- Current listings carry different ASINs and mention ETL certification and preheat. Whether the current throw is a redesigned controller is UNVERIFIED. Tell readers to check the model number against the recall list. Do not imply the recall is irrelevant.
- Source: https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2023/Bedshe-International-Recalls-Bedsure-Electric-Heating-Blankets-and-Pads-Due-to-Fire-and-Thermal-Burn-Hazards-Recall-Alert
- Separate: a Sept 2026 Hoodline article covers a JKMAX heated blanket recall (195,000 units). That is a different brand. Details UNVERIFIED.

## 6. Running cost vs a 1500W space heater
- Rate: EIA national residential average. Search snippets show EIA-based figures of 17.91 c/kWh (2026 average, EIA Electric Power Monthly via energyfactbook.com), 19.41 c/kWh (May 2026), and 18.34 c/kWh (Sept 2026, electricchoice.com). EIA STEO Dec 2025 forecast: 18.02 c/kWh for 2026 (17.29 in 2025). I did not read the EIA page directly. Recommended citation: EIA Electric Power Monthly, Table 5.6.A, https://www.eia.gov/electricity/monthly/. Re-check the latest value before publishing.
- Calculation at 17.91 c/kWh (my arithmetic): 1500 W = 1.5 kWh/hour, so $0.27 per hour (about $2.15 for an 8-hour night). A 50x60 throw at 50-100 W (range from solartechonline/alibaba insight pages, not a Bedsure spec; check the label) costs about $0.009-$0.018 per hour, so roughly 15 to 30 times cheaper per hour. The comparison is not equal: a space heater warms the room; a throw warms one person.
- Sensitivity: at 19.41 c/kWh the heater is $0.29/hour; at 15 c/kWh it is $0.225/hour. One source's "$0.18 per hour" implies 12 c/kWh and is outdated for the national average.

## Writing guardrails
- Say "reported at $X on [date] by [source]"; never "costs $X". Link to Amazon with a "price may have changed" note.
- Include the 2023 recall paragraph and the model-number check.
- Mark the UNVERIFIED items above in the article or leave them out: Costco, Home Depot, Temu/AliExpress, S&S/coupons, 10-year warranty scope, Prime Big Deal Days throw price, Serta/Beautyrest/Biddeford prices.
- Affiliate disclosure needed. Do not call Good Housekeeping's result a Bedsure throw "winner" unless the model is confirmed.
