## 2. America's Test Kitchen / Cook's Country / Cook's Illustrated: plastic containers (READ)
- URL: https://www.americastestkitchen.com/cookscountry/equipment_reviews/2061-the-best-plastic-food-storage-containers-for-every-need. The cookscountry.com and cooksillustrated.com/articles/661 URLs both returned the identical page (same ATK review hub, same 598,339-byte response), so there is no separate Cook's Illustrated article text to cite.
- Exact page title: "The Best Plastic Food Storage Containers of 2026 | America's Test Kitchen"; headline "The Best Plastic Food Storage Containers". Author: Valerie Sizhe Li (associate editor, ATK Reviews). Published: December 10, 2025 (page metadata datePublished and dateModified both 2025-12-10).
- Verdict: Rubbermaid Brilliance Food Storage Containers Set, 10 Piece (model 2183421, Tritan; one 9.6-cup, two 3.2-cup, two 1.3-cup containers; gasket attached; latch lids) is the Top Pick/Winner. Page says it was "impressed with the seal quality, durability, and flat lids".
- Paraphrase of findings: two latches per container clip on securely and reinforce the gasket; a large container filled with blue water and shaken vigorously let no drops escape; to open, release both latches and lift a protruding lid corner; gaskets are permanently attached, soft enough to clean with a sponge without removing them. The 9.6-cup fits a potluck dish, 3.2-cup fits a sandwich or salad, 1.3-cup suits snacks. The page text supplied here did not list a flaw for the winner in its summary paragraph; the page's ratings table (Performance, Ease of Use, Durability, Cleanup) is graphical, star values not captured.
- Test method (page): shake 15 s with dyed water; oil-packed tuna overnight and pasta sauce for several days then check odor; open/close 100 times; drop filled with water; freeze filled overnight and drop from about 2.5 ft; repeat leak test; week-long home testers. (The earlier brief said 25 latch cycles; the current page says 100.)
- Design points the page makes: gaskets plus latches beat press-down lids, which "consistently leaked"; flat lids stack; attached gaskets preferred. Tritan and polypropylene sets were "equally successful" in toughness tests.
- Caveats ATK states (important for our post): ATK suggests never microwaving food in plastic and not cleaning plastic in the dishwasher (hand wash in warm soapy water); never put hot food in plastic, cool to room temperature first; limit time fatty and acidic foods sit in plastic (Dr. Jane Muncke, Food Packaging Forum, quoted); cites a 2023 study that micro/nanoplastics can move from containers to food; "well-cared for" plastic lasts three to five years (Michael H. Tunick, Drexel University food science); sunlight can accelerate degradation. This conflicts with Rubbermaid's own dishwasher/microwave-reheat guidance (see section 6): present both.
- Other sets on the page: OXO Good Grips Prep & Go 20 Piece Set; Ello Plastic Mixed Set (Recommended with Reservations: a few droplets leaked in the shake test, tuna odor lingered); Tupperware Date, Store & Freeze. Not Pyrex or Glasslock (both are glass; see below).

## 3. ATK glass containers review (READ), for the glass comparison
- URL: https://www.americastestkitchen.com/equipment_reviews/1876-glass-food-storage-containers. Title: "The Best Glass Food Storage Containers of 2026 | America's Test Kitchen". Author: Valerie Sizhe Li. Published December 10, 2025.
- Winner: Rubbermaid Brilliance Glass Food Storage Containers (9-piece, model 2118308). ATK notes the Brilliance line won its plastic test too, and that plastic lids are interchangeable between the glass and plastic Brilliance sets. Seals never leaked; plastic lids keep weight down; tiny lid vents ease pressing the lid down; wide rims help carry hot dishes; flat lids stack.
- Runner pick: Bentgo Signature Leak-Proof Glass (glass lids, "Best (Almost) All-Glass Option").
- OXO Good Grips Smart Seal Glass set: Recommended with Reservations. Latches sealed and never leaked but were stiff; removable gaskets were easy to take out but the channels were hard to clean and dry; lids kept fishy odors; thick gaskets and stiff latches took significant force to close. (This is OXO Smart Seal, not OXO POP; POP was not on the page.)
- Pyrex FreshLock 14-piece: leakproof, but round/rectangular/square shapes need three stacks; narrow lid channel hard to clean; noticeable gasket odor over time; clunky stiff latches; the only glass set not oven-safe.
- Glasslock: not in either ATK page. No source for it in this pass.
- General glass vs plastic from ATK: glass can be heated and dishwashed at high heat without microplastic worry, works in microwave and usually the oven, and looks nicer for serving; but it can shatter, is heavy, and most glass sets still have plastic lids. Glass sets trended smaller (only one glass set had a container over 9 cups; large sizes were common in plastic).

## 6. How to use in the post
- Strongest verified expert claim: ATK's Dec 10, 2025 pick, with the shake, 100-cycle latch, freeze-and-drop tests and the attached-gasket/flat-lid reasoning.
- Honest counterpoint: the same ATK page says do not microwave plastic or use the dishwasher; the Rubbermaid care text (unverified here) allows both under conditions. Tell readers to follow the label for warranty purposes and know that ATK is more cautious.
- Glass comparison: ATK picked the Brilliance line in both materials; OXO Smart Seal and Pyrex FreshLock (glass) were less favored on stiff latches and hard-to-clean gasket channels. Glass wins on heat, dishwasher, stain/odor; plastic wins on weight, drop resistance and big sizes.
