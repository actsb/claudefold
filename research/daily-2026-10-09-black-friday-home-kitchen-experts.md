**Which categories experts say are worth buying on Black Friday** (explicit statements are rare; UNVERIFIED where inferred)

- Cookware: CR's cookware ratings page lists "BEST TIME TO BUY: November, December" (per search snippet). https://www.consumerreports.org/home-garden/cookware/
- Robot and stick vacuums: CR and Tom's Guide documented deep cuts in 2025 (see section 4); CR still does not recommend cordless sticks for reliability, so a discount is not a reason alone.

- Air purifier, bidet, spin mop, heated throw, multicooker: no expert statement found on whether Black Friday is the cheapest time. UNVERIFIED. RTINGS-based reasoning: the Winix 5530 is already priced accessibly.
