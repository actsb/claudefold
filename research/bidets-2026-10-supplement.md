## 0. Method & limits
- Research was done on 2026-10-05 with WebSearch only. WebFetch was blocked for youtube.com (including oEmbed), scientificamerican.com, nrdc.org, amazon.com, homedepot.com, slickdeals.net and news.ycombinator.com. WebSearch also refuses reddit.com, so **no Reddit (r/bidets) threads were found or cited**.
- WebSearch gives a model-written summary of the results plus a list of URLs. It does not give raw snippet text. Every claim below comes from those summaries. No page was opened by a person.
- Labels:
  - **[SNIPPET]**: the search summary states it and ties it to the URL given.
  - **[UNVERIFIED]**: the figure could not be tied to one specific page, or it is our own calculation.
  - **[MFR]**: manufacturer or seller claim.
- Video IDs and titles are copied exactly from youtube.com URLs and result titles in the search results. No channel name could be confirmed (see §1).

## 2. Environment & cost facts

### 2a. Toilet paper use (US)
- **141 rolls per person per year (~12.7 kg).** This is an estimate from Statista's market model.
  - Source: Statista, "The U.S. Leads the World in Toilet Paper Consumption", Oct 5, 2018. https://www.statista.com/chart/15676/cmo-toilet-paper-consumption/ [SNIPPET]
- **"The average American uses three rolls of toilet paper a week."**
  - Source: NRDC/Stand.earth, "The Issue with Tissue", Feb 20, 2019. The exact page in the report was not identified. [SNIPPET]
  - Disputed by The Federalist, Mar 2, 2019: https://thefederalist.com/2019/03/02/no-average-american-doesnt-use-three-rolls-toilet-paper-per-week/ (title only).
- **Americans are "just over 4 percent of the world's population but account for over 20 percent of global tissue consumption."** This covers all tissue, not only toilet paper.
  - Source: NRDC 2019 report, https://www.nrdc.org/sites/default/files/issue-tissue-how-americans-are-flushing-forests-down-toilet-report.pdf ; press release https://www.nrdc.org/media/2019/190220 [SNIPPET]
- **"The average American household uses more than 100 pounds of tissue paper annually."** This covers all tissue.
  - Source: NRDC resource page, undated, https://www.nrdc.org/resources/issue-tissue [SNIPPET]
- **Unattributed figures:** "more than 50 pounds [of tissue] per person" and "85 rolls per person per year" [UNVERIFIED].

### 2b. Trees, water, chlorine and energy to make toilet paper
- **Scientific American (Earth Talk column), "Wipe or Wash? Do Bidets Save Forest and Water Resources?", Dec 16, 2009.** https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/earth-talks-bidets/ [SNIPPET]
  - "Americans use 36.5 billion rolls of toilet paper every year, representing the pulping of some 15 million trees."
  - "473,587,500,000 gallons of water to produce the paper and 253,000 tons of chlorine for bleaching."
  - Manufacturing energy: "about 17.3 terawatts of electricity annually" (unit as given in the snippet).
  - **Caveat:** these are figures from Justin Thomas (metaefficient.com) quoted by the column. They are not Scientific American's own research. Cite them as "according to Justin Thomas of MetaEfficient, quoted in Scientific American (2009)".
- **"37 gallons of water, 1.3 kWh of electricity and ~1.5 lb of wood per roll."** This is widely repeated, e.g.:
  - Big Think: https://bigthink.com/health/toilet-paper-is-a-giant-waste-of-resources/
  - ManyBidets: https://www.manybidets.com/blog/be-green/
  - **Caution:** Lloyd Alter says he started the 37-gallon figure himself in a 2009 TreeHugger post. Working from Environmental Product Declarations, he now estimates **6–13 gallons per roll**. Source: "Who first said it takes 37 gallons of water to make a roll of toilet paper?", https://lloydalter.substack.com/p/who-first-said-it-takes-37-gallons (date not shown) [SNIPPET]. **Do not present 37 gal as established fact.**
- **NRDC "The Issue with Tissue":**
  - 2.0 report, June 2020: https://www.nrdc.org/sites/default/files/issue-with-tissue-2-report.pdf [SNIPPET]
  - "Toilet paper made with recycled content has one-third the carbon footprint of toilet paper made from forest fiber." The exact NRDC page is uncertain. [SNIPPET]
  - **6th edition, Sep 16, 2024:** scored 145 at-home tissue products, including 61 toilet paper brands; P&G/Charmin got an F for the sixth year. [SNIPPET]
    - Fact sheet: https://www.nrdc.org/sites/default/files/2024-09/Issue_with_Tissue_6th_edition_0.pdf
    - Press release: https://www.nrdc.org/press-releases/nrdcs-issue-tissue-shows-procter-gamble-fails-sustainable-toilet-paper-test
  - Newer editions exist: a fact sheet dated 2025-11 (https://www.nrdc.org/sites/default/files/2025-11/Issue_with_Tissue_SC_FS_25-12-A_03_locked.pdf), and an "eighth edition" mentioned on Oct 1, 2026. Details [UNVERIFIED].
  - No NRDC tree, water or chlorine totals appeared in the snippets.
- **"One tree ≈ 1,500 rolls; 31 million trees/yr"** [UNVERIFIED, no source].

## 4. Owner voices (paraphrased)
**No Reddit or r/bidets threads could be reached**, because WebSearch blocks reddit.com. A person needs to search Reddit by hand before we write "Reddit users say". The points below come from retailer reviews, Amazon Q&A, Hacker News and editorial reviews. Post dates were mostly not shown.

### 4a. LUXE NEO 185
**Praise**
- **4 years of ownership.** The owner hasn't bought toilet paper in a year or more. They missed a heated seat and warm water less than expected, and never upgraded to a TOTO. Source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=19949418. It is unclear whether the comment is on HN or quoted from Reddit. [SNIPPET]
- **Cold water is less of a shock than feared.** Owners say it is fine once you expect it, and some call it "refreshing". [SNIPPET; blended across these pages]
  - Walmart: https://www.walmart.com/reviews/product/51435055
  - Best Buy: https://www.bestbuy.com/site/reviews/luxe-neo-185-non-electric-self-cleaning-nozzle-universal-attachment-bidet-blue/6410339
  - Home Depot: https://www.homedepot.com/p/reviews/LUXE-Bidet-NEO-185-Plus-Non-Electric-Attachable-Bidet-System-Bidet-Attachment-for-Toilet-Seat-Patented-Features-Chrome-NeoPlus185ch/341835349/3
- **Easy install, cheap, cleans well.** This is overall sentiment, not one owner. Source: https://www.walmart.com/reviews/product/1216184601 [SNIPPET]
- **Editorial (not owners):** [SNIPPET]
  - CNN Underscored calls the NEO 185 Plus its runner-up attachment: good pressure control for about $60, but less comfortable than the TUSHY Classic 3.0. https://www.cnn.com/cnn-underscored/reviews/best-bidets
  - toiletbidet.com calls it a good beginner choice. https://www.toiletbidet.com/luxe-bidet-neo-185-review.php

**Complaints**
- **Feels cheaply made;** a plastic insert can become a blockage. From one of the Walmart, Best Buy or Home Depot pages above; which one is unclear. [SNIPPET]
- **Nozzles can't be repositioned, and pressure is hard to set:** too low is slow, too high is uncomfortable. Source: toiletbidet.com (editorial, not an owner). [SNIPPET]

**Failure modes**
- **Nozzle stopped retracting after 7 months.** Vinegar didn't help. The cause was a swollen O-ring that can't be reached without breaking the nozzle assembly. This is the clearest long-term failure report found. Source: Amazon Q&A, https://www.amazon.com/ask/questions/TxJCKDKC6G9QOZ/ [SNIPPET]
- **Supply-line / T-valve leaks across the LUXE NEO line.** ClassAction.org has an open lawsuit *investigation* (not a ruling) into leaks at the hose connection on the Luxe 90/95, NEO and NEO Plus. Some reports mention flooding. It is not specific to the NEO 185. Source: https://www.classaction.org/luxe-bidet-leaking-supply-line-lawsuit [SNIPPET]
- **T-adapter leaks** usually come from a missing or misplaced rubber washer, or from cross-threading. LUXE sends replacement T-adapters. [MFR]
  - NEO 320 manual: https://files.bbystatic.com/g1OhCvHPcM/tor1YqrDWNw==/d4954bc6-c102-4ad3-8bbe-cc7e62c6e1af.pdf
- **Nozzle sticking** from hard-water deposits, or when the unit sits too close to the tank. Source: https://manuals.plus/luxe/luxe-bidet-neo-185-owner-s-manual/attachment/luxe-bidet-neo-185-troubleshooting-1 [MFR]
- **Seat shifting or misalignment,** worse with seats that have outer hinge tabs or a curved underside. [SNIPPET]
  - https://www.justanswer.com/plumbing/tdb6y-luxe-bidet-185-toilet-seat-misalignment.html
  - https://www.amazon.com/ask/questions/Tx1HJCUPGI754T2
- **Generic, not LUXE-specific:** one HN commenter's cheap attachment had plastic supply threads and developed a small leak only after 8 years. Source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22618565 [SNIPPET]
- **Hinge breakage, gradual pressure loss, cracked dial:** no owner reports found [UNVERIFIED].

**First-timer advice** (mostly from the manufacturer, not owners)
- Move the unit forward if the nozzle hits the tank. [MFR] Source: the manuals.plus troubleshooting page above.
- Tighten the seat bolts firmly while holding the nuts underneath. Prefer a flat-bottomed seat without outer hinge tabs. [MFR][SNIPPET]
  - NEO 185 Plus manual: https://images.thdstatic.com/catalog/pdfImages/4a/4a360d59-8ca6-4e67-8588-5881b2b792c2.pdf
  - The JustAnswer page above.
- Turn the water on slowly and watch for drips. Hand-tighten plus about a quarter turn; don't overtighten. Check the washer is seated. One LUXE source gives a different tightening instruction, so check the current manual. [MFR]
  - LUXE manual: https://images.thdstatic.com/catalog/pdfImages/85/85e4cd87-fa67-4885-a0ab-ce75ca3bbb48.pdf
  - https://luxebidet.com/pages/first-experience
- If the pressure is too strong, close the supply valve partway. [MFR]
- **Warranty:** 18 months, plus 6 more with registration. Source: toiletbidet.com [SNIPPET]

**First-timer takeaways** (our reading of the complaints, not tips owners wrote)
- Thread the metal T-valve onto the plastic inlet carefully; it cross-threads easily.
- Check the handle for leaks during the first weeks.
- Check the M300's opening size and bolt-hole fit on an elongated bowl before buying.
- Braided hose, turning the water on slowly, not overtightening: no Kohler owner snippets found saying this [UNVERIFIED].
