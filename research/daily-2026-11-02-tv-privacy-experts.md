## 0. Corrections and new facts that change the blueprint

1. **LG says ACR is OFF by default.** LG global newsroom statement "Understanding privacy on LG smart TVs", dated Sep 12, 2026 (page header): "ACR is an optional feature that is turned off by default" and users can "disable the feature at any time through TV settings" (no menu path given; no mention of Copilot). https://www.lg.com/global/newsroom/news/statements/statement-understanding-privacy-on-lg-smart-tvs/ . The blueprint treats Live Plus as a switch to turn off; say "LG states it is off unless you accept the Viewing Information agreement during setup; check yours".
2. **Samsung does have a samsung.com support page for ACR** (blueprint says none found): https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10010616/ "Samsung Smart TV Automatic Content Recognition (ACR) Feature". No date on page. Text read: ACR runs only if you opted in to Viewing Information Services during setup; "You can turn ACR on or off at any time by going to the Settings menu on your Smart TV and updating your preferences." It gives no step-by-step path, so use Consumer Reports for the path.
3. **Model numbers confirmed by RTINGS page titles (2026-11-02):** Samsung S90H = QN42/48/55/65/77/83S90HAEXZA (US "A" suffix, so QN55S90HAEXZA); TCL QM7L = 55QM7L/65/75/85/98QM7L; Roku Pro Series 2025 = **55R8C5**/65R8C5/75R8C5 (the blueprint's "55R8B5" is wrong or older; kitele's R8C5 for 2025 matches RTINGS); Roku Pro Series OLED = 55RAD5/65RAD5; LG C6 77/83" are the separate C6H (OLED77C6HUP). Sony Bravia 7 II US codes: abt.com lists K75XR70M2 and K98XR70M2, so 55" is probably K55XR70M2 (UNVERIFIED for 55").
4. **Roku Pro Series OLED** (TechRadar, James Holland, 2026-10-06): 462 nits HDR (Dark mode) vs 835 for LG B6, "hands-free voice control is gone", Roku OS rated 4/5 for smart TV. Launch/MSRP in blueprint stays.
5. **Texas status (checked 2026-11-02):** Samsung (Mar 1, 2026) and LG (May 11, 2026) settled; no settlement found for Sony or TCL (a vendor blog says Sony and TCL cases saw no reported motions; weak). Hisense reportedly had a TRO (sources conflict). Texas AG pages: LG https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/news/releases/attorney-general-ken-paxton-secures-major-agreement-lg-protect-texans-privacy-and-stop-data-being (fetch returned HTTP 402, snippet only); Samsung https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/news/releases/attorney-general-paxton-secures-major-agreement-samsung-ensure-texans-are-protected-smart-tvs ; TCL petition https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/sites/default/files/images/press/TCL%20TV%20Petition%20Filed.pdf .
6. Gemini is also on Sony Bravia (Bravia 3 II video title says "120Hz & Gemini"; 9to5Toys URL "sony-2026-bravia-7-ii-hdr-google-tv-gemini-deals", 2026-09-03; abt.com lists "Google TV with Gemini" for K75XR70M2). So the blueprint's guess that Sony is likely lowest on AI is weak: treat Sony as "Gemini on Google TV" like TCL. No page found on turning Gemini off on any TV (UNVERIFIED).
