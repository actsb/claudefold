## Verdict

1. **The account is still on probation, so the most important number is 3.** Amazon only reviews the application after three qualifying sales within 180 days of applying. Without them, the application is withdrawn ([Amazon help G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM), n.d.; [G7MJTPEP9NC3YKMG](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G7MJTPEP9NC3YKMG), n.d.). Prime Big Deal Days (Oct 6–7) and Black Friday week (about Nov 19–30) are the two best chances this year to get those sales.
2. **There is one compliance problem in our own posts, and it should be fixed before review.** Amazon's policies forbid showing Amazon customer reviews or star ratings unless they come from the Creators API or PA-API ([Program Policies](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies), n.d.). Several posts do exactly this. The bidet guide shows a "How about 40,000 Amazon ratings split" bar chart and the line "Of about 40,000 Amazon ratings, 79% are five stars"; the O-Cedar, Levoit, NOCO, robot-vacuum and earbuds posts contain similar lines (grep of `posts/*/src/00-easy.html`, 2026-10-05). Hand-typed Amazon prices ("about $46–$50 when we checked") are a grey area under the same policy. The safe pattern is: no Amazon star figures, no Amazon review quotes, and no Amazon price stated as a number. Use price bands, prices from other retailers, and "check today's price" buttons.
3. **Commissions are thin, so choose categories deliberately.** On the standard table, Kitchen and Automotive pay 4.5%. Home, Home Improvement, Pet, Tools, Outdoors and Toys pay 3%. Health & Personal Care pays 1% ([Amazon Standard Commission Income Statement](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ), n.d.). Reported cuts in 2026 hit mainly publishers on custom rates ([Adweek, May 2026](https://www.adweek.com/media/amazon-associates-affiliate-rate-cuts-publishers/)). Tilt the queue toward $40–$150 kitchen, car and tool products, and avoid personal-care items.
4. **Expect little money until traffic arrives.** Benchmarks suggest roughly $5–$25 of Amazon revenue per 1,000 pageviews, so $100 a month needs about 4,500–20,000 pageviews a month ([INFERENCE] from the [Geniuslink](https://geniuslink.com/blog/under-or-over-performing-epc-conversion-rates/) and [Fat Stacks](https://fatstacksblog.com/amazon-affiliate-revenue-desktop-mobile-tablet/) data in §3). For a one-month-old site, the work that pays is publishing **Black Friday, gift and January pages in the next three weeks**, so Google has time to index them.
5. **Korea is not a blocker.** Fill in the W-8BEN tax interview, answer that all services are performed outside the US, and get paid by US-style direct deposit into a Payoneer or Wise USD account. Korean banks and KRW do not appear among Amazon's local-currency payout countries.
6. **Keep AdSense off the money pages, at least for now.** There is no policy conflict, but at this traffic level AdSense would earn a few dollars. Ad networks themselves advise switching ads off on top affiliate posts.

## 1. Compliance (2025–2026)

### 1.1 Three qualifying sales in 180 days
- "the Associates team will check your application once you've driven qualified sales (we require at least three within the first 180 days)". The review covers every listed site, which needs "robust original content" (roughly 10 posts), and a decision usually comes "within a day or two" of the third sale. [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM, n.d.]
- An application "can be withdrawn if there were not enough qualifying sales for 180 days after your application was submitted". [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G7MJTPEP9NC3YKMG, n.d.]
- You can reapply, but Amazon cannot reinstate a rejected ID. When reapplying, create new tracking IDs and swap the links. [https://affiliate-program.amazon.sg/help/node/topic/G5CHSDWVK9JWVXRK (Singapore mirror of the US help text), n.d.; https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies, n.d.]
- Orders by you or your family don't count, and buying through your own links breaks the agreement: "You will not purchase any Product(s) through Special Links for use by you or for resale." [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/participation/, n.d.; the household point is **[BLOG]** https://getaawp.com/blog/amazon-affiliate-program-requirements/, 2026]
- Update effective **2026-04-14**: a purchase must be shipped or streamed **and paid for** within 180 days to earn commission. A new "original content" standard requires "commentary, analysis or transformation". [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/compare, 2026-04-14]

### 1.2 Disclosure wording and placement
- The Operating Agreement requires you to "clearly and prominently" state **"As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases."** [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement, reported last updated 2025-10-15; policy changes effective 2026-04-14]
- Amazon's help page lists two separate duties: "(1) include a legally compliant disclosure with your links and (2) identify yourself on your Site as an Amazon Associate". A link disclosure "could be as simple as '(paid link)', '#ad', or '#CommissionsEarned'" and "should be placed near any affiliate link... They shouldn't have to hunt for it." [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GHQNZAU6669EZS98, n.d.]
- FTC FAQ: "paid link" next to the link should be adequate. "Affiliate link" alone may not be understood. A single disclosure on the home page or at the foot of the page is not enough. [https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking, revised 2023] The revised Endorsement Guides (16 CFR 255) define "clear and conspicuous" as "difficult to miss" and, online, "unavoidable". [https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2023/07/26/2023-14795/guides-concerning-the-use-of-endorsements-and-testimonials-in-advertising, 2023-07-26]
- **Our setup:** the byline sentence sits above the first link, and every button carries "(paid link)". That matches Amazon's own example. [INFERENCE] Low-cost insurance: repeat the Associate sentence in a sitewide footer or sidebar, and add "#ad" to pins that link to Amazon.

### 1.3 Prices, availability, ratings
- Prices or availability may be shown only through an Amazon-served link or through data from Creators API, PA-API or a Data Feed. Non-image data may be cached for at most 24 hours. Data refreshed less often than hourly needs a date and time stamp plus the standard disclaimer: "Product prices and availability are accurate as of the date/time indicated and are subject to change…" [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies, n.d.]
- "may not display or otherwise use Amazon customer reviews or star ratings… unless [obtained] through Creators API or PA API." [same page; also https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/participation/, n.d.]
- Typed-in static prices fall outside both allowed routes. Third-party guides treat them as a violation. **[BLOG]** https://geniuslink.com/blog/amazon-associates-requirements/, n.d.
- **Action:** remove the Amazon star-split widgets and the "N Amazon ratings, X% five stars" lines. Stop quoting Amazon review text. Our own score and owner themes summarized from Reddit or other retailers are fine. Turn "about $46–$50 when we checked (Amazon)" into a band ("under $50") or a non-Amazon price with its source. This also matches the README's rule that "no live Amazon price is ever stated as fact".

### 1.4 Images and the APIs
- SiteStripe dropped its Image and Text+Image options on 2023-12-01, and old image links stopped working around 2023-12-31. SiteStripe now makes text links and amzn.to short links. **[BLOG]** https://bloggingfornewbloggers.com/amazon-associates-sitestripe-images-update/ ; https://techtactician.com/amazon-sitestripe-features-update/
- PA-API 5 is retired. Amazon's docs banner said "PA-API will be deprecated on May 15th, 2026". Emails gave 2026-04-30 as the deprecation date and a later "migrate by June 30, 2026". The replacement is the **Creators API**. [https://webservices.amazon.com/paapi5/documentation/, 2026; https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/creatorsapi/docs/en-us/paapiv5-deprecation, 2026; https://github.com/internetarchive/openlibrary/issues/12255, 2026-04-01]
- Creators API access: "you must also have at least **10 qualifying sales within the past 30 days**". [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/creatorsapi/docs/en-us/introduction, n.d.] It is a rolling window: access is suspended below 10 and restored automatically. **[BLOG]** https://blog.freshstore.com/amazon-creators-api-credentials-eligibility/, 2026
- API images may not be stored as files, and image links may be cached for at most 24 hours. [Program Policies, n.d.]
- **Our setup:** Amazon has no rule against our own SVG product art; the image rules cover Amazon's Program Content only. **[BLOG]** https://getlasso.co/can-i-use-amazon-product-images-on-my-affiliate-website/. Do not draw Amazon logos, "Amazon's Choice" badges or Amazon-style stars into the art. [INFERENCE from the trademark rules in §1.6]

### 1.5 Links: shortening, cloaking, email, PDFs, social, Pinterest
- "You will not use a link shortening service… in a manner that makes it unclear that you are linking to an Amazon Site". "You will not cloak, hide, spoof, or otherwise obscure the URL…". Redirecting links are excluded. [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies, n.d.] amzn.to is fine. Our full `amazon.com/...&tag=verdictpicks-20` links with `rel="nofollow sponsored noopener"` (checked in `posts/2026-10-best-bidet-2026/post.html`) are the safest form.
- **Email:** the Agreement bans offline use, "printed material, mailing, SMS, MMS, email or attachment to email" [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/participation/, n.d.]. Amazon's help page now allows Special Links "via emails, SMS messages, or social media posts… to recipients who have opted-in… and are given an easy way to opt out" [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GQ5CMSPVXBXSFDFV, n.d.; a change reported around March 2024, **[BLOG]**]. Safest: newsletters link to the blog post, not to Amazon.
- **PDFs, ebooks and downloadable documents** are still not allowed. [https://associates.amazon.ca/help/node/topic/GQ5CMSPVXBXSFDFV, n.d.]
- **Social media and Pinterest:** "Site" includes social media content, and each profile must be listed with its full URL. Amazon's example: "www.instagram.com/YourHandle is correct but www.instagram.com is not, and this can lead to closure of your account" [https://affiliate-program.amazon.sg/help/node/topic/G5CHSDWVK9JWVXRK, n.d.]. Pinterest lists Amazon Associates US among supported affiliate partners [https://create.pinterest.com/blog/affiliate-marketing-link-basics/, n.d.]. Direct Amazon pins with #ad are widely used **[BLOG]** (https://www.lilachbullock.com/can-you-use-pinterest-promote-amazon-affiliate-links/). Our workflow pins to the blog post, which is the lower-risk route. **Action:** confirm that the Associates "Websites and Mobile Apps" list contains `https://acts39.blogspot.com` and the full Pinterest profile URL.
- **Paid ads:** since 2026-04-14, purchases referred "through any paid or boosted advertisement linking to Amazon" are disqualified, with limited exceptions. Bidding on Amazon trademarks has long been a prohibited placement. [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/compare, 2026-04-14; /help/operating/participation/, n.d.] Do not run Pinterest or Meta ads straight to Amazon.
- Browser extensions and client-side software are prohibited. [/help/operating/participation/, n.d.]

### 1.6 Trademarks
- "Amazon" may not appear in a domain, subdomain, Store ID or social handle. Do not imply that Amazon endorses you, and do not alter its logos. [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/amazonmarks/, n.d.; Program Policies, n.d.] "Verdict Picks", `acts39.blogspot.com` and `verdictpicks-20` are all fine.
- "Prime Day" is a registered Amazon mark (USPTO 90844255). Amazon's own Associates Resource Center encourages Prime Day content, so descriptive use in post titles is fine. [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/resource-center/prime-your-audience-for-prime-day---steps-for-building-prime-day-buzz, n.d.; https://uspto.report/TM/90844255]
- We found no Associates rule specific to "Amazon's Choice" or "Best Seller" wording. Badges change often, so a stated badge can go stale and mislead. Prefer "our top pick". **[BLOG / INFERENCE]**

### 1.7 What review blogs commonly get closed for
From Amazon's rules; forum reports are flagged.
1. Incentives to click: money, rebates, giveaways or "support us by clicking". [/help/operating/participation/]
2. Own, family or household purchases. [same; **[FORUM/BLOG]** reports]
3. Unlisted traffic sources: a blog URL or social profile missing from the account. [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G44W6S8NGFJ94W9Y, n.d.; **[BLOG]** https://geniuslink.com/blog/amazon-associates-guide-to-getting-account-banned/]
4. Static or misleading prices, and Amazon star ratings or review text shown without the API (§1.3).
5. Cloaking, cookie stuffing, redirects, extensions (§1.5).
6. A missing or hidden disclosure (§1.2).
7. Links in PDFs, print, or email to people who have not opted in (§1.5).
8. Thin or copied content: the 2026-04-14 "commentary, analysis or transformation" rule. "Lack of original content" is the most reported rejection after the three sales. **[FORUM/BLOG]** https://affiliateshaven.com/amazon-associates-beginner-guide/
9. Paid or boosted ads straight to Amazon (§1.5).
10. VPN or shared-IP closures exist only as forum anecdotes. Clicking or buying through your own links from your household is the real risk. **[FORUM]** https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/6fa39f71-fee2-4b6b-af1f-d4301497c943

### 2.2 The 24-hour cookie
- A click opens a 24-hour session. The session ends when the customer orders, when 24 hours pass, or when they click another Associate's link. Items added to the cart during the session still count if ordered within **89 days** of the click. [Program Policies, https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies, n.d.]
- **Other products bought in the same session ("halo") still earn for off-site links.** The 2026-04-14 same-ASIN rule applies only to **Onsite** commissions. [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/compare, 2026-04-14; **[BLOG]** https://mentionlink.com/blog/2026/04/30/amazon-associates-april-2026-update/, 2026-04-30]
  - A conflicting **[BLOG]** claims halo ended for everyone (getchatads, 2026). Watch "All Other Items Ordered" in the reports to settle it.
- No rigorous data exists on the share of indirect sales. A **[BLOG]** says baskets often run 3–5× the linked item (https://brandid.app/blog/how-much-do-amazon-affiliates-make, 2026). This matters most in Q4, when one click can lead to a whole gift basket. [INFERENCE]

### 2.3 Bounties
- The bounty program is live with 35+ bounties, including Prime, Prime Video, Amazon Music, Audible, Subscribe & Save, Prime Student and the baby and wedding registries. [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/resource-center/how-to-earn-bounties, n.d.; https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/resource-center/the-amazon-bounty-program-101---a-guide-for-associates, n.d.]
- Typical amounts are from **[BLOG]** mirrors of Amazon's table: Prime trial $3; Audible $5 per trial and $10 per paid sign-up; Kindle Unlimited and Music Unlimited about $3; Amazon Business $15; registries about $3, with $5 also reported for baby. [https://moneymarketingconnection.com/amazon-bounty-programs-2026-earn-without-anyone-buying-a-product/, 2026; https://getaawp.com/blog/amazon-bounty-program/, n.d.]
- Prime-trial sign-ups through amazon.com/primeday and amazon.com/tryprimefree earn the bounty. [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GRAN5HBU74F8RDKL, n.d.]
- **Use:** add one "Not a Prime member? The 30-day trial gets you the deal price" line with a tryprimefree link to every deal post. It is a cheap bounty, and Big Deal Days prices require Prime. [INFERENCE]

### 2.4 Influencer Program, Onsite Commissions, Creator Connections
- **Influencer Program:** needs a YouTube, Instagram, Facebook or TikTok account. There is no official follower minimum; approvals are commonly reported at 1k–5k followers. **A blog-only Associate cannot join.** **[BLOG]** https://www.adbadger.com/blog/amazon-influencer-program/, 2026
- **Onsite Commission Income** requires Influencer registration and a dedicated Store ID [Program Policies, n.d.]. It unlocks after 3 approved videos [https://www.amazon.com/b?ie=UTF8&node=21423476011, n.d.]. Since 2026-04-14 it pays only on the same ASIN variant (or after a minimum viewing time for video) [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G4ARBJC7Z2NK48CA and /help/operating/compare, 2026-04-14]. Onsite rates are roughly half the off-site rates. **[BLOG]** https://www.influencer-hero.com/blogs/amazon-influencer-commission-rates, 2025
- **Creator Connections:** brand-funded bonus commissions. Reported eligibility is at least 100 clicks and at least $1 earned, and a storefront is not needed, so a blog Associate may qualify. **[BLOG]** https://myamazonguy.com/amazon-marketing/amazon-creator-connections-guide/, 2026. Check the Promotions or Creator Connections tab after the account is approved.
- **Verdict:** the faceless SVG/video house style could feed a YouTube Shorts channel later, but that is not a priority for Q4 2026. [INFERENCE]

## 3. What converts, and how much traffic is needed

### 3.1 Benchmarks
- **Conversion:** amazon.com converted 11.1% of clicks into orders, against a 2.3% average across 40+ programs (Geniuslink, 400k+ clicks, Q1 2022). Its suggested benchmark is 10–20% for amazon.com. **[VENDOR]** https://geniuslink.com/blog/under-or-over-performing-epc-conversion-rates/, 2022
- **EPC (earnings per click):** about $0.05 in Geniuslink's 2018 study (2M+ clicks), and about $0.10–$0.25 in a later Geniuslink figure. **[VENDOR]** https://geniuslink.com/blog/geniuslink-amazon-epc-improvement-roi/, 2018, updated
- **Amazon revenue per 1,000 sessions:** $14.01 overall, but $27.50 on desktop against $3.67 on mobile. **[BLOG income data]** https://fatstacksblog.com/amazon-affiliate-revenue-desktop-mobile-tablet/, n.d. Pinterest traffic is mostly mobile, so expect the low end.
- **Click-out rate:** 10–25% of visitors click through to Amazon on optimized sites. **[BLOG]** https://wpadvancedads.com/how-much-can-amazon-affiliates-make/, n.d.
- **Typical earnings:** most Amazon affiliates earn under $1k a month, and beginners $50–300. **[BLOG]** citing an Authority Hacker survey, https://manychat.com/blog/how-much-do-amazon-affiliates-make/, n.d.

### 3.3 Layout and CTA evidence
- **Comparison table with ratings and a sticky "back to table" button.** One home heating/cooling Amazon site tested this and got +6.5% clicks per visitor, **+67.7% orders per visitor** and **+43.1% revenue per visitor** (99% confidence). **[AGENCY CASE STUDY]** https://convertica.org/affiliate-case-study-43-1-increase-in-revenue-per-visitor/, n.d. Use our own scores in the table, not Amazon's stars (§1.3).
- **Links early on the page.** Links in the first three paragraphs are claimed to get 40–60% more clicks, and one site moving its comparison table above the fold reported +38% CTR and +22% EPC. **[BLOG, unsourced]** https://www.postaffiliatepro.com/faq/increase-affiliate-link-clicks/ ; https://www.getchatads.com/blog/increase-affiliate-conversion-rate-without-new-posts/, 2026. Our "Skip to the buy box" key-takeaways link already does this. Add one in-text paid link to the top pick in the lead paragraph. [INFERENCE]
- **CTA wording.** Switching "Buy Now" to "Check Price", among other changes, was credited with doubling revenue in under 30 days. Anecdotal and confounded. **[VENDOR]** https://getlasso.co/call-to-action-examples/, n.d. "Check today's price on Amazon" is also the price-free wording the policy favours (§1.3).
- **Text vs image links.** One affiliate's data: text links 9.41% conversion, image links 3.64%. **[FORUM]** https://my.wealthyaffiliate.com/ariefwibowo/blog/affiliate-link-in-text-vs-image, n.d. Keep contextual text links next to the buttons.
- **Price band.**
  - Amazon conversion is 15–25% under $20, 8–15% at $50–100, and 3–8% above $300. These are seller-listing figures, not affiliate data. **[BLOG]** https://ecombrainly.com/amazon-conversion-rate/, 2026
  - Our sweet spot is $40–$150 at 4.5% (Kitchen/Automotive). [INFERENCE]
  - Prime Big Deal Days baskets are small: average order $45.42, and 44% of orders were $20 or less ([Numerator, 2025-10-09](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/10/09/3164227/0/en/AMAZON-PRIME-BIG-DEAL-DAYS-EARLY-READ-RESULTS-ARE-IN-CONSUMERS-FOCUSING-ON-HOUSEHOLD-ESSENTIALS-NUMERATOR-REPORTS.html)).
- **Review count and Prime eligibility.** We found no quantified affiliate study; advice to favour high-review, Prime-eligible items is opinion only.

## 4. Seasonal calendar, October 2026 – January 2027

**Indexing lead time.** Google says crawling can take "a few days to a few weeks" ([Ask Google to recrawl](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/ask-google-to-recrawl), n.d.). Ahrefs (2025 update): only 1.74% of new pages reach the top 10 within a year, and of those, 40.8% got there within a month ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/how-long-does-it-take-to-rank-in-google-and-how-old-are-top-ranking-pages/), 2025). Google recommends **one permanent URL per event**, created "well before the sale", linked from the home page, and updated every year ([Google Search Central, Oct 2020](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2020/10/best-practices-black-friday)).

We found no official "4–8 weeks" rule. Our working targets are **6+ weeks ahead for search and 8+ weeks for Pinterest** [INFERENCE]. Pinterest says "45% of users start planning holidays 60+ days in advance" and that holiday activity "spikes as early as September" ([Pinterest Business, 2026](https://business.pinterest.com/blog/holiday-2026-starts-now-a-pinterest-guide-to-success/)).

## 5. Korea-resident Associate: tax, payment, blockers

- **Tax interview.** In Associates Central: Account Settings → View/Provide Tax Information → Take Interview. Amazon must have this information on file to pay. The default withholding for non-US people is "up to 30%" unless a valid W-8BEN is on file. [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GYJB2LE2AB473W2L, n.d.]
- **Services outside the US.** Amazon says a non-US Associate posting links to a US site "does not constitute US sourced income". The interview asks for a share of US time only if you were physically present in the US. [same page] Answer that **all services are performed outside the United States**; that should mean no US withholding. [INFERENCE from Amazon's text; "0%" outright is **[BLOG]**, https://beancount.io/blog/2026/05/10/form-w-8ben-w-8ben-e-foreign-vendor-30-percent-withholding-us-business-cross-border-payments-guide, 2026-05-10] The agreement also has non-US Associates agree to perform all services outside the US unless they tell Amazon otherwise. [Program Policies, n.d.]
- **US–Korea treaty** (signed 1976, in force 1979). Article 8 (business profits) exempts profits with no US permanent establishment. Article 14 (royalties, 10–15%) does not fit commissions. [https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/korea.pdf] Korean blogs saying "royalties 0%" are wrong on the label. **[KR-BLOG]** W-8BEN Rev. 10/2021: https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw8ben
- **Korean side.** Recurring blog commission income is normally 사업소득 (business income), reported in the May 종합소득세 (comprehensive income tax) return. The likely business code is 940306 1인미디어콘텐츠창작자 (one-person media creator) or 940909 (other self-employed). [https://www.nts.go.kr/nts/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?mi=2480&cntntsId=7802 (National Tax Service, snippet only); **[KR-BLOG]** https://help.3o3.co.kr/hc/ko/articles/19156591135385; **[KR-FORUM]** https://www.findsemusa.com/service/consult/consultView.do?qidx=26620] Confirm the code with a 세무사 (tax accountant).
- **Payment methods.** Direct deposit, Amazon.com gift card, or check "where available". Minimums are $10 for direct deposit and gift card, $100 for check. Payment comes about 60 days after month-end: January earnings arrive by the end of March. [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G63DR893K4DH55XZ, n.d.]
- **Currency.** Local-currency bank transfer covers about 52 countries via SWIFT, with no Amazon fee. **South Korea/KRW did not appear on any list we found.** [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/resource-center/receive-your-international-affiliate-earnings-in-your-local-bank, n.d.] The Agreement lets you pick a payment currency, converted at Amazon's rate. [Operating Agreement, n.d.]
- **Practical route.** Use a Payoneer or Wise USD account (US account and routing numbers) as a "US" direct-deposit account. Payoneer charges about 2% to convert; Wise is free for incoming ACH. **[BLOG]** https://geniuslink.com/blog/amazon-affiliates-payoneer/ ; **[KR-BLOG]** https://livehome.me/1915 ; https://www.propkorea.com/home/tools/payoutprovider/wise. No Amazon page endorses or forbids this.
  - Keep the payee name, the W-8BEN name and the receiving-account holder name identical, romanized exactly as on the passport. **[FORUM]**, Seller Central analogy: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/424421b3-6330-428f-b8b6-06cdd191d6bb
- **Checks.** Amazon charges a $15 fee for checks to US addresses and waives it abroad where direct deposit is unavailable. [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GGD9H76RMDDNEWAE, n.d., may be dated] A gift card is usable only on Amazon.com, which is of little use in Korea.
- **What blocks payment:**
  - No tax interview: Amazon can hold commissions. [Operating Agreement]
  - Fewer than 3 sales in 180 days: the application is withdrawn and the ID cannot be reinstated. Whether commissions already earned are paid is **unconfirmed**.
  - A policy violation: Amazon can withhold "any and all commission income". [Operating Agreement / Policies]
  - Commissions can be held after termination to cover returns.
  - After 3 years with no earnings, a maintenance fee of up to $10 a month. [Operating Agreement]

## 6. AdSense on the same pages

- **No conflict in Amazon's rules.** Amazon's agreement restricts how Amazon content is used, not other ads on your own site. [https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies, n.d.] Amazon Native Shopping Ads and banners were retired around 2023-08-25 to 2023-09-06. **[FORUM]** https://wordpress.org/support/topic/amazon-native-shopping-ads-retired/
- **Google's own rules:**
  - Affiliate links are allowed, but **thin affiliate pages** are not: merchant descriptions copied with no original value. Google lists value-adding examples: original reviews, testing, ratings, comparisons. [https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies; https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/48182]
  - Ads must not exceed content. [https://support.google.com/publisherpolicies/answer/11169917]
  - No placement that makes ads look like navigation or content, and content must not be styled to look like ads. [https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1346295]
  - On mobile, ads above 30% of the main content's height breach the Better Ads standard. [https://support.google.com/webtools/answer/7073774]
- **Blogger eligibility.** Applicants must be 18+ and have original, policy-compliant content [https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724]. The claim of "six months active" for some Asian countries is **[FORUM/BLOG]** only. We found no official six-month rule for Korea, and Korean guides cite 10–15+ substantial posts. **[KR-BLOG]** https://adsensefarm.kr/google-adsense-approval/
- **Effect on affiliate conversion.** Mediavine advises turning ads off on your best affiliate posts, because the auction can show competing products. Mediavine and Raptive both support per-page ad disabling. **[AD-NETWORK BLOG]** https://www.mediavine.com/blog/ads-and-affiliate-marketing/ ; https://help.raptive.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014667991. We found no controlled public test; the claim that ads cut affiliate CTR by 8–15% is **[BLOG, unsourced]** (https://automatetoprofit.com/display-ads-affiliate-site/, 2026).
- **RPM.** AdSense typically pays $2–6 per 1,000 pageviews, against $25–45 of affiliate revenue per 1,000 pageviews in one developer's case. **[BLOG]** https://www.techconda.com/2026/02/adsense-rpm-benchmarks.html (2026); https://dev.to/danie_rozin/why-we-chose-affiliate-links-over-display-ads-and-the-revenue-math-behind-it-4em5
- **Google Search.** The helpful-content self-assessment asks whether ads "distract from or interfere with the main content" (quoted via [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/what-is-helpful-content-google-387360)). Affiliate links need `rel="sponsored"` ([Google, 2021-07](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2021/07/link-tagging-and-link-spam-update)); ours already have it. Google's [guidance on high-quality reviews](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews) asks for first-hand evidence, measurements, comparisons and pros and cons; this was not re-checked this session.
- **Layout if AdSense is added [INFERENCE from the above]:**
  - Use Auto ads, but exclude the cover, lead, key takeaways, buy box and comparison table via Excluded areas ([https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/12626543](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/12626543)).
  - Use Page exclusions for the Black Friday hubs and gift guides ([https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9262311](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9262311)).
  - No anchor or vignette ads on money pages.
  - Keep mobile ad density well under 30%.
  - Never style our green "Check price" buttons near an ad unit.
- **Recommendation:** apply for AdSense once the blog has steady search traffic, and put ads on informational pages (how-to, care, explainers) first. Until then, AdSense would bring single-digit dollars a month and risks taking clicks away from Amazon. [INFERENCE]
