## 5. How often BF is truly the low (evidence)
- Visualping analysis of 1,500+ products (Oct to Cyber Monday 2025, reported by MobileSyrup Dec 4, 2025; country unstated, retailers named suggest Canada): about one-third of BF deals gave no real savings (avg 37% above lowest observed); tech had the lowest fake-deal share (33%). Example: Best Buy Sony WH-1000XM4 $278 vs $429.99 regular genuine. https://mobilesyrup.com/2025/12/04/some-2025-black-friday-deals-fake/ (fetched 2026-10-14)
