# Research brief: owners and shoppers on fake Black Friday deals (agent 1)

For: "Is That Black Friday Deal Real? How to Check a Sale Price in 2026"
Compiled: Nov 20, 2026 (Black Friday is Nov 27, 2026; Cyber Monday is Nov 30, 2026)
Method: about 50 web searches plus 4 page fetches. No amazon.com pages were opened.

House rules followed:
- No Amazon reviews, star ratings, review counts, Q&A or prices. Studies built on Amazon price data (Groundwork Collaborative, the TechRadar/Keepa analysis, the Techlicious coverage) are left out on purpose, so the article does not repeat Amazon prices.
- Everything is paraphrased. Quotes appear only where a search snippet or fetched page showed the literal text.
- Most retailer review pages (Target, Best Buy, Walmart, Sam's Club) carry no visible date in search results. Those are dated "page seen Nov 2026, review date not shown."
- UNVERIFIED marks anything I could not confirm against a second source or a primary page.

## A. Shopper evidence of fake or inflated "was" prices (US, 2024–2026)

### A1. Large tracking studies (US-relevant, not Amazon-based)

- **Consumers' Checkbook "Sale Fail" study.** Checkbook tracked 25+ items at 25 national chains every week for 24 weeks, starting February 2025.
  - The 19 non-honest retailers showed items as "on sale" about 76% of the time on average.
  - Only Apple, Costco and Dell consistently ran legitimate discounts. Walmart was borderline, with items on sale 48% of the time.
  - The named worst offenders were Bass Pro Shops, Bed Bath & Beyond, Dick's, Foot Locker, Gap, JCPenney, Michaels, Nordstrom, Old Navy and Wayfair.
  - Sources: Checkbook press release (PDF) — https://www.checkbook.org/pdfDocs/doc/Sale_Fail_2025_press_release.pdf — Nov 2025. Also PR Newswire — https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/sale-fail-consumer-group-tracks-major-retailers-sale-prices-for-months-finds-many-discounts-bogus-shopping-around-is-key-302617649.html — Nov 2025. Also WTOP — https://wtop.com/business-finance/2025/11/looking-forward-to-black-friday-sales-theyre-really-just-lying-to-you-warns-dc-consumer-expert — Nov 2025.
- **Kohl's reference-price disclaimer.** Checkbook singles out Kohl's disclaimer as the most absurd. Its reference price can be a former *or future* price, for the item *or a comparable item*, at Kohl's *or another retailer*. — Checkbook — https://www.checkbook.org/salefail/ — page undated (UNVERIFIED date). Kohl's was not on the 2025 worst-offender list.
- **Best Buy "Comparable Value."** Best Buy's pricing page says "Comparable Value" is based on prices at which the product or a comparable item was, *or in the future will be*, offered by Best Buy, marketplace sellers, manufacturers or other retailers. — Best Buy pricing policy — https://bestbuy.com/pricing — page seen Nov 2026.
- **Visualping 2025 report.**
  - Scope: daily tracking of 2,620 products at 9 retailers, Oct 1 – Dec 1, 2025.
  - Finding: 33% were "fake deals," meaning cheaper earlier in the season than their Black Friday/Cyber Monday price.
  - By category: luggage 61% and microwaves 50% were worst; backpacks 4% were best.
  - CAVEAT: Retail Insider describes the retailers as Canadian, and the report's intro gives a different product count (1,500+). Cite it as "a 2025 price-tracking study," not as US-specific.
  - Sources: Visualping — https://visualping.io/blog/black-friday-2025-fake-deals-report — Dec 2025. ConsumerAffairs — https://www.consumeraffairs.com/news/black-friday-warning-1-in-3-deals-were-cheaper-before-the-holiday-study-finds-110226.html — Dec 2025.
- **InvestigateTV local-TV report (carried by Gray stations, e.g. WBTV, KPTV, Cleveland19).**
  - The report opened with a Macy's shirt marked 40% off a $149 "original," which appeared higher than what the shirt had sold for.
  - A former employee described being told to put "sale" stickers on items that were not discounted.
  - A former FTC official said both inflating a price before a sale and advertising a fictitious list price violate the FTC Act.
  - Source: WBTV/InvestigateTV — https://www.wbtv.com/2026/07/27/how-some-retailers-inflate-original-prices-create-fake-discounts/ — Jul 27, 2026.
- **ClickOrlando (WKMG) "Dollars & Sense: Fake sales – the discount deception."** A local consumer segment with this explanation: a "50% off $200" claim only holds if $200 was a genuine, good-faith price for a reasonable period. — ClickOrlando — https://www.clickorlando.com/news/local/2026/10/30/dollars-sense-fake-sales-the-discount-deception/ — Oct 30, 2026. Only the opening was visible; the rest of the article is UNVERIFIED.

### A3. Derivative and retailer-exclusive model numbers

- **Black Friday derivative TVs.**
  - What they are: cheaper versions built for the sale that look almost identical on the store page to the reviewed model. They can have fewer HDMI ports, a lower refresh rate or a different panel, and reviews of the regular model may not apply.
  - The check: look up the full model number on the brand site and compare specs.
  - Source: Money Crashers, citing Consumer Reports' "derivative" term — https://www.moneycrashers.com/black-friday-tv-model-numbers/ — date UNVERIFIED (appears to be 2025).
- **Retailer-exclusive TVs drop features.** Manufacturers and stores offer exclusive sets that drop features such as panel quality or extra HDMI ports. — BGR — https://www.bgr.com/2037172/best-smart-tv-deals-black-friday-2025/ — Nov 2025.
- **Consumer Reports 2025 deal roundups.**
  - CR flags some sets as untested, or as models found mainly at one retailer.
  - Example: a Samsung sold at Costco under a different model number (QN77S84FD) from the Best Buy version.
  - Source: Consumer Reports — https://www.consumerreports.org/black-friday/best-black-friday-tv-deals/ — Nov 2025 (paywalled; snippet only).
- **Classic derivative test.** Search the model number. If you find only Black Friday ads and no reviews, it is probably a derivative.
  - Sources: NBC News — https://www.nbcnews.com/business/consumer/black-friday-brief-derivative-tvs-smoking-deal-or-sham-n464296 — Nov 2015 (old but still the clearest explainer). Market Realist says the same — https://marketrealist.com/consumer/are-black-friday-tvs-lower-quality — date UNVERIFIED.
- **Shark vacuums.**
  - RTINGS lists the Stratos Cordless family under several numbers (IZ840H, IZ862H, UZ865H), with IZ840H as a rose-gold colorway sold at specific retailers.
  - A Costco robot-vacuum guide says club versions are often genuinely different SKUs, with different accessories, dock capacity or color, and that the model number almost always changes.
  - Sources: RTINGS — https://www.rtings.com/vacuum/tools/compare/shark-stratos-cordless-iz862h-vs-shark-powerdetect-cordless-ip1251/34576/74265 — page seen Nov 2026. RoboVacGuide — https://robovacguide.com/costco-robot-vacuums/ — date UNVERIFIED.
  - **IZ840HRW vs IZ840H: UNVERIFIED.** No source found explaining the "RW" suffix. Do not name that pair in the article unless agent 2 confirms it.

## B. Price-history tools, their limits, and price-adjustment policies

### B1. Tools

- **Chrome Shopping Insights / price history.**
  - On supported retail pages, a "Shopping insights" chip appears in the desktop Chrome address bar. It opens a side panel with about 90 days of price history, a "typical price" range, and price-drop email tracking.
  - Limits: desktop only, US (Google's merchant help page), and based on merchant feed data.
  - Merchants can opt out of having their price data shown. Google processes the request within about 5 business days.
  - Sources: Google Merchant Center help — https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/14198594 — page seen Nov 2026. 9to5Google launch coverage — https://9to5google.com/2023/11/07/chrome-shopping-insights/ — Nov 2023. Android Authority (says it is also available in AU/CA/IN/JP, which conflicts with Google's page) — https://www.androidauthority.com/chrome-price-tracking-online-shopping-3569767 — date UNVERIFIED.
- **Google Shopping "low / typical / high" labels.**
  - The typical price is based on sellers across the web.
  - The 30-day-low label uses total price, including shipping.
  - The "price drop" badge appears only after a significant drop from a fairly stable price.
  - Google publishes no formula, and I found no independent accuracy audit.
  - Sources: Google help — https://support.google.com/faqs/answer/10675605 — page seen Nov 2026. Merchant help on sale annotations — https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/9017019 — page seen Nov 2026.
- **Google Search AI price tracking and agentic checkout (Nov 2025).**
  - You can track a specific size, color and target price, and optionally let Google buy via Google Pay when the price hits.
  - It works only with eligible US merchants such as Wayfair, Chewy, Quince and some Shopify stores, and you must confirm before purchase.
  - Sources: Google blog — https://blog.google/products/shopping/agentic-checkout-holiday-ai-shopping/ — Nov 13, 2025. TechCrunch — https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/13/google-expands-ai-shopping-with-conversational-search-agentic-checkout-and-an-ai-that-calls-stores-for-you — Nov 13, 2025.
- **Honey (PayPal) controversy.**
  - The trigger: MegaLag's Dec 2024 video alleged Honey replaced creators' affiliate cookies and did not always surface the best codes.
  - The lawsuit: LegalEagle (Devin Stone), Wendover Productions and Ali Spagnola filed a class action in N.D. Cal. on Dec 29, 2024 (some outlets say Jan 2, 2025).
  - PayPal's response: Honey follows industry last-click attribution.
  - Later developments: Awin suspended Honey; Rakuten later reinstated it after adding protections. An Aug 2026 MegaLag follow-up and analysis report the class action survived a motion to dismiss and that Honey lost more than 7M Chrome users.
  - Sources: TechCrunch — https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/05/youtuber-legaleagle-sues-paypal-over-sleeping-leech-honey-extension — Jan 2025. Tubefilter — https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/12/30/legaleagle-honey-lawsuit-wendover-productions-ali-spagnola/ — Dec 30, 2024. Dexerto — https://www.dexerto.com/youtube/honey-loses-over-7-million-users-and-7000-stores-following-youtubers-viral-expose-3397619/ — Aug 2026. Akita analysis — https://akitaonrails.com/en/2026/08/12/digital-david-and-goliath-understanding-megalag-vs-honey-paypal/ — Aug 12, 2026.
  - The motion-to-dismiss outcome is from secondary sources only: UNVERIFIED against the court docket.
- **Capital One Shopping.**
  - The lawsuit: creators sued in Jan 2025 alleging the extension stripped their affiliate cookies. A court let the suit proceed. Capital One agreed to pay about $4M in Dec 2025, and the court granted preliminary approval in Jan 2026.
  - User complaints (Firefox add-on reviews, Trustpilot summaries): inaccurate or stale price comparisons, coupons that fail, and rewards tracking problems.
  - Sources: Tubefilter — https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/01/24/capital-one-shopping-honey-lawsuits-creator-affiliate-marketing/ — Jan 24, 2025. American Banker — https://www.americanbanker.com/news/capital-one-settles-lawsuit-over-influencers-commissions — Dec 2025. ### B2. Price-adjustment and price-match policies

- **Target.**
  - Target ended competitor price matching (Amazon, Walmart and others) on Jul 28, 2025, after 12 years. Matches now cover Target's own prices only.
  - The 2026 Holiday Price Match Guarantee covers purchases from Nov 1 that drop in price at Target on or before Dec 24, 2026. The standard window outside the holidays is 14 days.
  - Sources: CBS News — https://www.cbsnews.com/news/target-price-matching-policy-july-2025 — Jul 2025. Axios — https://www.axios.com/2025/07/27/target-price-matching-policy-change-amazon-walmart — Jul 27, 2025. Target fact sheet — https://corporate.target.com/press/fact-sheet/2026/11/holiday-shopping-information — Nov 2026. 2025 version — https://corporate.target.com/press/fact-sheet/2025/10/holiday-savings — Oct 30, 2025.
  - The exclusion of Doorbusters and Lightning Deals comes from one DealNews snippet: UNVERIFIED.
- **Best Buy.**
  - It does not match competitors during the Black Friday stretch, and items marked "Black Friday pricing" are excluded.
  - It does adjust for its own later price drops within the return window: 15 days standard, 30 days for My Best Buy Plus/Total members.
  - The 2026 blackout dates conflict across sources (one says Nov 19–30): UNVERIFIED.
  - Sources: Best Buy price match page — https://www.bestbuy.com/pmg — page seen Nov 2026. Business Wire 2025 holiday release — https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251020811034/en/ — Oct 20, 2025.
- **Costco.**
  - Costco gives a 30-day price adjustment if its own price drops.
  - It often fails for Black Friday because the deal items carry different item numbers that are not buyable beforehand. RetailMeNot's editor put it this way: "You can't buy something early and ask for a price adjustment later, if you can't buy it early in the first place."
  - Reddit anecdote: a TV bought the week before Black Friday was refunded automatically as a Shop Card.
  - Sources: GOBankingRates — https://www.gobankingrates.com/?p=1978227 — 2023. AOL/Cheapism on the automatic refund — https://www.aol.com/finance/costco-member-shocked-money-back-130000962.html — date UNVERIFIED.
  - Current 2026 Costco page not checked: UNVERIFIED.
- **Walmart.**
  - Walmart matches competitor prices only at checkout (where offered) and does not refund later price drops; the workaround is return and rebuy.
  - Some blogs still claim a 7-day markdown window. That is UNVERIFIED and conflicting; treat Walmart as "no guaranteed post-purchase adjustment."
  - Sources: DealNews — https://qa1.dealnews.com/features/walmart/price-match — 2025. Market Realist on TikTok anecdotes — https://marketrealist.com/media-and-entertainment/walmart-price-adjustment-policy-tiktok — date UNVERIFIED.
