Black Friday 2026 actuals are not published yet (Adobe pages 503; no SpendingPulse/Numerator/Circana BF data). Figures are Thanksgiving 2026 actuals, Nov 1-22 to date, and forecasts, labeled.

## Headline numbers
- Thanksgiving (Nov 26, 2026) online spend $7.1B, +10.4% YoY, above Adobe's $6.9B forecast (Adobe via Reuters; Seeking Alpha Nov 27 2026 https://seekingalpha.com/news/4677049-black-friday-came-early-for-retailers-as-thanksgiving-delivered-record-71b-in-sales ; Epoch Times Nov 27 2026).
- Nov 1-22 online spend $82.5B, +9.1% (Adobe, Epoch Times).
- Thanksgiving gaming vs October daily average (Adobe): consoles +1,058%, video games +817%, accessories +595% (USA Today via Yahoo, Nov 27 2026 https://shopping.yahoo.com/deals/articles/black-friday-tips-finding-deals-184535479.html).
- Mobile share 63.2%, AI-referred traffic +162% (secondary sources only, UNVERIFIED).
- Adobe forecast, Sept 28 2026 (https://news.adobe.com/news/2026/09/adobe-us-holiday-shopping-season-to-hit-record): Black Friday $12.9B (+9.2%), Cyber Monday $15.1B (+6.2%), Cyber Week $47.5B, season $275.1B; peak discounts electronics up to 30%, toys 29%, TVs 23%, computers 23%, apparel 23%, sporting goods 19%, appliances 18%, furniture 18%; discounts up to 14% early Nov, 21% just before Thanksgiving, 30% at Cyber Week peak.
- Salesforce: Thanksgiving US online about $8.8B; BF forecast $18.7B US (+4%) (partly unverified, paywalled).
- NRF Nov 19 2026: 182.7M expected to shop Thanksgiving weekend, 126M on Black Friday (https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/183-million-consumers-expected-to-shop-during-thanksgiving-weekend).
