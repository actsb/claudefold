## 5. Warranty, registration, returns

- 1-year limited warranty, repair or replace at the company's discretion; replacement unit gets 12 months from receipt; applies only to purchases from authorized retailers; proof of purchase required. Source: https://instantpot.com/pages/product-warranties and PDF https://images.thdstatic.com/catalog/pdfImages/5c/5c1071b9-ca42-4fca-8788-c7c388d7211e.pdf (snippets, Oct 2026; text not opened).
- Registration: within 30 days at instantpot.com/support/register to "validate" warranty, but not registering does not reduce warranty rights. Same source.
- Customer Care: 1-800-828-7280, support@instantpot.com (snippet of the warranty page).
- Instant Pot store return policy: 30 days from receipt, unused, original packaging, RMA via email. Source: https://store.instantpot.com/pages/return-policies (snippet; UNVERIFIED current). Retailer return windows (Walmart, Target, Costco, Best Buy, Home Depot, Kohl's): NOT RESEARCHED/UNVERIFIED; check each store's policy.
- Third-party sellers (including marketplaces) that are not authorized resellers are not covered by the warranty (same warranty page). Source for general claim: https://instantpot.com/pages/product-warranties.

## 8. Instant Brands bankruptcy and ownership

- Instant Brands filed Chapter 11 on June 12, 2023; appliance business sold to Centre Lane Partners (Nov 2023); emerged as stand-alone Instant Pot Brands in March 2024; housewares (Corelle, Pyrex) emerged separately Feb 27, 2024. Sources: https://www.cnn.com/2023/06/13/business/instant-brands-bankruptcy/index.html , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instant_Brands , https://elevenflo.com/blog/instant-brands-instant-pot-sale-corelle-emergence (search snippets).
