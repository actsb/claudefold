## 3. Official manual (read in full)
- Instant Pot Duo Multi-Cooker User Manual, v5 (covers Duo Mini v5 3 qt, Duo 60 v5 6 qt, Duo 80 v5 8 qt); footer date 08/12/2025. File: https://myturn-prod-attachments.s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/6/516/item/1275217/file_attachment/113-0059-01_110-0043-01_112-0170-01_IB_DuoV5_MANUAL_399-1235-03US_US_R40_FINAL-08-12-2025_LowRes-36654DCB-D944-7794-010B-690E4A43F9C0.pdf (a third-party library S3 copy, not instanthome.com; prefer to link instant brand's own manual page, e.g. https://instantpot.com/products/instant-pot-duo-6qt-multi-use-pressure-cooker per search result, and check it hosts the same manual).
- Another v5 manual (2023-08-15): https://files.plytix.com/api/v1.1/file/public_files/pim/assets/27/1d/e1/63/63e11d272c0736000157d3a9/texts/ca/e8/ae/65/65aee8ca6bdc3a174a8935b1/113-0059-01_IB_DuoV5_MANUAL_US_FINAL-08-15-2023_LoRes.pdf (seen in results only).
- Specs (v5 table): Duo 60 v5, 6 qt / 5.7 L, 1000 W, 120 V.
- Test run (v5): use pressure cooking steps with the Steam Smart Program; add water, no food, as in "Using liquid to create steam"; set time 5 minutes; after cooking discard water; wash and dry pot.  NOTE: this is NOT the older "3 cups, 2 minute" water test cited in blogs/videos; mention that older videos differ.
- Minimum liquid (v5 table): 3 qt 1 cup (250 mL); 6 qt 1 1/2 cups (12 oz / 375 mL); 8 qt 2 cups (500 mL), "unless otherwise specified in recipe". Liquid should be water-based (broth, stock, juice). So "1 cup" is WRONG for the 6-qt in the v5 manual. A search-result summary claiming 18 fl oz/500 mL minimum appears to come from a different (Duo Plus / older) manual: UNVERIFIED, do not use. Recommend stating 1.5 cups for the current Duo 6-qt and "check your model's manual".
- Fill lines (v5 safeguards): do not fill above the line labeled 'PC MAX' (or 'Max PC Fill'); not above '1/2' line for foods that expand (rice, dried beans, dried vegetables) or foam/froth (applesauce, cranberries, pearl barley, oatmeal/cereals, split peas, noodles, macaroni, rhubarb, spaghetti). (The "2/3" figure is not in the text I extracted; do not state it unless seen on the pot.)
- Sealing ring: must be installed before use; clean after each use; install one ring at a time (one sweet, one savory); replace every 12 to 18 months or sooner if stretched/deformed/damaged; place over sealing ring rack and press firmly so there is no puckering; should be snug and not fall out when lid is turned over. Use only authorized replacement ring.
- Lid: line up arrow on lid with unlock symbol on base, turn clockwise to lock symbol. Preheat up to 30 minutes to reach pressure (frozen food longer). Keep Warm is ON by default.
- Natural steam release: leave switch in Seal until all pressure releases ("up to 40 minutes or more"); float valve drops; then turn lid counterclockwise. Manual says use for foods that can simmer like stews, chili, pasta, oatmeal, beans, grains.
- Quick steam release: set switch to Vent and wait for all pressure to release; float valve drops; then open. Manual says use for foods that overcook, like vegetables and seafood.
- Manual elsewhere: beans/legumes use Natural Release; 10-minute timed release for fluffy rice; use Quick Release to avoid overcooking.
- Safety warning: allow all pressure to release before opening lid; keep hands/face away from steam release.
- Cleaning tip: to remove sealing-ring odor, add 1 cup (see manual page on Cleaning; line ~1088 of extracted text) - exact recipe not read in full.

## 4. Model comparison facts (what is supported)
- Duo vs Duo Plus: GH Institute (via syndication, UNVERIFIED) says Duo Plus adds cake/egg settings and a bigger, brighter display. Duo Plus 6-qt manual (Williams-Sonoma): https://www.williams-sonoma.com/netstorage/pdf/Instant-Pot-6Qt-Next-Gen-DUO-PLUS-V4-112-0169-01-User-Manual.pdf (not read).

- Duo Crisp: ATK snippet notes C7 error on stew until more liquid added. It is a 6.5-qt air-fryer lid combo; Duo Crisp requires the detachable air-fry lid (Digital Trends/TechRadar snippets).
- Ninja Foodi / Cosori: ATK Best Buy is Cosori 6-qt; CR rates multiple Ninja Foodi models; Duo browned better than Cosori in SmartPickers blog test.
- Wirecutter: Rio 6-qt is top pick; Duo was pick for seven years; Duo keeps low-pressure option.
- Size: no expert lab size guidance seen. Blog snippets (Paint the Kitchen Red, summeryule.com, etc.) say 6 qt for about 4-6 people, 8 qt for 6-8+; 6 qt fits a 5-6 lb chicken. These are blogs: label as rule of thumb.
