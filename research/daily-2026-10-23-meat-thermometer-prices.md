## 4. Warranty, calibration, returns

- **Thermapen ONE:** 5-year warranty (thermoworks.com info sheet https://www.thermoworks.com:443/content/pdf/thermapen-one-info-sheet.pdf; case warrantied 5 years). NIST-traceable calibration certificate included. Adjustable if needed.
- **Thermapen NANO:** 5-year warranty (search summary, UNVERIFIED on page).
- **ThermoPop 2: 2-year warranty, not 3** (ThermoWorks instant-read category page and support page https://help.thermoworks.com/?p=6763). NIST-traceable certificate included. Accuracy +/-1 F.
- **SIZZLE:** 2-year warranty (search summary; certificate UNVERIFIED).
- **Returns (ThermoWorks):** returns page https://thermoworks.com/pages/returns-rma. Search summary: only direct purchases eligible, 30-day window for most unused products, restocking fee if no RMA number or if item damaged/missing parts. Slickdeals text says 30-day refund less shipping, box/certificate must be intact. Exchange vs refund wording is UNVERIFIED (page body didn't load).
- Phone: 800-393-6434.
- **Authorized-dealer rule:** since Jan 1, 2016 warranty and tech support apply only to units bought from ThermoWorks or authorized dealers; Amazon, eBay, walmart.com, sears.com and their sellers named as NOT authorized (forum repost of ThermoWorks notice, https://tvwbb.com/threads/warning-from-thermoworks-about-unauthorized-product-sales.64245/latest). Later ThermoWorks began selling directly on Amazon (https://www.thermoworks.com/blogs/learning-center/why-thermoworks-is-now-selling-on-amazon, body not retrievable, so current dealer terms UNVERIFIED). Verify the current rule before telling readers a given retailer is covered.
- **ThermoPro/TempPro TP19H:** 1-year limited warranty, extended to 3 years on registration (text from product-listing warranty copy; confirm on the brand site). Newegg directs buyers to the seller for warranty. A forum user said the company is strict on warranty dates and receipts (https://tvwbb.com/goto/post?id=961681). ThermoPro certificate: none found (UNVERIFIED). TP19H has calibration mode.

## 6. Knockoff warnings

- ThermoWorks FAQ warns of "counterfeit Thermapen look-a-likes" and says only a few authorized dealers exist. https://help.thermoworks.com/?p=4246
- ThermoWorks has said it received complaints about third-party sellers passing off counterfeit, copycat, stolen and "open-box" units as new, and that Google ads for "Thermapen" led to copycat products (bait and switch). https://pitmaster.amazingribs.com/forum/accessorize-your-pit/digital-thermometers/355982-thermoworks-predatory-practice-warning
- Temu and AliExpress specifically: I found no source documenting fake Thermapens there. UNVERIFIED. The generic risk of look-alikes on low-cost marketplaces is supported only by non-thermometer articles. Keep the claim to "look-alikes exist; ThermoWorks warns about marketplace sellers" rather than naming platforms.
- Practical tell: genuine Thermapen warranty only via ThermoWorks or authorized dealers; third-party price far above or below list is a red flag. The ONE is made in Worthing, UK (Slickdeals commenter, UNVERIFIED).
