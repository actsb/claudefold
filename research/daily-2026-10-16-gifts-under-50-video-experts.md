### 3. Bedsure heated throw
- Expert Reviews (UK), Jo Plumridge, Feb 24, 2026. https://www.expertreviews.co.uk/sleep/bedsure-electric-blanket-heated-throw-review . Gets very warm, very fast, soft, reasonably priced. Con: thin material, not luxurious. Machine washable at 30C; cannot be tumble dried or ironed.
- Safety, UL: "Electric blankets are cozy. Are they safe?" https://www.ul.com/insights/electric-blankets-are-cozy-are-they-safe . No author/date shown. Quotes Derek E. Bell, MD (Kessler Burn Center). Advice: pre-warm then switch off before getting in; inspect for worn spots, loose plugs, cracked wiring, charred spots and discard if found; do not fold, cover with bedding, or combine with a heating pad; avoid sofa beds and adjustable beds; unplug when done and store rolled; buy UL-tested new, not secondhand; check CPSC recalls. Not recommended for infants/young children, or people who can't safely operate them or who are paralyzed; diabetics more vulnerable.
- CPSC product safety warning 26-616, July 16, 2026, Melinora heated blankets (sold on Amazon Feb-Oct 2025; wires can overheat and melt fabric; stop using and dispose). https://www.cpsc.gov/Warnings/2026/CPSC-Warns-Consumers-to-Stop-Using-Melinora-Electric-Heated-Blankets-Immediately-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-from-Burn-and-Fire-Hazards . Not Bedsure; useful as "buy a certified brand, check recalls".
- CPSC Home Electrical Safety Checklist (PDF unreadable by fetch; content only from search summary: check blankets for cracked wiring, charred or frayed spots; nothing on top; don't tuck or pinch wires). https://www.cpsc.gov/s3fs-public/513.pdf . Treat as UNVERIFIED until opened by hand.
- Bedsure official manual: NOT found. Care label on the product governs. Listings conflict on auto shut-off (8h vs 3h) and washing. Bedsure's "74 safety tests / beyond UL" is a manufacturer claim only. GH Seal: UNVERIFIED (see top).

### 9. ThermoWorks ThermoPop 2
- America's Test Kitchen, Hannah Crowley, Valerie Sizhe Li, Miye Bromberg, Jun 24, 2025. https://www.americastestkitchen.com/equipment_reviews/1891-digital-instant-read-thermometers . Best Buy: as accurate as predecessor, a bit faster, large auto-rotating display, better waterproofing. Avg read 2.6 s; error 0.2F at 32F and 0.3F at 125F; 4.5 in probe; waterproof to 39 in for 30 min. Cons: handle could be a bit larger, probe doesn't fold, backlight is manual. Thermapen ONE is overall winner.
- ThermoWorks blog "Check Thermometers", Tim, Nov 2, 2023. https://blog.thermoworks.com/check-thermometers/ . Ice-bath check: fill vessel with ice, add water to ~1/2 in below the ice, stir probe in the center; ThermoPop spec is +/-2F, so 30-34F in a good ice bath is in spec and should not be adjusted. Digital units should not need calibration, but periodic checks are fine. NOTE: this blog speaks of the ThermoPop generally. The ThermoPop 2 manual lists +/-1.0F (14-208F), so confirm which spec to quote.
- Operating instructions (ThermoPop 2, 8 in): https://thermoworks.com/content/pdf/thermopop2-operating-instructions-8-inch.pdf . Direct fetch returned 404, so the following is from a search snippet only (UNVERIFIED): spec +/-1.0F (-10 to 98C) range; 2-year warranty; support (801) 756-7705 / techsupport@thermoworks.com. NO user calibration procedure found; do not tell readers to "recalibrate" the ThermoPop 2.
