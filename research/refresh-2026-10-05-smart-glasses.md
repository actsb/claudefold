### Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3 details (reported)
- 9 h battery (Gen 2: 8 h); case adds ~50 h. 12 MP camera, 3K video unchanged. Six-mic array
  (Meta claims >90% background-noise reduction), new action/AI button, slimmer frame, adjustable temple tips.
  Sources: 9to5Google 2026-09-23 (https://9to5google.com/2026/09/23/meta-ray-ban-gen-3-audio-only-smart-glasses/),
  Engadget hands-on (https://www.engadget.com/2267213/ray-ban-meta-gen-3-hands-on-better-battery-life/),
  Yahoo Tech hands-on (https://tech.yahoo.com/ar-vr/articles/hands-ray-ban-meta-gen-000000660.html).
- 27 frame/lens combos across Wayfarer, Aviator and cat-eye "Zena". (9to5Google; Engadget)
- UK price from £409 (lensology.co.uk summary) — UNVERIFIED.
- Chip: pre-Connect leaks said Snapdragon AR1+; Meta's launch materials do not name it — UNVERIFIED.
- "Live AI for hours" (the leak our September guide repeated): NOT confirmed in launch coverage — UNVERIFIED.
  Our guide had said "faster chip, Live AI in hours, two models"; actual Gen 3 = modest refresh, one Gen 3
  line plus the separate camera-free Audio model.
