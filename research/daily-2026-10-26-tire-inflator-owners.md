## Cross-brand themes (what owners actually say)
1. Battery-platform advice dominates: "buy the one that uses batteries you already own." MyGrandRV #23/#24 (Nov 3-4, 2025) https://www.mygrandrv.com/threads/best-portable-battery-air-compressors-for-rv-tire-inflation.1450972/latest [FETCHED]
2. Cordless inflators are top-off tools, not air-up/air-down tools. TacomaWorld, rustyfromskowhegan and Technique, Aug 18, 2020: DeWalt "not quite compressor status", suggests Viair/ARB for frequent airing down; Technique says a friend's DeWalt failed after use on 33" and 265/70R17 tires. https://www.tacomaworld.com/posts/24003732 [FETCHED]
3. Duty cycle / overheating is the #1 failure complaint for 18V/20V drill-style units (Ryobi especially), and for small mini units battery runs out after 2-3 tires.
4. Gauge drift/inaccuracy reported on Ryobi; Fanttik praised for auto-stop accuracy by a few owners (see below); AstroAI gauge complaints are only from non-owner/aggregated sources (UNVERIFIED).
5. Mini "pocket" units (AstroAI L7, Fanttik X8/S-series) are praised for bikes, motorcycles, car top-offs; criticized as slow for car/truck fills.

## Questions people ask (and best evidence)
- Truck tire 35 to 65 psi? No owner timing found for exactly this. Related: Forest River forum (Aug/Sep 2025?) 12V compressor ~1 hr for four truck tires 25 to 65 psi [SNIPPET, UNVERIFIED]. M18 owners: 60-80 psi trailer/truck fills fine (spankis, Mitch 350G). Mini units (AstroAI L7, Fanttik X8) "run low after 2-3 truck tires" (andymand, Meh, Jul 29, 2025). Ryobi: needs 4Ah+, cool-down breaks.
- Bike tires: small-volume pump works on L7/Fanttik (haydesigner, Tumbleweed7200); M18 "fails miserably" on small-volume presets (PEMP); Presta adapters are fiddly (NullPointer); drill-style units often lack Presta adapter (EBR 2019). Road 80 psi/ tubeless seating on cordless pumps: UNVERIFIED, no owner evidence.
- Battery: 2Ah too small for anything beyond top-offs (Ryobi 2Ah ~20-30 psi total, UNVERIFIED; M12 2.0Ah 1 flat or ~4 top-ups, UNVERIFIED).
- Time from 0: only anecdote: Ryobi P737 ~7-8 min to 36 psi on a car tire (eBay UK, Mar 27, 2017). Seller claims (AstroAI "0 to 36 in 5 min") not owner-verified.

## Recommended roundup picks (my judgment, based on above)
1. Milwaukee M18 2848-20 - best for truck/trailer; strong owner consensus; cost: big, loud, bare tool, battery hungry.
2. DeWalt DCC020IB - best all-rounder for bikes + cars; 12V/AC fallback; 4-yr owner; heavier, pricey.
3. Ryobi P737D/P747 - budget, huge install base; but include duty-cycle/gauge-drift warning with TomPanning/Slickdeals quotes. Confirm current model.
4. Fanttik X8 Apex or S-series (S200/S300) - best "glovebox" pick; owners praise accuracy but battery fades and mini-battery limits truck use.
5. AstroAI L7 - cheap bike/motorcycle/topping-off pick; be explicit it is slow for cars; L8 has no owner evidence, so don't recommend L8 on evidence.
6. Milwaukee M12 2475-20 - compact option for Milwaukee users (evidence thin, mostly UNVERIFIED snippets).
Leave out/label: EPAuto (corded), Black+Decker (thin, mixed), Viair (corded; mention as "if you air down often").
