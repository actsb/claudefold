## 2. Expert / safety sources (claims supported by page or snippet)
**Cost data**
- EIA Electric Power Monthly Table 5.6.A (fetched): US residential average 18.31 cents/kWh for July 2026 (preliminary), vs 17.45 in July 2025; released Sept 24, 2026. URL: https://www.eia.gov/electricity/monthly/epm_table_grapher.php?t=epmt_5_6_a
  - Math: 1.5 kW x $0.1831 = about $0.275/hour at full power (about 27.5 cents). 8 h/day at full power = about $2.20/day. Thermostat cycling lowers real use. (Newsweek citing EIA: 18.83 c/kWh for April 2026, snippet only.)
- Tom's Guide Dreo WH517S review (via search summary): assumed 16 c/kWh; eco mode 5% duty = est. $97.80/yr (figure as relayed; unverified in full text). Tom's Pelonis review: about 1,330 W measured on high (snippet).

**Safety bodies**
- CPSC news release, Jan 23, 2026 (fetched): keep heaters at least 3 ft from drapes, furniture, bedding, combustibles; "Plug electric space heaters directly into a wall outlet - never into an extension cord or power strip"; turn off when sleeping; portable heaters involved in avg 1,600 fires/yr, about 70 deaths, 150 injuries/yr (2020-2022 avg). https://www.cpsc.gov/Newsroom/News-Releases/2026/Keep-Warm-and-Safe-This-Winter-Tips-for-Using-Generators-Furnaces-and-Space-Heaters
- CPSC 2012 checklist (snippet): ordinary home extension cord probably inadequate; if unavoidable use heavy-duty cord rated for wattage. https://www.cpsc.gov/Newsroom/News-Releases/2012/CPSC-Issues-Warnings-On-Use-Of-Space-Heaters-Offers-Eight-Point-Safety-Checklist
- NFPA (search snippet; tip-sheet page fetch returned no text; undated): 3-ft clearance; use heaters listed by a nationally recognized lab; automatic shut-off if tipped; plug directly into wall, no extension cord/power strip; do not share outlet with other devices; turn off when leaving/sleeping; space heaters = one-third of heating fires but about 9 in 10 deaths. https://www.nfpa.org/downloadable-resources/safety-tip-sheets/electric-portable-space-heater-safety and https://www.nfpa.org/education-and-research/home-fire-safety/heating
- UL (search snippet, ul.com): 3 ft from combustibles, supplement not replacement for home heating, look for UL Mark, avoid extension cords, flat surface. https://www.ul.com/news/turning-down-danger-home-heating-hot-spots

Recommend "small to medium room, about 150 sq ft" wording.

## 3. Dreo official documentation (support.dreo.com, DR-HSH004 / Atom One / Ceramic Heater 314 page, fetched)
- US manuals listed: DR-HSH004 US (2024-09-30), HSH004A, HSH004B. Other models: DR-HSH006 (Atom 316, 2024-02-05), DR-HSH020 (Heater 320, 2025-06-27). Direct PDF downloaded but has no text layer, so manual body not read. URL: https://support.dreo.com/en/support/product/atom-one/standard
- ECO mode: heater stops heating once set temp reached, restarts when room cools; oscillation stops in that state. H3 mode referenced for restoring oscillation.
- Overheat: above 50 C / 122 F ambient, heating stops and shows "FF"; unplug, restart when cooler. Surface normally below 55 C.
- Advises against extension cords; tripping may indicate overloaded circuit.
- Cleaning: unplug and cool; wipe outside with soft cloth; no cleaning agents/detergents; keep water from motor, element, switches; clean at least once per season and before storage; dust at air inlet with dry cloth/swab.
- Remote battery: replace only with same/equivalent type. New unit may have slight odor.
- Warranty: support page links to warranty activation (support.dreo.com/en/warranty) and policy (https://www.dreo.com/pages/warranty-terms-conditions - fetch 429, not read). Manuals reportedly say register at dreo.com/warranty to extend (snippet).
- Support contact in manuals (via snippet): support@dreo.com, (888) 290-1688.
