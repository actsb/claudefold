## 1. Instant-read thermometer
Models: ThermoWorks Thermapen ONE (and Classic/MK4); budget option ThermoPro TP03/TP19.
Owner signals (paraphrased from search summaries, dates UNVERIFIED):
- Praise, Thermapen ONE vs Classic: owners with both say ONE is faster (about 1s vs 2-3s) and the backlight is worth it; one owner said the speed gain is not huge. Source: https://tvwbb.com/threads/thermapen-classic-vs-one.92910/latest (date UNVERIFIED; fetch blocked)
- Praise, durability: one owner used an original Thermapen daily for years, only recently replacing batteries (same tvwbb thread, UNVERIFIED).
- Advice to wait for a sale, especially Black Friday (same thread, UNVERIFIED).
- Complaints, failure modes: forum posters report wild or flickering readings (one cited 675 F) at roughly one year, intermittent operation, and loss of trust after several years. ThermoWorks support reportedly blamed a circuit-board short. Sources: https://tvwbb.com/threads/thermapen-problem.27078/latest ; https://tvwbb.com/threads/thermapen-trust-issues-wild-temperature-fluctuations.61520/post-681169 (dates UNVERIFIED)
- Warranty: 5-year, per one search summary; support described as responsive but variable. Confirm on thermoworks.com.
- A reviewer said a faulty screen appeared after a year on a ONE. Source: https://www.foodfirefriends.com/thermoworks-thermapen-one-review/ (a blog review, not an owner forum; date UNVERIFIED)
- Battery: ThermoWorks claims 2,000 hours on one AAA. Not owner-confirmed.
- ThermoPro TP03: Walmart listing shows about 4.6-4.8 stars on 118-289 reviews for variants, per search snippet. Source: https://www.walmart.com/ip/seort/1060364978 (snippet only; date UNVERIFIED).
Who should skip the Thermapen: occasional hosts who only need a turkey-temp check. A TP03-class unit is likelier enough, but owner evidence is thin (UNVERIFIED).
Variants that matter: ONE vs Classic (speed, backlight, price). Colour is cosmetic.

## 2. Roasting pan with rack
Models: Cuisinart Chef's Classic stainless 16" roaster with rack; Calphalon Tri-Ply; All-Clad (premium).
- Cuisinart: retailer-page review snippets praise heavy stainless, sturdy handles, a removable rack that cleans easily, and dishwasher safety. A listing showed 4.7 stars on 138 reviews. Sources: https://www.kohls.com/product/prd-2872457/cuisinart-chefs-classic-stainless-steel-16-in-roasting-pan-with-rack.jsp and the Macy's listing https://www.macys.com/shop/product/cuisinart-chefs-classic-stainless-steel-16-roaster-with-roasting-rack?ID=506620 (which listing the figures came from, and dates, UNVERIFIED).
- Warping: a Cook's Illustrated summary says it dropped All-Clad for warping on the stovetop and now picks Calphalon Tri-Ply. This is an editorial test, not owner evidence. Source: https://www.americastestkitchen.com/equipment_reviews/1447
- No owner long-term failure data found. UNVERIFIED.
Who should skip: anyone cooking a turkey over about 20 lb in a small oven (check pan fit), and anyone who only roasts once a year (a disposable pan may do).
Alternative: Nesco 18 qt roaster oven (22 lb turkey capacity per the brand). Walmart listings show about 4.3-4.5 stars (7-115 reviews) and Wayfair about 4.7 (131 reviews) per snippets; no review text read. Source: https://www.walmart.com/ip/300261962 (UNVERIFIED).

## 3. Electric carving knife
Models: Cuisinart CEK-40; Hamilton Beach; Black+Decker.
- Cuisinart CEK-40: Macy's listing reportedly 4.6 stars on 364 reviews. Reviewers praise power, tidy storage tray and slicing ham and turkey. Complaints: handle breakage, a safety switch that fails over time, a flimsy case, and noise. Source: https://www.macys.com/shop/product/cuisinart-cek-40-electric-knife?ID=62871 (snippet; date UNVERIFIED). Also https://www.zappos.com/product/review/7735187.
- Black+Decker: an editorial source says the motor is weak and may last only months to a year with use. That is not owner evidence. Source: https://thekitchenprofessor.com/blog/best-electric-knife/
- Hamilton Beach: described as inexpensive and good on turkey and roasts, but poor on crusty bread (editorial).
Who should skip: anyone comfortable with a sharp 10-12" slicer; buy only if you carve for a crowd or have hand-strength limits.

## 4. Fat separator
Models: OXO Good Grips Good Gravy 4-cup; Cuisipro 4-cup.
- OXO: Cook's Illustrated/ATK testing favours it for a controllable trigger and low splash. Source: https://www.americastestkitchen.com/equipment_reviews/1725 (editorial). OXO's own store review page was blocked, so no owner reviews were read.
- Cuisipro: slow to drain but a better yield (editorial).
- Owner complaints, leaks and hand-wash advice: none found. UNVERIFIED.
Who should skip: people who make small gravy batches (a spoon or a freezer chill works). Size: 4-cup suits a full turkey's drippings; a 2-cup OXO variant exists (UNVERIFIED).

## 5. Slow cooker or warming tray for sides
Models: Elite Gourmet triple buffet server (EWMST-325R slow-cooker type; EWM-6171 warmer); Nostalgia BCD-992 3-station; Hamilton Beach 6 qt programmable (clip-tight lid); Crock-Pot 7 qt Cook & Carry.
- One search summary says a user kept all 3 Elite Gourmet dishes very hot for 5 hours on Thanksgiving. Source: Walmart listing https://www.walmart.com/ip/seort/21554256 (snippet; date UNVERIFIED).
- Hamilton Beach 6 qt: Walmart/Target listings show 4.2 (63 reviews) and 4.7 (46 ratings). Source: https://www.target.com/p/hamilton-beach-6qt-programmable-cooker-silver/-/A-79318254 (UNVERIFIED).
- Crock-Pot 7 qt at Target: 4 stars on 749 ratings per snippet (UNVERIFIED).
- Warming trays: 2.5 qt pots are small for family-size sides; I have no failure-mode data.
Who should skip: hosts with spare oven space and 8 or fewer guests.

## 6. Leftovers containers
Models: Rubbermaid Brilliance (glass and plastic sets).
- Target-listing snippets: airtight and stain/odor resistant. Complaints: lids cracking, no replacement lids sold, and glass cracking in the microwave. Source: https://target.com/p/rubbermaid-brilliance-18pc-glass-food-storage-container-set/-/A-91597493 (summary only; date UNVERIFIED).
- Glasslock, Pyrex: no evidence gathered.
Who should skip: anyone who reheats in the microwave from the fridge without checking the lid vent; consider disposable or zip bags for giving guests food.

## 7. Folding chairs
Models: Flash Furniture Hercules plastic (650 lb and 800 lb capacity); Cosco vinyl-padded steel (250 lb, from an editorial roundup).
- Walmart listings, per snippets: 800 lb white 4.7 stars (249 reviews); 650 lb black 4.6 (938); 650 lb grey 4.9 (340). Reviewers say sturdy, quiet on floors, comfortable for larger bodies, flat to store. Sources: https://www.walmart.com/ip/20-Pack-Flash-Furniture-HERCULES-Series-800-lb-Capacity-Premium-Plastic-Folding-Chair-Beige/139017450 and https://www.walmart.com/reviews/product/48803953 (counts not verified on the page; dates UNVERIFIED).
- Capacity variant matters: the fabric-and-metal Hercules is 300 lb, the resin ones 650 or 800 lb.
- Complaints: none gathered. UNVERIFIED.
Who should skip: hosts with fewer than 3 extra guests; renting or using ottomans may do.
