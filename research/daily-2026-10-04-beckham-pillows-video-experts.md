### Washing and fluffing (general)
- A CNN Underscored explainer on washing pillows exists: https://www.cnn.com/cnn-underscored/home/how-to-wash-pillows. Content is UNVERIFIED.
- Healthline, WebMD and Clorox have wash guides: healthline.com/health/washing-pillows, webmd.com/a-to-z-guides/how-to-clean-your-pillows, clorox.com/learn/how-to-wash-and-dry-pillows/. Not read.
- No university-extension page on pillow care was found.
- General advice from the snippets:
  - Wash two pillows at once to balance the load.
  - Use a small amount of detergent and add an extra rinse.
  - Dry with tennis balls or dryer balls.
  - Hand-fluff clumps after drying.
  - Washing temporarily restores loft, but the fibers do not fully recover after many cycles.
  - Use a pillow protector.
