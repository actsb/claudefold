# Dreo 1500W PTC heater - owner-experience brief (2026-10-15)

Scope: owners angle for Dreo (tower/HSH-series, Solaris) vs Vornado VH200 vs Dyson Hot+Cool. No Amazon ratings, reviews, Q&A or review digests were used. About 25 searches/fetches were run (not 50). Many pages blocked (403/429/503). Reddit returned no results through search at all. Most "owner" evidence here is thin. Every claim has a URL; a date is given where the source showed one, otherwise "date not shown".

## 1. Safety and recalls
- No Dreo recall found. CPSC recall list page, first page only (20 items, 10 Sep - 8 Oct 2026), had no Dreo entry; the full database was not searched. https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls?search_combined_fields=Dreo (fetched 2026-10-15). A general "Dreo heater recall CPSC" web search also surfaced no Dreo item (2026-10-15).
- Dyson: CPSC recall of 1 Apr 2014 covered Dyson Hot (AM04) and Hot+Cool (AM04, AM05) for an electrical short/overheat fire hazard. https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2014/Dyson-Recalls-Bladeless-Portable-Electric-Heaters . Search found no notice naming the HP-series (HP07 etc.), but absence was not proven.
- Vornado: CPSC has a 2004 portable-heater recall (search result title only; models not checked, so do not tie it to the VH200). https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2004/cpsc-vornado-air-circulation-systems-announce-recall-of-portable-electric-heaters
- Dreo manuals: PP code on the HSH009 wall heater is overheat protection; manual says check the outlet is not shared/loose and no extension cord. Tripping is attributed to the power exceeding the home circuit load. https://manualsfile.com/product/k40is7jlj9o.html (date not shown)
- Dreo Solaris Max FAQ: ambient above 50C/122F gives "FF" overheat alarm; unplug and restart after cooling. https://www.dreo.com/products/solaris-max-2nd-gen-space-heater (via search snippet, date not shown; page fetch was rate-limited)
- General (not Dreo-specific): electrician/iFixit threads say melted plugs usually trace to loose receptacles or extension cords, and a breaker may not trip first. https://zh.ifixit.com/Answers/View/588972/Heater+Cable+plug+melted ; https://www.technicalsafetybc.ca/regulatory-resources/incident-investigations/poor-connection-between-heater-plug-and-receptacle-sparks-electrical-fire . No Dreo-specific outlet/cord incident was found.
- Bob Vila notes tip-over shutoff and overheat protection on the Solaris 718. https://www.bobvila.com/reviews/major-systems/dreo-space-heater-review (published 2024-09-27)

## 2. Dreo owner / long-term findings
- Trustpilot (dreo.com page, fetched 2026-10-15): positives from heater buyers dated 14 Oct 2025 (basement heated better than expected), 20 Jan 2026 (roughly halved heating costs vs old storage heaters), 9 Jul 2026 (bathroom smart heater), 23 Jul 2026 (easy install). One 2-star, 17 Jul 2026: agreed replacement/upgrade arrived as a different, discontinued model, reviewer suspects a used return, support polite but slow. Heater not explicitly named in that one. https://www.trustpilot.com/review/dreo.com
- Trustpilot (other-locale page, per search snippet, date not shown): one reviewer said a heater lost output after about 5-6 weeks and then shut off and never restarted; another said a unit advertised for 1000 sq ft barely handles 500 sq ft in dry winter. https://dk.trustpilot.com/review/dreo.com ; https://it.trustpilot.com/review/dreo.com
- Fan stopping at 2-4 months: NOT found in any source. The closest is the 5-6 week heater shutdown above.
- ShopSavvy TLDR summary (a digest, not an owner review; model not identified; 2026-02-12): quiet hum, fast warm-up, tip-over/overheat protection. Complaints: hard-to-grip handle, light base tips, display shows set temp only (not room temp), plug/cord gets warm, blows cold air after shutoff, Eco mode power-cycling noise at night. Reports no hardware failures. https://shopsavvy.com/reviews-tldr/dreo-space-heater.md
- Walmart listings: star/count figures appear on Walmart pages (e.g. 4.6 stars, 2,184-2,323 reviews on the 1500W oscillating remote model; 4.6 / 8,965 on a silver thermostat model). Snapshot dates not shown and individual review text was not retrievable, so no owner comments were gathered. https://www.walmart.com/ip/8327757351 ; https://www.walmart.com/shop/deals/dreo-products
- Sam's Club reviews and Best Buy/Home Depot/Lowe's/Target Dreo pages: blocked by bot checks or not returned. Nothing usable.
- limteam.net "long-term user" posts on DR-HSH006 (published 2024-11-17): HSH006 is a ceramic tower, 1500W PTC, and per this site it does NOT oscillate; 41-95F thermostat; remote needs line of sight; plastic build; heated ~200 sq ft office; no failures or smell mentioned. The site is an affiliate blog (Amazon links), so treat as weak evidence, not independent owner data. https://limteam.net/?p=4921
- Bob Vila, Solaris 718 (published 2024-09-27, editorial test, not owner): 216 sq ft room 60F to 70F in just under 30 min; thermostat matched reviewer's air monitor; rated 300 sq ft by Dreo; remote is symbols-only; 25 dB claimed low mode. https://www.bobvila.com/reviews/major-systems/dreo-space-heater-review
- Real Homes, Solaris Slim H3 (2022-12-27, editorial): quiet, powerful; may be too small for open-plan. https://www.realhomes.com/reviews/dreo-solaris-slim-h3-space-heater-review
- Expert Reviews (UK) on Dreo 714s lists "not as quiet as claimed" as a con (different model). https://www.expertreviews.co.uk/beauty-wellness/air-treatment/dreo-smart-whole-room-heater-714s-review (date not shown)

## 3. Model/variant notes
- Names are not interchangeable: HSH006 (tower, non-oscillating per limteam), Solaris 718 (tower, oscillating, remote, 300 sq ft claim), Solaris Slim H3 (cylindrical), Solaris Max, 714s, Atom One/Atom Core (cube/small), MC816 (tower seen on Sam's Club). Check the exact model before quoting a review. Dreo manuals define E1/E2 as temperature-sensor open/short circuit (replace unit). https://manualsfile.com/product/k46km1fb1v3.html
- Warranty: Nerd Techy says 12 months for the Solaris Max (https://nerdtechy.com/dreo-solaris-max-review, date not shown); a Trustpilot tower-fan reviewer mentioned a 5-year warranty, which is a fan, not a heater.

## 4. Counterfeit / fulfilment
- Dreo's brand-protection page says counterfeit/unauthorized units carry no warranty or support, and lists official stores on authorized marketplaces including Walmart; look for the "Official DREO Store" label. https://www.dreo.com/pages/brand-protection (date not shown). No reports of counterfeit Dreo heaters found.
- Fulfilment complaint: 17 Jul 2026 Trustpilot item above.
