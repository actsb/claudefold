### What owners report
- **Auto-renewal at $19.99/mo is the default.** Google's terms say "To avoid being charged, you must cancel before the end of the Offer Period." If you cancel during the trial, access continues until the trial ends. Source: Google help (above).
  - A Pixel 9 Pro XL owner on an Android Central forum said their free year ended and they were charged $19.99. https://forums.androidcentral.com/threads/google-photos.1082245 (2025, exact post date UNVERIFIED)
  - MobileSyrup (Aug 27 2025) cited Reddit users saying Google bills month to month, so cancelling late costs about one month. https://mobilesyrup.com/2025/08/27/reminder-pixel-9-pros-free-year-of-gemini-pro-is-running-out/
- **Redemption problems (launch, Aug to Sep 2025).** PiunikaWeb (Sep 4 2025) collected owner reports:
  - "Check offers" showed none available, especially in the UK.
  - Owners who had claimed the Pixel 9 Pro offer the year before were locked out.
  - The offer was buried in settings.
  - Support agents gave contradictory answers on whether YouTube Premium disqualifies you.
  - Workarounds: Gemini app → profile → Upgrade; Google One → Settings → "Check for offers"; check again later.
  - Source: https://piunikaweb.com/2025/09/04/pixel-10-pro-free-ai-pro-offer-redemption-nightmare
  - Android Central forum, Aug 28 to 29 2025: one owner fixed a missing offer by reinstalling the Google One app. Another never saw it because their Google One plan was billed through T-Mobile (third-party billing). https://forums.androidcentral.com/threads/free-google-one-ai-pro-1-year-trial.1082137
- **Official exclusions (Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 offers):**
  - Add-ons such as YouTube Premium
  - A 5 TB-or-higher plan
  - Third-party billing
  - A family plan where you are not the plan manager
  - If you already pay for Google One, your existing plan is cancelled when the trial starts and may be refunded pro rata.
  - The plan can be shared with up to 5 family members.
  - Sources: Google help (above); 9to5Google Aug 12 2026.
- **The pre-order cancellation trap (Aug 2025).** Reddit owners who cancelled or changed a Pixel 10 pre-order lost their existing Google One / AI Pro plan, because the trial started at order time. Some also lost store credit and Fi perks. Sources: Android Authority, https://www.androidauthority.com/pixel-10-pre-order-warning-issue-3590774/ ; Android Headlines, Aug 2025, https://www.androidheadlines.com/2025/08/google-pixel-10-pre-order-benefits-warning-canceling-google-one.html
- **"Trial" is not full AI Pro (Oct 2026).** 9to5Google (Oct 16 2026) reports that free AI Pro trial users (Pixel and others) do not get:
  - Gemini 4 Argon in Antigravity
  - YouTube Premium Lite, described as "unavailable until trials … conclude"
  - Google One's 10% Google Store cashback
  - You also cannot convert to paid from inside the app; you must wait or contact support.
  - Source: https://9to5google.com/2026/10/16/google-restricts-every-free-ai-pro-trial-from-pixel-more-compared-to-paid-subscribers/ (also Android Headlines, Oct 2026, https://androidheadlines.com/2026/10/google-ai-pro-trial-restrictions.html)
- **Surprise second year (Sep 2026).** A Reddit user with a day-one Pixel 10 Pro reported redeeming a second 12-month AI Pro (5 TB) trial from a My Pixel app promo after the first one lapsed. Their parents did the same. Some found it in the Google One app instead. It appears US-centric, and no Pro Fold owners had reported it. Google has not confirmed it (UNVERIFIED as a general offer). Sources: TalkAndroid, Sep 25 2026, https://www.talkandroid.com/531958-free-google-ai-pro/ ; Android Authority, https://androidauthority.com/google-pixel-10-pro-ai-second-annual-free-trial-3714939 (403, snippet only)
- **Do owners actually use it?** I found no survey of keep-versus-cancel rates (UNVERIFIED). Anecdotes:
  - Digital Trends' one-year Pixel 10 Pro owner (Aug 9 2026) said Gemini features were "more useful than expected". https://www.digitaltrends.com/phones/ive-used-the-pixel-10-pro-a-year-i-hope-google-fixes-these-nagging-issues-on-the-pixel-11-pro/
  - Forum posters value the storage most. One 200 GB subscriber weighed the free year against about $239/yr afterwards; another planned to cancel and drop back to a cheaper plan (Android Central forum, Aug 2025).
- **When the trial ends: what happens to storage.**
  - Limits reset at the end of the billing cycle. Existing files stay, but you can't add new content over the limit.
  - Photo backup and Gmail send/receive may stop working.
  - Source: Google help (above).
  - Android Authority suggested downgrading to the cheaper 2 TB storage plan ($9.99) instead of paying for AI Pro (snippet, 2025, https://www.androidauthority.com/google-pixel-9-pro-free-trial-ending-3592030).

## 2. Per phone: praise, complaints, what fails first

**Pixel 10 Pro / Pro XL (Tensor G5)**
- **Praise:** software, camera, display, compact size.
  - Digital Trends, Aug 9 2026 (one-year owner).
  - Best Buy review summaries credit the camera, performance and battery (snippet).
- **Complaints, from one-year owner writeups:**
  - Battery won't reliably last a day.
  - Charging takes close to 2 hours and the phone gets warm while charging.
  - Touchscreen stops responding, 10 to 15 times on a bad day.
  - Poor call and network reliability.
  - Source: Digital Trends, Aug 9 2026 (URL above).
  - Android Authority's "after 10 months" piece (Jun 2026) lists dropped/missed calls, heat and slow charging. https://androidauthority.com/google-pixel-10-pro-ten-months-later-issues-3680886 (403, snippet only)

## 4. Questions people ask (with answers)

1. **"Is the free AI Pro worth it?"**
   - If you'd pay for storage anyway, yes: 5 TB plus Gemini for $0 during the trial.
   - But trial access is restricted: no Gemini 4 Argon in Antigravity, no YouTube Premium Lite, no 10% store credit (9to5Google, Oct 16 2026).
   - Cancel before it ends or pay $19.99/mo.
2. **"Does a Pixel 10 Pro bought on Black Friday still include the year of AI Pro?"** Per Google's page, no: the offer ended Oct 31 2026. A Pixel 11 Pro gets 6 months, and the base Pixel 11 gets nothing (Google help; 9to5Google).
3. **"Can I stack two trials (Pixel + Samsung)?"**
   - Officially no. You can hold one trial at a time, lengths are not stackable, and redeeming a new trial revokes the current one (Google offer documentation, https://support.google.com/product-documentation/answer/16667608?hl=en).
   - One Android Central forum user (Aug 2025) reported redeeming them one after another, after each expired. That is anecdotal.
4. **"I already pay for Google One. Will I lose it?"** Your current plan is cancelled when the trial starts, and you may get a pro-rata refund (Google help). Family members who aren't the manager can't redeem; leave the family group first (Gadgets To Use, Aug 27 2025, https://gadgetstouse.com/blog/2025/08/27/fix-cant-redeem-google-ai-pro-plan-on-pixel-10-phones).
5. **"Do I need a card?"** Yes. Both the Pixel and Samsung trials require a payment method (Google help; Samsung redemption guide).
6. **"Does the offer transfer, or work on used/open-box?"**
   - No source confirms this either way (UNVERIFIED). The offer is account-based and needs a purchased and activated device.
   - Google's Certified Refurbished Pixel 9 Pro came with its own 6-month AI Plus offer for new subscribers, voided on return (9to5Google, Oct 12 2026, https://9to5google.com/2026/10/12/google-pixel-9-certified-refurbished-release/).
7. **"Is Galaxy AI going paid?"** The basic features stay free per Samsung's Jan 2026 wording. A paid Premium tier at about $5/mo is reported but unconfirmed (see §2).
8. **"Unlocked or carrier deal?"** See §5. Unlocked Best Buy/Samsung discounts have no line lock. Carrier "free" deals pay out over 24 to 36 months.
