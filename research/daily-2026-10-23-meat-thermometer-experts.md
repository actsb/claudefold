## 3. Official manual facts
- Thermapen ONE [FETCHED, https://www.thermoworks.com/content/pdf/thermapen_one_operating_instructions.pdf]: one pre-installed AAA, "should last up to 2,000 hours"; low-battery icon; "Protected from dust and immersion in water to 39 in depth for up to 30 minutes" (listed as IP67 on product pages); do not rotate probe while submerged; not dishwasher safe; do not leave in oven. Minimum immersion 1/8 in. Manual has no ice-water steps; separate trim doc (Thermapen_Splash_Proof_Calibration.pdf) says accept 31.2-32.8F, trim only if necessary at 32F [SNIPPET].
- ThermoPop 2 [SNIPPET; manual PDF fetch returned 404/HTML]: CR2032 coin cell, ~4,000 hours claimed; IP67 waterproof; accuracy +/-1.0F from 14-208F; 2-3 s; 2-yr warranty; auto-off ~10 min; "Bat" flashes when low. ThermoWorks: within spec if 30-34F in a proper ice bath (+/-2F quoted for original ThermoPop - UNVERIFIED for Pop 2). Not for oven/grill use.
- ThermoWorks ice bath method [SNIPPET, https://blog.thermoworks.com/thermal-secrets-creating-properly-made-ice-bath/]: fill vessel with crushed ice, add water to ~1/2 in below ice top, stir, probe in center off walls/bottom; reads 32.0F +/-0.1F.
- ThermoPro TP19H [SNIPPET, manualslib copy]: 1 AAA; IP65 (rinse, not submersion); +/-0.9F (14-212F); 3-4 s claimed; CAL button hold 5 s then press to confirm; auto-off ~90 s; ice-water step UNVERIFIED for TP19H. Battery life claim (~3,000 h) from retailer listings only - UNVERIFIED.
- ThermoPro TP620 [SNIPPET]: 1 AAA; IP65; ice-water calibration (ice bottle + distilled water, ~10 min, hold CAL 5 s; mixture must be within +/-2C); 2-3 s, +/-0.9F claimed; battery life UNVERIFIED.
- Turkey insertion: FSIS = innermost thigh, wing, thickest breast, away from bone (see above). Manufacturer manuals have no turkey-specific text found (UNVERIFIED).

## Takeaways for the guide
- Thermapen ONE: top pick at ATK, Serious Eats (2023), Prudent, Amazing Ribs (Platinum). ThermoPop 2: budget pick at ATK, Serious Eats; Wirecutter (mirror only, UNVERIFIED); Amazing Ribs Gold. Javelin PRO Duo is the recurring mid-price alternative. ThermoPro TP19H: cheap, IP65, weak in Prudent test (slowest); TP622/TP620 fare better (ATK TP622 2.1 s).
- Read times disagree by test method: ONE 1.0-1.1 s (ATK/Prudent boiling), 2-3 s (Serious Eats); Pop 2 2.6 s (ATK) vs 4-8 s (Prudent).
