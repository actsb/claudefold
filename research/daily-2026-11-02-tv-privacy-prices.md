## 8. Recalls (brand/pages/recall-check.html and CPSC)

- `brand/pages/recall-check.html` covers 228 CPSC recalls since Jan 2025 in six categories (heating and sleep, power banks and batteries, car, kitchen, cleaning, bathroom). **It has no TV category and no Samsung, LG, TCL, Roku or Sony entries** (only "HSN television network" text). So it cannot be used as a TV recall check. If the guide links to Recall Check, say it does not cover TVs and link CPSC directly: https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls
- cpsc.gov recall list fetched 2026-11-02 (first page, Oct 15-29, 2026): **no TV recalls** (dressers, heaters, power banks, etc.).
- Search for 2025-26 CPSC recalls of Samsung, LG, TCL, Roku, Sony TVs: **none found**. Not exhaustive (category filter "Televisions" on cpsc.gov was not run; the site's search is JS-driven).
- Older, not 55": LG recalled ~52,000 86-inch TVs sold with stands for tip-over/entrapment: 86UQ8000AUB, 86UQ7070ZUD, 86UQ7590PUD, 86NANO75UQA; sold at Walmart, Best Buy, Costco, March-September 2022; published 2023-01-12 [Indep: Fox Business]. Wall-mounted units unaffected. Not relevant to the 55" picks.
- CPSC "Anchor It" TV tip-over reminder before Super Bowl LX, Feb 2026: https://cpsc.gov/Newsroom/News-Releases/2026/CPSC-Urges-Fans-Anchor-TVs-and-Furniture-Before-Super-Bowl-LX . Useful as a safety line.

## 9. Buy-or-wait notes (Inference)

- Cheapest verified path to a "least AI" setup: TCL QM6K or Roku Select 55" ($250-$550) + Apple TV ($299, from Nov 13), TV kept offline. Total about $550-$850.
- S90H already 35% off at Best Buy and Samsung.com (2026-10-19). Walmart and Best Buy early events (Nov 6-15) are the first chance for a deeper price; compare with the 40% line in the blueprint.

- Sony 7 II: Abt $1,398 vs $2,099.99 list = 33% off. Sony does not sell as cheap as the others; position it as the "least ads/AI features if you disable Samba" pick, not value.
