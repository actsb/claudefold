## 6. Temu / AliExpress look-alikes
No Temu/AliExpress-specific test or CPSC action on cordless inflators was found. What can be supported:
- Gauge error on cheap unbranded inflators: forum reports of -7 psi error and a tire showing 20 when a gauge read ~45 [SNIPPET: https://budgetlightforum.com/comment/1792762 ]; anecdote only.
- Even in a mainstream brand test the Ideaworks gauge was "inaccurate" (CR 2017). Which? says a wrong screen reading is a safety risk [SNIPPET].
- CPSC has warned about uncertified lithium batteries sold on AliExpress, Walmart marketplace, eBay, DHgate and others (UPP e-bike battery, April 2024; Temu not named) https://www.cpsc.gov/Newsroom/News-Releases/2024/CPSC-Warns-Consumers-to-Stop-Using-Unit-Pack-Power-UPP-E-bike-Batteries-Due-to-Fire-and-Burn-Hazards-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-and-Death [SNIPPET]. Generalizing to inflators is inference; phrase as "uncertified lithium packs are a known risk category", not "Temu inflators catch fire".
- Overheating/duty cycle even in good units: Avid recommends 3-min cooldown per 10 min; Trekure shut down at 37 psi from heat (Outdoor Life).
- Caution list for copy: no recognizable certification (UL/ETL for battery), inflated mAh claims, no brand warranty, uncertain return path, gauge unverified. Also Walmart.com marketplace sells similar no-name 150 PSI units at $13-$30 (e.g. Slickdeals Walmart items), so cheap look-alikes are not only a Temu issue.

## 8. Cost of ownership
- Gas-station air: most stations charge about $1-$2 for a few minutes (2026 guide, SNIPPET); Connecticut and California mandate free air (CBS NY / state sources; "only two states" quote is undated, UNVERIFIED): https://www.cbsnews.com/newyork/news/hempstead-gas-stations-free-air/ . The brief's "$1-2.50" upper end is UNVERIFIED; use "$1-$2".
- Tow: one Kentucky tow company's published rate sheet, effective 2026-08-01: $100 hookup + $2.50/mile en route + $5.00/loaded mile, $125 minimum, +$100 holiday/weekend/overnight [SNIPPET: https://drive.ky.gov/Motor-Carriers/Towing%20and%20Storage%20Rate%20Library/AJ's-Towing&Recovery-Rate-Sheet.pdf]. Generic guides: $75-$125 for 5-10 miles (low-quality SEO; use only as a range with the KY sheet). So a single tow plausibly $125-$250+. 
- Many roadside plans tow free to ~15 miles or nearest shop; plan example Allstate Roadside Advantage "starting at $89 first year" (SoFi, FETCHED): https://www.sofi.com/learn/content/roadside-assistance-flat-tire-cost/
- Honest framing: a TPMS light (fires at 25% under placard, NHTSA FMVSS 138 https://www.nhtsa.gov/sites/nhtsa.dot.gov/files/fmvss/tirepressure-fmvss-138.pdf) usually means you can drive slowly to air; a tow is for a tire that is flat or leaking fast, where an inflator may buy time to reach a shop but is not a fix. Do not claim an inflator replaces a tow. Plug/patch cost $6-$40 (low-quality guide).
- Break-even: a $1.50 air machine visit x ~12 per year = ~$18; an $89.99 Fanttik breaks even in about 5 years on air fees alone; the value is convenience and emergencies, not savings. This arithmetic is mine, based on the $1-$2 range.
