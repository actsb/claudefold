## 2. How deep 2025 Black Friday went

* Adobe Analytics, as reported for Nov 28, 2025: average discount 20.2% on appliances and 18.6% on furniture, versus 18.2% and 16.8% on the Wednesday before Thanksgiving, and 2.1% and 1.2% on Nov 1. Electronics peaked at about 29% (secondary report; the Adobe original was not retrieved, so label it "as reported"). Pre-season forecast: electronics 28%, appliances 18%, furniture 18% (businesswire.com/news/home/20251006964384/en).
* Adobe: record $11.8B US online spend on Black Friday 2025, up 9.1% (techcrunch.com/2025/11/29/black-friday-sets-online-spending-record-of-11-8b-adobe-says). Salesforce said prices were up about 7% on average, with orders down 1%.
* Home Depot via NBC Select (Nov 2025): "32% off Ninja kitchen appliances" and "39% off Shark vacuums", with the caveat that not every item in a brand is cut. Dyson and Shark were "up to 50% off" in the Cyber Monday list (nbcnews.com/select/shopping/home-depot-cyber-monday-deals-2025-rcna246636).
* Target 2025: "up to 50% off" on kitchen and floor care (Crock-Pot, Dyson, Bissell). An early-November claim of "up to 75% off" kitchen is an early-sale claim only (techradar.com, Nov 2025).
* Best Buy 2025: a 40% sale on select Ninja, Instant Pot, Samsung and LG items, per a roundup (toptenreviews.com/best-buy-black-friday-deals). Ninja AF101 air fryer $149.99, was $169.99 (a different air fryer, not the 4-qt).
* Costco 2025: Ninja Crispi $124.99, was $159.99 (about 22%), until Dec 1; T-fal Clipso 8-qt $79.99, was $99.99 (20%), until Nov 28 (eatthis.com/costco-black-friday-deals-nov-2025/).
* Rule of thumb for the post: 20% is typical for appliances, 25-40% for named doorbusters on Ninja/Shark/Instant Pot, and Dyson and KitchenAid stay the stingiest at 15-30% off a high list. Not tied to the Amazon price.
* Wirecutter, CNET, Consumer Reports and Tom's Guide 2025 Black Friday pages were not retrievable in a form that supports numbers. Consumer Reports says its deal pages need membership (consumerreports.org/money/sales-promotions/best-black-friday-deals-home-depot-lowes-a7236595058/). UNVERIFIED as a source for percent figures.

## 4. Return windows, price match and policies

* Amazon: 2025 holiday window ran Nov 1-Dec 31, returnable until Jan 31, 2026 (aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-holiday-return-policy-2025). The 2026 terms are not found. Standard is 30 days.
* Walmart: the 2025 season ran purchases Oct 1-Dec 31 with returns until Jan 31. A third-party site says 2026 is essentially unchanged, Oct 1-Dec 31, 2026 until Jan 31, 2027 (geekseller.com/blog/walmart-holiday-return-policy-2026-extended-returns-through-january-31-2027/), UNVERIFIED because Walmart's own page wasn't read. Exclusions include major appliances, Dyson, wireless phones and protection plans. Marketplace items follow seller rules. Walmart price match is limited to Amazon-sold identical items per 3rd-party guides, UNVERIFIED.
* Target: 2025 holiday extension gave 30 days from Dec 26 for electronics (deadline Jan 24, 2026), 14 days for Apple and Beats. Sources differ on when the purchase window opened (Nov 1 vs Nov 7). 2026: not found. Price match only against Amazon and Walmart (sold and shipped by them) and Target's own price in the last 14 days (changes in Aug 2024; Doctor of Credit and priceva.com/blog/target-price-match).
* Best Buy: holiday returns through Jan 15, 2026 for purchases from late Oct (Priceva, 3rd-party; the Canadian page differs). Standard is 15 days. The holiday price match covered Best Buy-sold items, with exclusions for Black Friday/Cyber Monday event pricing, marketplace sellers, clearance and open-box (priceva.com/blog/best-buy-price-match; 9to5toys Oct 20, 2025).
* Home Depot: no extended holiday window found; standard is 90 days with receipt, 365 days on a Home Depot card (dealnews.com/features/home-depot/return-policy/). Price match: identical in-stock items from the retailer itself, not third-party sellers, at time of purchase only (dealnews, June 2025; homedepot.com price guarantee page). The 10% beat is Canada-only per some sources, UNVERIFIED.
* Costco: no price matching. A 30-day price adjustment applies if Costco's own price drops after purchase; manufacturer-funded promos may be excluded. Electronics, including major appliances, have a 90-day return window; small appliances are UNVERIFIED (dailydot.com/costco-90-dollar-price-adjustment-policy; slickdeals.net costco-price-adjustment-policy).
* Subscribe & Save (Amazon): 5% for 1-4 items per delivery, 15% for 5 or more on one date; up to 20% on some baby items; no Prime needed (daily.slickdeals.net/shopping/the-ins-and-outs-of-amazon-subscribe-save/). It applies to consumables, such as the Levoit filters, O-Cedar refills and Little Green solution, not the appliances themselves.
* Bundles: Walmart sells the Little Green 1400B with tools; Costco has an O-Cedar bundle; KitchenAid sells an accessory-pack deal; Made In gives free pieces. Instant Pot, Ninja and Shark ship accessory sets in different SKUs, so check the model number.
