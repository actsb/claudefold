Mirrors that quote the manual: manuals.plus, manualslib, device.report (snippets below came via these/search summaries of the NOCO guide; wording is paraphrase).

Steps (per snippets of NOCO guide):
1. Turn off all vehicle loads (headlights, radio, A/C). Connect red clamp to positive, black clamp to negative/ground (manual detail on exact ground point UNVERIFIED).
2. Press the Power button. If connected properly the white Boost LED lights; unit auto-detects battery and enters boost.
3. Start the vehicle. Most start immediately; some need the boost connected up to 30 seconds. If it does not start, wait 20-30 seconds and retry.
4. Once running, disconnect clamps and remove the unit.

LEDs: Charge LEDs 25/50/75/100% show internal battery (green 100% solid when full, pulses during maintenance charge). Power LED white = on. White Boost LED = boost active/ready. Red Error LED solid = reverse polarity (unit will not operate); Error LED blinking = internal battery too hot, let it cool.

Manual override (boost button, red exclamation-in-circle): hold 3 seconds; white Boost LED flashes (one source says charge LEDs chase). Use for 12 V lead-acid batteries below about 2 V that the unit cannot detect. Disables spark-proof and reverse-polarity protection; manual warns of sparks/heat/injury and that misuse voids warranty. Does not add power.

Safety: do not attempt more than 5 consecutive jump starts within 15 minutes; rest 15 minutes. Do not jump a damaged or frozen battery. Stop if the unit becomes excessively warm. Operating ambient -20 to 50 C per manual snippet (a spec-site snippet says -30 to +50 C / -4 to 122 F; conflict, UNVERIFIED which is current).

Charging/storage: charge via USB IN (2.1 A port) with included cable; about 3 hours from 0% at 2 A (0.5 A about 12 h, 1 A about 6 h). Store dry at -20 to +50 C. Recharge every 6 months per manual snippet (UNVERIFIED against the 2025 PDF; Family Handyman says holds charge up to a year; older NOCO docs may say 3 months). Confirm before publishing.

Engine limits and specs: 1000 A peak, 12 V; up to 6.0 L gas and 3.0 L diesel; up to 20 starts per full charge (reviewer snippet claims real-world 10-15, UNVERIFIED); 24 Wh (2150 mAh per one retailer snippet); 100-lumen light with 7 modes; USB-A 2.1 A output; IP65; about 2.4 lb with clamps; 12 V only (not for 6 V/24 V systems; UNVERIFIED).

Warranty: One (1) year limited warranty from date of purchase, original purchaser only (from warranty-guide snippet). Note some retailers/NOCO marketing advertise longer terms for other NOCO lines; GB40 = 1 year per this snippet, UNVERIFIED against current NOCO site.
