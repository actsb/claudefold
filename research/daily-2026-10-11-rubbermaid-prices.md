## 6. When Amazon is not the cheapest
(Judgment from the non-Amazon data above. I did not compare against Amazon numbers.)
- Costco's 18/20-pc sets have hit $1.80 to $2.50 per container, below the cheapest 10-pc per-container prices found at Walmart or Target ($2.75 to $3.96).
- Target at 50% off list (12-pc, 2026-02-12) and Circle member pricing (about $3.33 to $3.52 per container) are in the same range as the deal-post prices that came up for Amazon.
- Non-Prime shoppers with a cart under $35 may pay shipping at Amazon, while Walmart/Target store pickup avoids it.
- The 90-day Walmart returns and the Target 120-day guarantee (Rubbermaid's Q&A answer) reduce risk compared with third-party sellers.
- Caveat: no live Amazon comparison was made, so the claim that Amazon is not cheapest is directional, not proven.
