## Brief C — deep dive: Rabbitgoo harness, KONG Classic/Extreme, Earth Rated bags

### Rabbitgoo No-Pull Dog Harness
* ASINs: Large B01M8JT6FT, Medium B01M6YASY1, Small B01MA58881; newer 2026 relistings B0GRQZB2QF / B0GRR1PCXS (UNVERIFIED whether the same harness). Sibling models: 3-buckle Easy On & Off, Tactical, Escape Proof (3-strap; reviewguid 2026: 14,200+ reviews at 4.5). Price $20–26 (2026); Prime Day June 2026 $16 (was $21). Rating "over 100,000 reviews, consistent 4.5 stars" (MyPawAdvisor 2026); brand-wide 422,125 reviews at 4.4 (Kimola). Sold-by, badges, S&S: UNVERIFIED. Also at Chewy (#186425) and Walmart.
* Sizing: Rabbitgoo says measure lower-neck girth and the widest chest just behind the front legs; never by weight alone; two fingers under each strap. Size charts in snippets were mixed across models — take the chart from the listing. A 50-lb Jindo typically measures about 26–30 in chest (UNVERIFIED estimate) → usually Large; pick the size that puts the measurement mid-range. Deep-chest evidence mixed: Rebarkable found it tight under the armpits on deep-chested dogs; a Chewy reviewer with a 50-lb deep-chested dog found no chafe; four-point adjustment praised.
* Escape risk (spitz breeds): dogs back up, drop the head, "Superman" the front legs and slide out; fixes — tighten to two fingers at all four adjusters (especially the neck), seat the girth strap behind the widest ribs, move to a three-strap design (Rabbitgoo Escape Proof), train positively; trainer verdict (Rebarkable): usable budget harness, "not recommended for strong, reactive or escape-prone dogs", wears sooner than expected.
* Fitting steps (manual/RG blog): unbuckle both sides and loosen all four straps; neck loop over the head, logo up; bottom strap between the front legs; clip both buckles; adjust chest then neck by arching the strap through the slider, both sides equally; two-finger check; chest plate clear of the throat, girth clear of the armpits (scratching at straps = too tight or rubbing). Front clip for pullers/lunging, back clip for relaxed walks and jogging. Hand wash only, air dry.
* Owner voice (retail reviews, forums): sturdy, reflective, handle, no-choke, price; a Shiba's lasted two years (Trustpilot). Complaints: buckles breaking after 2–3 months (low single digits), armpit chafe (5–10% of critical reviews), slipping out when the neck is loose, sizing confusion, faster wear than expected.
* Experts: Rebarkable; MyPawAdvisor 2026; Consumer Reports "5 Best Dog Harnesses of 2026" tested it (verdict paywalled); Treeline 2026 best overall Non-stop Dogwear Line 5.0; Dog Gear Review buckle note. Wirecutter pick status UNVERIFIED.
* Alternatives: cheaper Voyager Step-In Flex (~$10–15, no front clip); pricier Ruffwear Front Range (~$60), 2 Hounds Freedom (~$35–45), Non-stop Line 5.0; in-family Rabbitgoo Escape Proof (~$25–30).
