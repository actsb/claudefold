NOCO GB40 manual jump procedure (paraphrased from the guide):
1. Check the vehicle battery is 12V lead-acid; read the vehicle owner's manual.
2. Connect the red clamp to positive, then the black clamp to negative or chassis.
3. Plug the clamp connector (X Connect) into the GB40; turn off all vehicle loads.
4. Press the power button: all LEDs flash; white Boost LED lit means ready; red Error LED means reversed clamps, so swap them.
5. Try to start; some vehicles need up to 30 seconds connected. If it fails, wait 20-30 seconds and retry. No more than five consecutive jumps in 15 minutes; rest 15 minutes after that.
6. Once running, disconnect the clamps (negative first) and remove the unit.
Manual override (force start): used when the battery is below 2V and the Boost LED stays off. With clamps correctly on and the connector plugged in, press and hold the red-exclamation Manual Override button for 3 seconds; the white Boost LED flashes on/off. Spark-proof and reverse-polarity protection are disabled; the manual says to power the unit off before removing the clamps and not to let the clamps touch. GB50 manual is reported (via search, UNVERIFIED) to use the same 3-second hold.

GOOLOO GP4000 manual jump procedure (paraphrased, read): have the unit above 50% charge (2+ bars); plug the smart jumper cable fully in, both cable lights off; red clamp to positive, black to negative; check the cable lights: solid green means start now, solid red with beeping means reversed (fix it); blinking green means low vehicle battery voltage: press BOOST on the cable, green goes solid, start within 30 seconds; no lights/no beep means the vehicle battery may be damaged: check connections, press BOOST, start within 30 seconds. After the engine starts, remove the cable from the pack and the battery. Do not start more than four times in two minutes; do not press BOOST with clamps reversed; do not keep it connected after the engine starts.

## Quick takeaways for the guide
- The brand sizing ladder (6L/3L, 7L/4.5L, 8L/6L gas/diesel) is NOCO's own and consistent across sources; GOOLOO claims one size up to 10L gas/diesel at 4000A peak.
- Peak-amp numbers are not comparable across brands (4000A vs 2000A); say so, and point to cranking amps (GP4000: 800A, from its manual).
- Cold is the real limit: CR found nothing started weak batteries at 0 F with the pack also at 0 F; operating limits on paper are -4 F (GB40, GOOLOO). Keep the pack warm in winter.
- Boost/override disables spark-proof and reverse-polarity protection on NOCO; GOOLOO's BOOST button is separate and its manual warns against using it with reversed clamps.
