## Scope correction (read first)

- **The current Pixel line is the Pixel 11 series.** It was announced Aug 12, 2026 and went on sale Aug 20. The Google Store menu lists Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro Fold, Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro and Pro XL, Pixel 10 Pro Fold and Pixel 9a.
- **The Pixel 10 Pro's year of AI Pro is closed.** The redeem-by date was Oct 31, 2026. Google's Pixel 10 Pro store page still headlines "Unlock Google AI Pro for a year on us ($239 value)", but its own footnote says "Redeem the offer by October 31, 2026 at 11:59pm PT." That is a stale banner, and it is a strong "watch out" point for the guide.
- **Samsung:** the S26 and S26 Ultra have **no** Google AI Pro offer in the US. The current US AI Pro offer covers the Z Fold8, Z Fold8 Ultra, Z Flip8 and S26 FE.
