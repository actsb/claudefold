# KitchenAid Classic vs Artisan vs Artisan Mini vs 5.5-qt Bowl-Lift: prices and channels brief
Research agent 3 (Price and channels). Compiled 2026-11-27 (Black Friday 2026; Cyber Monday is Nov 30).

How we got the data:
- **[F] page fetch.** Only Target product and search pages could be fetched. KitchenAid.com gave 503 (WebFetch) or an Akamai 403 (curl), and Best Buy gave 503. Walmart showed a bot wall. Kohl's, Macy's and Williams Sonoma returned 403, and Costco returned 429.
- **[S] search snippet.** Prices from other stores come from these and are only as fresh as the search index.
- **[D] deal site.** Slickdeals, NBC Select and others, with dates.
- **Amazon prices are excluded completely.** That rules out Amazon listings, Keepa/camel/trackpin-style trackers, and any deal post "at Amazon". Most press roundups this week (Koupon, 9to5Toys, The Gadgeteer, TechRadar) lead with Amazon prices, so they were dropped.
