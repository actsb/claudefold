### 3. Brief A's recommendation

* **Best value on Amazon now: LUXE NEO 185 (B00P2XZIP2)** — two nozzles (rear and feminine) and self-cleaning for $45.99 (Amazon, 2 Dec 2025); 4.7 on 30,850 ratings is the highest score among attachments with more than 10,000 ratings in this data (NEO 120 4.6 on ~84k; Veken 4.4 on 27k; SlimEdge 4.4 on 24.5k; TUSHY Classic 3.0 4.5–4.6 on 9.1k); about half the TUSHY Classic 3.0's price; about 600 times the review base of the KOHLER M100 (50 Home Depot reviews at 4.0).
* Runner-ups: cheapest good = NEO 120 (from $31.40; #1 in Bidet Attachments; 1 nozzle); warm-water attachment = NEO 320 (~$60; vs TUSHY Spa 3.0 $149, 4.7 on 11.2k); electric under $350 = SmartBidet SB-1000 ($279.99 Amazon, 20 May 2026; Home Depot 4.7 on 428); premium seat = TOTO Washlet S2 ($420; CNN's 2026 best overall); manual seat = Brondell Swash EcoSeat ($89.98; #1 in Bidet Seats) or KOHLER M300 ($93–$110; Kohler.com 4.3 on 91).
