### 2. Health and medical evidence

* **Cleveland Clinic, "Power Wash: Why Using a Bidet Is Sanitary and Safe"** (gastroenterologist Christine Lee, MD) [S]: more sanitary than toilet paper; water beats "a few squares of dry TP" at removing trace fecal matter; gentler on skin (wiping can chafe and cause tiny cuts); can be more comfortable with hemorrhoids or fissures. Cautions: spray front to back, right pressure and angle so water isn't forced into the vagina, to avoid vaginal or urinary infections.
* **Mayo Clinic Press, "Hemorrhoid Care Expert"** (Sarah Umar, MD, gastroenterologist) [S, paraphrase]: bidets help people with symptoms after a bowel movement and cut down on wiping; "a Mayo Clinic expert" says bidets won't harm or aggravate hemorrhoids (confirm the speaker before attributing it to Dr. Umar).
* **Harvard Health** hemorrhoid pages [S]: repeated wiping worsens itching; sitz baths and patting dry; **no Harvard statement on bidets found**.
* **Baig et al., "Be Kind to Your Behind" systematic review** (Evid-Based Complement Altern Med, May 2022; doi:10.1155/2022/1633965) [S]: habitual use had no impact on the odds of hemorrhoids; habitual use **may increase** the odds of anal itching; 2 case series of perianal burns or anterior anal fissures; 1 RCT: bidets non-inferior to sitz baths for post-hemorrhoidectomy pain.
* **Asakura, Nakano & Omae** (Epidemiol Infect, 2018; 10,305 Japanese respondents, 2013–2016) [S]: habitual use did not significantly raise hemorrhoids or urogenital infections; male habitual users more often reported new irritated skin around the anus (adjusted RR 1.36).
* **Ryoo et al.** (J Korean Med Sci 2011) [S]: low or medium pressure, warm water and a wide stream lowered anal resting pressure, much like a warm sitz bath.
* **BIDLOW randomized trial** (BMC Surgery; hemorrhoidectomy; electronic bidet at 38 °C and very low force vs warm sitz baths, 7 days) [S]: day-7 pain 38.3 vs 42.0 (no difference), but non-inferiority not proven.
* **AJOT 75(5), automated bidet intervention** [S]: feasible and reduced the physical help needed with toilet hygiene (caregiver burden).
* **Tsunoda A** (J Anus Rectum Colon, Oct 2021) [S]: excessive use may cause anal itching and anal incontinence; immunocompromised hospital patients should be careful with shared bidet toilets.
* **Ogino et al.** (J Obstet Gynaecol Res 2010) [S]: among 268 women with increased discharge, Lactobacillus absent in 42.86% of habitual users vs 8.77% of non-users (observational, symptomatic women). **Obstet Gynecol, June 2013** (2,545 Tokyo births) [S]: preterm birth 15.8% vs 16.0% (adjusted OR 1.04); normal use poses no clinical risk for preterm birth or bacterial vaginosis.
* **Nozzle hygiene** [S]: Tokyo Medical University Hospital hematology ward (2020–2021): nozzles may harbor multidrug-resistant Pseudomonas aeruginosa (hospital setting); warm-water tanks lose residual chlorine, raising bacterial counts. Consumer cleaning frequency UNVERIFIED — point to the manufacturer's instructions.
* UNVERIFIED (vendor/lay sites only, do not publish): bidets reduce UTIs in Japanese nursing homes.

### 3. DIY installation facts

* Steps (non-electric attachment) [S, vendor/retailer how-tos]: close the valve behind the toilet and flush; disconnect the supply hose; remove the seat, set the attachment over the bolt holes, refit the seat bolts ("firmly but do not overtighten"); fit the T-adapter on the fill valve, the supply hose on the T and the bidet hose on the branch; open the valve slowly and check every joint for leaks.
* Time [S]: "about 15 minutes with one wrench" (vendor how-to); TUSHY claims 8.5 minutes (via Bob Vila); GH testers: TUSHY met its "10 minutes or less" promise.
* Warm-water kits screw a female 3/8" T-adapter onto the sink's hot-water valve [S]; the extra hose is usually 9–10 ft; usually one small hole through the vanity. 7/8" toilet fill-valve thread: UNVERIFIED this session.
* Common mistakes [S]: overtightening damages fittings; not checking for leaks; cheap brands that leak (Mahoney). Teflon tape: follow the manual.
* Compatibility [S, fitment guides]: hinge bolt spacing normally about 5.5 in (155 mm); one guide says under 5.5 in or over 7.5 in won't fit; allow at least 1.5 in between the bolt holes and the tank; one-piece toilets are OK if the tank meets the bowl at roughly 90°; skirted toilets usually work but may need extra parts; hidden hinges UNVERIFIED.
* Electric seats [S]: NEC 210.8(A) requires GFCI protection for bathroom receptacles (local adoption varies); no extension cords or power strips (vendor guidance); seats draw about 500–1,400 W; cords often about 4 ft, outlet within about 3–4 ft of the toilet centerline. **Electrician costs** [S]: Angi (2026 data) GFCI outlet installed $130–$300, average about $210; HomeGuide/HomeAdvisor new GFCI install $150–$350; a Fort Worth electrician $350–$600 for a new 20-amp circuit with GFCI outlet incl. permit.
* Renters [S]: non-electric attachments need no permanent changes; warm-water models may need a small hole in the vanity (check the lease).
* Backflow [S]: ASME A112.4.2/CSA B45.16 "Personal hygiene devices for water closets" covers bidet seats, sprayers and retrofit devices; look for that certification.

### Counterfeits, look-alikes, safety
* No documented counterfeit LUXE/TUSHY units (UNVERIFIED either way). Context: ITIF (8/20/25) bought 51 products from suspicious Temu, AliExpress and SHEIN listings and judged 24 likely counterfeit; follow-up 2/17/26.
* Inflated reference prices: NBC Select pitched a Pocaland attachment as "76% off, $150 down to $40" (4/1/26) — far above LUXE's everyday $39.99–$59.99. Judge deals by dated price history, not percent-off badges.
* T-valves (vendor guidance): metal/brass crack less than plastic; overtightening can crack the tank inlet or the T; a missing or twisted O-ring causes drips; never loosen fittings under pressure.
* TOTO C2 is rated 403 W (512 W max) — needs a grounded outlet within reach. Warm-water attachments deliver whatever the water heater supplies (scald guidance UNVERIFIED). No bidet-seat recalls found on CPSC (not proof none exist).
