## 1. Expert sources

### America's Test Kitchen / Cook's Illustrated: strongest source, current and fully read
- URL: https://www.americastestkitchen.com/equipment-reviews/2593 ("The Best Stand Mixers of 2026")
- Authors: Lisa McManus and Sarah Sandler. Published October 1, 2026.
- **Picks:**

  | Label | Model |
  |---|---|
  | Winner | Ankarsrum Assistent Original 7 Liter |
  | **Best Mid-Priced Mixer** | **KitchenAid Classic Series 4.5 Quart Tilt-Head** |
  | Best Lighter-Weight Mixer | NutriMill Artiste |
  | **Best Small Mixer** | **KitchenAid Artisan Mini 3.5 Quart Tilt-Head** |

- **Classic 4.5 qt**
  - Listed at $400, seen on sale at $280 at Amazon. Measured working capacity 3.25 qt, weight 21.5 lb, 1-year warranty.
  - Its "across-the-board performance knocked out many competitors that were bigger and much more costly."
  - Downsides:
    - The tilt head broke during an extreme abuse test.
    - The bowl has no handle.
    - The tools are white epoxy-coated, not stainless.
  - It has an adjustable beater height.
- **Artisan 5 qt (KSM150)**
  - "a competent mixer," but about $150 more than the Classic for roughly similar performance.
  - Actual capacity 3 qt despite the 5-qt label. Weight 20.7 lb, 1-year warranty.
  - It clicked and struggled at first with bagel dough, but finished.
  - It rocked and got very warm in abuse testing, yet completed all 12 extra batches.
  - The bowl sometimes sticks on its base after hard use.
  - Testers praised the large bowl handle.
- **Artisan Mini 3.5 qt**
  - Listed at $450, seen on sale at $330. Actual capacity 1.9 qt, weight 16.05 lb, 1-year warranty.
  - It aced nearly every test except sticky, high-hydration pizza dough, which suggests too little torque for the hardest jobs.
  - It **can't fit double batches**.
  - Testers wanted a bowl handle and a lower price.
  - It fits all KitchenAid attachments except the ice cream maker.
- **Artisan Plus 5 qt (new 2026)**
  - 350 W versus 325 W on the Artisan. Adds slow start, ½ fold speed, a bowl light, a double flex-edge paddle and uncoated stainless tools.
  - It nearly walked off the counter while kneading and overheated twice.
  - Verdict: "not our favorite KitchenAid mixer."
- **7 Qt Bowl-Lift**
  - Listed at $650, seen on sale at $499. Actual capacity 5.25 qt, weight 25.6 lb, **2-year warranty**.
  - Testers wrote "Wow." It handled big batches and 12 extra batches of dough without trouble.
  - Downside: it is heavy and takes a lot of space. *ATK did not test the 5.5-qt KSM55.*
- Shared note: KitchenAid advises not going above speed 2 with the dough hook. Testers said speed 2 worked but took much longer.
- The older small-mixer URLs (`/equipment-reviews/2025-small-stand-mixers`) now 301-redirect to this page.

## 3. Manual / instructions
The official kitchenaid.com PDFs (e.g. `/content/dam/global/documents/200908/owners-manual-W10190231-RevB.pdf`, `.../202604/product-guide-w11769852-revb.pdf`) returned 403/503 here and are **UNVERIFIED** for content. The facts below come from official KitchenAid manuals hosted by other sites. They are the manufacturer's documents, but check them against the US kitchenaid.com copy before quoting as current.

### US Artisan KSM150 owner's manual
- Source: KitchenAid KSM150PSER owner's manual, hosted at https://www.appliancefactoryparts.com/content/pdfs/141608-1.pdf. It is a partial copy.
- **Speed guide:**
  - Speed 2 "SLOW MIXING": "...mix and knead yeast dough."
  - Speed 10 "FAST WHIPPING."
  - "With cakes, for example, beating time may be half as long as with other stand mixers."
- **Beater-to-bowl clearance:**
  1. Turn speed control to OFF and unplug.
  2. Tilt the motor head back.
  3. "Turn screw (A) SLIGHTLY counterclockwise (left) to raise the Flat Beater or clockwise (right) to lower the Flat Beater."
  4. Adjust so the beater "just clears the surface of the bowl."
  5. If over-adjusted, "you may not be able to lock the motor head."
- **US warranty:** "One-year limited warranty from date of purchase" for the 50 US states, DC, Canada and Puerto Rico. In the US and Canada, KitchenAid will pay for "Hassle-free replacement of your stand mixer." It does not cover use other than "normal single family home use."

### International 5K45SS (Classic) / 5KSM150PS (Artisan) "Tilt-Head Stand Mixers" instructions
- Source: https://c.searspartsdirect.com/doc/L0710375. The same text appears at https://wch.thegoodguys.com.au/dxdam/pdf/50038602_kitchenaid.pdf. This is the EU/AU edition, with metric-first units.
- "Do not exceed Speed 2 when preparing yeast doughs as this may cause damage to the mixer."
- "Use Speed 2 to mix or knead yeast doughs. Use of any other speed creates high potential for unit failure."
- **Flour limits:**
  - "NEVER use recipes calling for more than 0.87 kg (7 cups) all-purpose flour or 0.81 kg (6 cups) whole wheat flour when making dough with a 4,25 liter (4.5 qrt) tilt-head mixer."
  - For the 5-qt tilt-head the limit is "1.00 kg (8 cups) all-purpose flour or 0.81 kg (6 cups) whole wheat flour."
- **Knead time:** "knead on Speed 2 for 2 minutes, or until dough is smooth and elastic" (after the dough clings to the hook).
- The international warranty differs: 5 years for the Artisan and 2 years for the Classic in Europe/AU/NZ. **Do not use this for US readers.**

### Artisan Mini KSM3300 series instructions
- Source: W11091673C, hosted by QVC at https://www.qvc.com/footers/cd/pdf/K48363_Manual.pdf
- Covers KSM3306, KSM3311, KSM3316 and KSM3317.
- "Do not exceed Speed 2 when preparing yeast doughs as this may cause damage to the Stand Mixer."
- "Do not use Speed 1 to mix or knead yeast doughs."
- Clearance: same screw directions as the KSM150 manual. Over-adjusting means "the bowl lock lever may not lock into place."
- **Warranty (US/DC/PR/Canada):** "One Year Full Warranty from date of purchase," with Hassle-Free Replacement in the first year, at 1-800-541-6390.
- Overheating: "allow it to cool for 30 minutes" if it won't start.

### Dime test (official KitchenAid help page)
- Source: https://producthelp.kitchenaid.com/Countertop_Appliances/Stand_Mixers/Product_Info/Stand_Mixer_Assembly_and_Operation/Adjust_the_Beater_to_Bowl_Clearance. Read 2026-11-27; the page shows no date.
- **Steps:**
  1. Put a US dime in the empty **stainless** bowl.
  2. Attach the flat beater and run at Speed 1.
- **Reading the result:**
  - Correct: the beater grazes the dime and moves it "roughly 1/2 to 1 inch per sweep."
  - Too low: it drags the dime continuously.
  - Too high: it never touches the dime.
- **Turning the screw:** the tilt-head screw is in the hinge neck. Clockwise lowers the beater; counterclockwise raises it. Turn "no more than 1/8 to 1/4 of a turn" at a time. The whole range is ½ turn.
- **Limits:**
  - Never dime-test glass or ceramic bowls.
  - Pre-1980 mixers have no external screw.
- **Conflicts between sources:**
  - KitchenAid's own pinch-of-help page reportedly says "about a quarter inch" per pass (snippet; kitchenaid.com blocked, **UNVERIFIED**).
  - ATK (https://www.americastestkitchen.com/articles/2728-keep-your-kitchenaid-mixer-running-smoothly-with-this-simple-fix, snippet) says ¼–½ inch, and gives the screw direction the opposite way.
  - Use the manufacturer's direction.

### Kneading (help page)
- Source: https://producthelp.kitchenaid.com/Countertop_Appliances/Stand_Mixers/Tilt_Head_Mixer/Mixer_Performance/Kneading_Times_and_Speeds_-_Stand_Mixer
- "When kneading dough, we recommend using speed 2."
- Judge by texture: dough should be smooth, slightly tacky and elastic, and spring back. Over-kneading makes dough tough.
