## 2. Expert / published sources
Consumer Reports
- "How to Find the Best Dog Bed", Angela Lashbrook, published Jan 14, 2022, no update date. https://www.consumerreports.org/home-garden/how-to-find-the-best-dog-bed-a9052035557/
  - Egg-crate and memory foam both described as supportive for tender joints. Memory foam "tends to trap heat" (quoted expert Forsyth); egg crate stays cooler via airflow; memory foam called a good cold-weather option.
  - Elevated beds reduce pressure points but dog must be able to get in/out. Suggests a machine-washable/removable cover; a "washable, waterproof cover" for older dogs with bladder weakness (Boyd).
  - Does not address cold floors directly. Sizing advice is thin (puppies can grow into bigger bed; small dogs need low entry).
