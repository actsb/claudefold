## 5. Counterfeit, look-alike and safety issues
Jump starters (lithium):
- CPSC warning #26-768, Sept 17, 2026 [FETCHED 10-28]: Hererod PW40 and EW40 jump starters/power banks, 16 fire reports (7 with property damage), about 12,500 sold Aug 2024-Aug 2026; the maker has not responded to CPSC. CPSC says stop using and dispose via hazardous-waste channels. CPSC names the sales channels as Amazon.com, CarKart.com and eBay.com; if the writer names the channels, keep it to the CPSC wording and do not add price or rating. https://www.cpsc.gov/Warnings/2026/CPSC-Urges-Consumers-to-Stop-Using-Hererod-Jump-Starters-Power-Banks-Immediately-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-and-Death-from-Fire-Hazard
- CPSC warning #26-087, Nov 14, 2025 [FETCHED 10-28]: HALO Bolt ACDC 58830 portable chargers (can jump a car) made Dec 2019 or earlier; importer refused a recall; sold at Best Buy, QVC and online. Shows that a known brand is not proof of safety. https://www.cpsc.gov/Warnings/2026/CPSC-Warns-Consumers-to-Stop-Using-HALO-Bolt-ACDC-58830-Portable-Chargers-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Fire-and-Burns
- Counterfeit NOCO or Stanley specifically: no documented case found; NOCO has no public fake-spotting guide that surfaced. Octopart warns generally that non-authorized sellers carry mislabeled/counterfeit/no-warranty risk. UNVERIFIED: do not say "fake NOCOs are common". Safe advice: buy from the brand store or a big-box retailer, check the warranty is valid for new units only (a Slickdeals commenter reported a Genius charger warranty excluded "used-like new"; anecdote), and a CPSC/SaferProducts.gov search before buying an unfamiliar brand.
- Peak-amp numbers are marketing; the cold-weather issue is real for lithium (use inside the cabin when possible; NBC's guide says water freezes in a trunk). Harbor Freight Viking/Cen-Tech peak figures are likewise "peak".
Traction mats:
- No counterfeit or CPSC action on traction mats found. Look-alike risk is real in the market: "Escaper Buddy"-style mats sell under other names (e.g. FIREBUG) and prices for the genuine MAXSA 20333 range from $129.99 (Home Depot snippet) to $249.99 (Tractor Supply snippet). Check model number (20333 standard, 48 in. x 14.1 in.), seller and warranty (reported 90-day). UNVERIFIED that any look-alike is a copy of MAXSA's design.
- The Drive (2023): foldable mats are less durable than fixed boards; UV can make plastic brittle.
