## 3. Official Rubbermaid instructions
- Brilliance use and care (plastic line): https://help.rubbermaid.com/s/article/Use-Care-Instructions-Rubbermaid-Brilliance-Product-Line?language=en_US. Per search summary (page not opened): open the latches to vent steam when microwaving; reheat only (food at 165 F, not held more than 30 seconds, since cooking is not intended); remove the lid after microwaving to protect the wiper seal; no plastic wrap inside; avoid reheating very salty, oily, fatty or sugary foods; dishwasher safe but keep clear of the heating element; not for conventional or convection oven, browning element or stovetop.
- Glass line care (different product): https://help.rubbermaid.com/s/article/Use-Care-Instructions-Rubbermaid-Glass-Brilliance. Lids go on the dishwasher top rack. Retailer copy for glass also says leave freezer headspace for expanding liquids and never microwave empty. Do not apply glass oven rules to the Tritan set.
- Freezer: retailer listings call the plastic set freezer safe; the official freezer wording was not retrieved. UNVERIFIED. Rubbermaid Consumer Care (via a retailer reply) cautioned that extreme temperature swings, such as hot food straight from the stove or freezer to microwave, can affect the plastic over time.
- Product page for this set: https://www.rubbermaid.com/food-storage/everyday-use-containers/brilliance/brilliance-food-storage-containers-set/SP_2551585.html (leak-proof claim: airtight seals and secure latches).
- Top-rack: stated for the plastic set in a Walmart listing; confirm on the official page.
- Guarantee/warranty: https://www.rubbermaid.com/support?cfid=rubbermaid-warranty-information. Per search summary, Brilliance and EasyFindLids carry a satisfaction guarantee: claim within 120 days of purchase, bought from Rubbermaid or an authorized U.S. seller, original purchaser only, via Contact Us form with proof of purchase (photos may be requested); terms can change without notice; listed effective March 6, 2024. Conflict: a Rubbermaid product page lists "lifetime warranty" for a Brilliance kit, while the Help Center says consumer products typically carry 1 to 10 year limited warranties (https://help.rubbermaid.com/s/article/Does-my-product-have-a-warranty?language=en_US). Treat warranty length as unresolved.
