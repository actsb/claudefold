## 4. Subscriptions and running cost

* **Alexa+**: $19.99/month, included at no extra cost with Prime; non-Prime customers get a limited free text chat tier. [Official] https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/alexa-plus-available-free-prime-members-us (page dated 09/01/26, read 2026-11-06). Prime $14.99/month or $139/year: not on an Amazon page I could read (503); sources are aggregators that say the price is unchanged since Feb 2022 (UNVERIFIED as official, check amazon.com/prime). Best Buy's Echo listing says Alexa+ access comes with the Amazon account used at setup.
* 36-month math (assistant only, hardware excluded): Alexa+ without Prime **$719.64**; Prime annual **$417** (3 x $139), Prime monthly $539.64; with Prime already owned, $0 extra.
* **Google Home Premium**: Standard $10/month or $100/year (Gemini Live, Ask Home, 30 days video history); Advanced $20/month or $200/year (adds AI event descriptions, Home Brief, searchable video history); Google AI Pro includes Standard, AI Ultra includes Advanced. [Official] Google blog 2025-10-01 https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/google-nest/googe-home-premium-google-ai-pro-subscription/ . Google's own store page for Premium is not readable (truncated); prices re-confirmed by The Next Web 2026-06-17. The Home Speaker works free for smart-home control, reminders and quick answers; Gemini Live needs Premium. A 6-month Premium trial comes with the speaker (TNW, 2026-06-17); one source ties it to purchases before Sept 30, so whether a speaker bought now still gets it is UNVERIFIED.
* 36 months of Premium Standard: **$360** monthly or **$300** annual ($0 if you never subscribe); Advanced **$720** monthly or **$600** annual. With a 6-month trial and monthly billing, Standard = 30 x $10 = $300.
* **Apple**: no subscription found for HomePod mini 2 ($0). Apple's release says Siri AI is "coming in a future software update", needs an Apple Intelligence iPhone/iPad, "some server-based features have daily usage limits, and Apple says expanded access will cost a fee in the future". A fee is possible, amount unknown. [Official] Apple newsroom 2026-10-13.
* **Sonos**: $0; Sonos 27 is a free update (per the blueprint, [Official] 2026-09-08).
* **Electricity**: Sonos publishes idle watts, 120 V: Era 100 **1.98 W**, Era 100 SL **1.62 W** ([Official] https://support.sonos.com/en/article/sonos-power-consumption-while-idle, no date, (c) 2026). EIA US average residential price **18.16 cents/kWh, August 2026** (preliminary) [Official] https://www.eia.gov/electricity/monthly/epm_table_grapher.php?t=epmt_5_6_a . Computed: Era 100 = 17.3 kWh/yr, about **$3.15/yr** ($9.45 over 3 years); Era 100 SL = 14.2 kWh/yr, about **$2.58/yr** ($7.73). Echo Dot Max, Echo Studio, Google Home Speaker, HomePod mini 2: **no official idle watts found, UNVERIFIED**. Older Echo models: Amazon lists about 2.2-3.5 W networked standby (Echo Plus 2nd gen 2.23 W, Echo Show 5 3.47 W, other models), so an Echo at 2-4 W would cost about $3-6/yr (inference, not a Dot Max or Studio measurement). The only HomePod mini number is a 0.27 W Medium bench test of the original mini (Weak).

* Queried the SaferProducts API (2024-01-01 on) for Sonos, Amazon Echo, HomePod, Google Nest, Alexa, Apple as product names and for Sonos, Google as manufacturers: **0 results**. (The API shows recalls to Nov 5, 2026, newer than the CSV.)
* cpsc.gov web searches found only older items: Nest Protect smoke alarm (2014), Beats Pill XL (2015), VIVO Sonos Play:5 wall mounts (2019). Cambridge Audio Yoyo (M) recall 26-731 is not one of our picks.
* `brand/pages/recall-check.html` (generated from the SaferProducts API, 228 recalls since Jan 2025, categories heating, batteries, car, kitchen, cleaning, bathroom) has **no entry for these brands** and does not cover smart speakers. Verdict for the post: no open recall for any pick, as of the Nov 5, 2026 API data. Not a guarantee for recalls posted after that.
