**Rules applied:** no amazon.com page was opened. All Amazon prices, ratings and review counts are left out, including past lows that were Amazon-only.

**How each price was obtained**
- **FETCHED** means I loaded the page myself on 2026-11-26.
- **SNIPPET** means I saw the price only in a search-result excerpt.
- **ARTICLE** means a deal article reported the price.

**Access limits**
- **Retailer pages:** Best Buy returned 503 and Walmart showed a bot wall, so their prices come only from snippets or articles.
- **Other blocked pages:** electronics.sony.com, CNN Underscored (HTTP 451), Gizmochina and PhoneArena (403) could not be fetched.

**UNVERIFIED** marks a figure that rests on one undated or indirect source, or that sources disagree on.

## (b) Recalls and safety notices
- **CPSC search:** found no 2025–2026 CPSC recall of earbuds or charging cases from Apple, Beats, Sony, Bose, Samsung, Google, Anker/Soundcore, EarFun, Nothing, Technics/Panasonic or Shokz. This is based on searches and is not a full database export.
- **Bose S1 Pro / S1 Pro+ speaker battery packs:** recalled. These are speaker batteries, not earbuds.
  - CPSC recall 27-065, about 634,759 units in the US plus about 35,400 in Canada. Sold Dec 2017 through Jul 2026. The remedy is a free replacement.
  - The CPSC/news date is Nov 6, 2026. The local recall-check.html says "Recalled Nov 5, 2026", so the dates differ by one day.
- **Anker power banks:** two recalls, neither concerning earbuds.
  - PowerCore 10000 A1263, about 1.158M units (Jun 12, 2025).
  - A1647, A1652, A1257, A1681 and A1689, about 481k units (Sept 2025).
- **Grep of /home/user/claudefold/brand/pages/recall-check.html** (one long line, so the counts are lines):
  - Bose: 1 match, the S1 Pro battery-pack recall card ("Recalled Nov 5, 2026"). It is not earbuds.
  - Anker: 1 match, the "More than one million Anker power banks recalled", PowerCore 10000 model A1263 (Recalled Jun 12, 2025, sold Jun 2016–Dec 2022). It is not earbuds.
  - Sony: 1 match, but only inside an internal article link title ("Best Wireless Earbuds of 2026: AirPods 5 & Pro 3 vs Sony, Bose, Galaxy Buds and Pixel Buds"). There is no Sony recall.
  - Apple, Samsung, Google, Soundcore, EarFun, Nothing, Technics, Beats, Shokz: 0 matches.

## (c) When electronics discounts peak
- **Adobe Digital Insights forecast (news.adobe.com, Sept 28, 2026, FETCHED):**
  - Electronics discounts are expected to peak at 30% off list (30.1% in 2025), with the deepest cuts on electronics and computers on **Cyber Monday, Nov 30**.
  - Cyber Monday: $15.1B (+6.2%), the biggest day.
  - Black Friday: $12.9B (+9.2%), growing faster and expected to have the best discounts on TVs, apparel and appliances.
  - Thanksgiving: $6.9B (+8.5%).
- **Adobe update via The National News Desk / WKRC Local 12 (2026-11-25, FETCHED):**
  - Electronics peak on Cyber Monday at up to 30% off, with computers up to 23%.
