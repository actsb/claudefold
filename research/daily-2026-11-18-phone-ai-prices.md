## 5. Returns, price match, unlocked notes

- **Google Store:** 15 calendar days after delivery. The 2025 holiday extension (purchases Nov 11 to Dec 31, returnable to Jan 15, 2026) is documented, but a 2026 extension is **UNVERIFIED**. **Price match:** if the Google Store or Amazon (sold by Amazon), B&H, Best Buy, Signifi, Target or Walmart lists the same model, color and storage for less during your return window, Google refunds the difference. Conditions: **unlocked phones only**, live public link required, no bundles, coupons, open-box or clearance, and the refund is capped at your post-trade-in balance. https://support.google.com/store/answer/9631873?hl=en (fetched) ; returns https://support.google.com/store/answer/2411741
- **Samsung.com:** 15 days from delivery, plus 15 days to ship it back. Return shipping is free and S-series phones are exempt from restocking fees. Damage must be reported within 5 days. **Price match** against other retailers (not Costco or Sam's Club) is allowed before delivery or within the 15-day window, one per order. https://order-help.us.samsung.com/hc/en-us/articles/360057028614 ; https://order-help.us.samsung.com/hc/en-us/articles/360055936774-Does-Samsung-offer-price-matching
- **Target (official 2026 fact sheet):** The Holiday Price Match runs **Nov 1 to Dec 24, 2026** and covers **Target's own lower price only** (Target no longer matches competitors). Holiday returns: purchases Nov 1 to Dec 24, 2026; **mobile phones get 14 days counted from Dec 26, so the deadline is Jan 8, 2027, and only if unlocked or prepaid**. https://corporate.target.com/press/fact-sheet/2026/11/holiday-shopping-information (fetched)
- **Best Buy:** standard 15 days (60 for paid members), per third-party guides. A 2026 holiday window (Oct 27 to Dec 31, returnable to Jan 14, 2027) is reported only by third parties, so **UNVERIFIED**. Activatable devices (phones) have historically been **excluded** from the holiday extension. Best Buy price-match terms for 2026 are **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Unlocked vs carrier:** Google's price match covers unlocked phones only. Target's phone return requires an unlocked or prepaid phone. Carrier deals lock the phone and tie credits to the line for 24 to 36 months. Target also sells refurbished "AT&T Only" S26 units, so avoid those if you want unlocked.

## 7. Recalls and battery programs

- **CPSC:** I found **no** 2024 to 2026 CPSC recall of a Google Pixel or Samsung Galaxy phone, charger or battery. (The most recent Samsung phone CPSC recall is the Galaxy Note 7 in 2016.) The nearest relevant item is a third-party accessory recall: **Casely Power Pods 5000mAh MagSafe power bank, model E33A, about 429,200 units, first recalled Apr 17, 2025 and re-announced Apr 16, 2026 (CPSC No. 26-418) after a reported death.** Search results did not return a direct cpsc.gov record. Recalls listing: https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls
- **Pixel 6a Battery Performance Program (non-CPSC):** a mandatory update (July 8, 2025) cut capacity and charging on at-risk units because of overheating risk. Remedy was a free battery, $100 cash or $150 Store credit. Reports say it ended in July 2026 (**UNVERIFIED** closure). https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/16340779?hl=en
- **Pixel 4a battery program (non-CPSC):** Jan 2025 update for overheating risk, with free replacement or cash. Current status **UNVERIFIED**.
- Pixel 10 series: Google's "Battery Health Assistance" may throttle capacity over time (Android Authority, 2025), and there were April 2026 reports of idle drain. No recall.
