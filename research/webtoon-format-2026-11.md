# Webtoon format for Verdict Picks comics (research, 2026-11-23)

Owner request (2026-11-23): before introducing Amazon products, study the best webtoons and tell each product's story as a webtoon, starting with that day's post. We follow the format's conventions, never a specific series (no characters, art, names or plots from existing works). Our cast is original: Vera (the existing illustrated host), Sam and Jo (shoppers), a beagle-type dog.

## What the research found (sources)
- **Canvas.** WEBTOON Canvas images are at most 800 px wide and 1280 px tall each, 2 MB each, JPG or PNG; readers are mostly on phones ([Before-You-Publish Checklist](https://webtoons-static.pstatic.net/creator101/en/pdf/Before-you-publish-Checklist-Updated.pdf), [Creators Resource Handbook](https://webtoons-static.pstatic.net/creator101/en/pdf/Creators-Resource-Handbook-Updated.pdf)). We render 800-wide slices of at most 1280, at 2x, as WebP (about a quarter of the PNG size).
- **Pacing.** WEBTOON editors suggest at least 200 px between panels, 600–1000 px for a change of place or time, no more than two panels on screen at once, and wide "impact panels" for key moments (Handbook). The scroll is the reveal: suspense comes from the blank space before the surprise (Rachel Smythe on *Lore Olympus*, [The Spinoff, 2021](https://thespinoff.co.nz/culture/25-11-2021/lore-olympus-behind-the-scenes-of-a-phenomenally-successful-online-comic)); scrolling speed sets the pace (Presser et al. 2021; Kim 2017). One action reads best as three beats: anticipation, action, result ([Clip Studio](https://tips.clip-studio.com/en-us/articles/4143)).
- **Endings.** Some series end on a new conflict (cliffhanger), others on a resolved, quiet beat (Hwang 2024, [KoreaScience](https://koreascience.kr/article/JAKO202435143254292.page)). Ours ends on the decision and hands off to the picks below.
- **Lettering.** At most three balloons per panel, one or two sentences each; 12–30 px text at 800 px width, and if the reader squints it is too small (Handbook). Read-first balloons sit higher; tails do not cross ([Clip Studio](https://tips.clip-studio.com/en-us/articles/3751)). We use 28 px balloons and 25 px captions.
- **Structure.** 기승전결: setup, development, turn, resolution. Our short episode: establishing scene → the problem → turn (impact panel after a wide gap) → the facts → the decision → a final tip.
- **Branded comics.** Readers accept a product message that is tied to the story and its characters; they reject soulless promo with no link to the story (Lee 2017, [KOSCAS](https://koreascience.kr/article/JAKO201732060820493.pdf)). Naver reportedly stopped in-story PPL in 2020 after complaints (unverified, search summaries only).
- **Disclosure (FTC).** Labels go before the content and close to it, in plain words; a visual needs a visual disclosure ([Native Advertising Guide](https://ftc.gov/tips-advice/business-center/guidance/native-advertising-guide-businesses), [16 CFR 255.0](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/255.0)). An obviously fictional dramatization is not an endorsement, but people shown as "actual consumers" must be real or clearly disclosed as not (16 CFR 255.2(c)). Korea's KFTC reportedly requires a virtual-person label on ads using virtual characters from 2026-06-01 (unverified).

## Our rules (daily/README.md section 4, "Comic before the picks")
1. One comic per new guide, `posts/<dir>/webtoon.json`, 10–14 panels, placed with `<!--TOON:<id>-->` just before the verdict.
2. The story is a reader's problem; the product facts drive the turn. Every number, claim and quote in a balloon or caption comes from the post with its source (name the source in a caption or on the board).
3. Characters never claim to have used or tested a product, never give results, and are never presented as real customers. The title card and end card say the characters and scenes are fictional; the label above the comic says it is not sponsored and that the Amazon links further down are paid links.
4. Props are generic and unbranded (a robot vacuum, a heater, a hand warmer). Never draw a specific product as if it were the product; product names may appear in text only.
5. No prices, ratings or review counts from Amazon in the comic; no Amazon images.
