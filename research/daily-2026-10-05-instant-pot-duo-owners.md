## Failure modes / owner pain points (from how-to sites, not owner reviews; undated)
- Sealing ring odor: ring absorbs smells; remedy = soak in diluted vinegar, wash, air dry; owners keep separate rings for savory/sweet. https://www.paintthekitchenred.com/16-reasons-why-instant-pot-not-sealing/ and https://gourmetdoneskinny.com/common-instant-pot-problems-and-how-to-fix-them/ (undated). One owner anecdote: recurring failure to seal led them to buy an 8 qt (UNVERIFIED, from search summary).
- Burn / OvHT: same overheat safety shutdown; causes = too little liquid, thick sauces, stuck food, dirty ring/valve; stainless pot (304 stainless, no nonstick coating) needs deglazing; optional ceramic nonstick pot sold separately. https://instantpoteats.com/instant-pot-burn-message/ , https://kristineskitchenblog.com/instant-pot-burn/ , https://www.paintthekitchenred.com/instant-pot-burn-message/
- Lettered errors C1/C2/C6/C6L/C7/PrSE: sensor or heating faults; if they recur after power-cycle, contact support. https://dialanddish.com/multi-cookers/instant-pot-error-codes , https://www.ifixit.com/Wiki/Instant_Pot_IP-DUO80_V2_Troubleshooting
- Lifespan: no verified data found. Warranty is 1 year, only from authorized retailers. https://instantpot.com/pages/product-warranties
- Lid-locked-shut complaint ("holds food hostage ~1 in 4 uses... no manual release") was attributed to the Duo CRISP by a search summary, source retailer/date unknown: UNVERIFIED, do not quote.

## Size / variant facts
- Duo 6-qt: 1000 W, 14 programs, high pressure 10.2-11.6 psi, low 5.8-7.2 psi, 120 V (spec snippet; Newegg listing https://www.newegg.com/instant-pot-ip-duo60-6-quarts-electric-pressure-cooker/p/19U-0006-00003 and manual https://files.plytix.com/api/v1.1/file/public_files/pim/assets/27/1d/e1/63/63e11d272c0736000157d3a9/texts/ca/e8/ae/65/65aee8ca6bdc3a174a8935b1/113-0059-01_IB_DuoV5_MANUAL_US_FINAL-08-15-2023_LoRes.pdf , unopened). Sources: https://instantpoteats.com/instant-pot-duo-vs-duo-plus/ , https://mrkitchenadviser.com/instant-pot-pro-vs-duo/ .
