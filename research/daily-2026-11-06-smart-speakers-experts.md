## 3. Makers' official privacy, retention and settings pages

### 3.1 Amazon (Echo / Alexa+)

- **Alexa, Echo Devices, and Your Privacy**, https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GVP69FUJ48X9DK8V . Read 2026-11-06. The page shows no "last updated" date (footer: "© 1996-2026"). Quotes:
  - Review/delete: "You can review and delete Alexa voice recordings, text transcripts of those recordings, and typed requests to Alexa associated with your Amazon account by visiting More > Alexa Privacy in the Alexa app and Echo Show devices or https://www.amazon.com/alexaprivacysettings."
  - Auto-delete: "you can choose to automatically delete your voice recordings, text transcripts, and typed requests to Alexa after a specified period of time (3 or 18 months), or you can choose to not save any voice recordings."
  - Don't save: "we will automatically delete your voice recordings after we process your requests and automatically delete all existing voice recordings associated with your account, but your text transcripts of those recordings and any typed requests will be retained for 30 days after your last interaction with Alexa in a given chat, after they will automatically be deleted."
  - Voice ID: "If you choose not to save any voice recordings, voice IDs associated with your account will be deleted and voice ID will not work."
  - Mic off: "Echo devices are equipped with a microphones enable/disable button. When the button is pressed, the power to the microphones is disconnected and a dedicated red light is illuminated. When the microphones are turned off, you cannot interact with Alexa using voice, as your device cannot record and stream audio to the cloud, even when you say your chosen wake word."
  - Cloud: "Your audio is transcribed in the cloud and then automatically deleted" (in the Alexa.com chat voice answer).
- **Delete Voice and Text Chat History in the Alexa App** https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GVLEU55A9NDZRWBU : not readable (503). A search snippet says voice requests cannot be used to delete Alexa+ history; deletion must be in the app or on the web. [Weak]
- **Alexa+ price**, Amazon's own newsroom: https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/alexa-plus-available-free-prime-members-us , footer "09/01/26", read 2026-11-06. "Alexa+ is now available in the U.S. for $19.99 per month and included at no additional cost to Prime members." Free tier: "A free tier lets non-Prime members in the U.S. try the Alexa+ chat experience via Alexa.com and the Alexa app." Amazon's own alexaplus product page and Alexa+ activation help page returned 503; a search snippet of them says Echo access needs Prime or a Standard subscription and that "there's no free experience for Alexa+ on devices (Echo and Fire TV)." [Weak, snippet] Keep the guide's wording to "free chat tier on Alexa.com and the app."

### 3.2 Apple (HomePod / Siri)

- **Ask Siri, Dictation & Privacy**, https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/ask-siri-dictation/ , page ends "2026-10-26", read 2026-11-06. "Your audio data is not stored by Apple unless you opt in to Improve Siri and Dictation or Improve Siri & Apple Intelligence." If you opt in, "Apple will collect, store, and review additional data, including audio and text of what you say"; Apple "may retain and use this data for up to two years."
- **Change Siri settings on HomePod**, https://support.apple.com/guide/homepod/change-siri-settings-fk4zlkp1c5mq/homepod , read 2026-11-06 (guide is "for HomePod software version 27.2 or later"). Delete: Home app > tap the HomePod > Settings > "Siri History" > "Delete Siri History." Turn off: Settings > Siri section > "Listen For 'Siri' or 'Hey Siri,' then turn off Siri for the HomePod" and turn off "Touch and Hold for Siri." Retention: "your request history is associated with a random identifier for 6 months"; afterward dissociated and possibly kept up to 2 years. This contradicts Mozilla's "no auto-delete" snippet; use Apple's page.
- **HomePod mini page**, https://www.apple.com/homepod-mini/ , read 2026-11-06 (this is the product page for the current lineup; it shows gen-2 copy). "Siri only listens for 'Hey Siri' or 'Siri' so you can speak freely knowing nothing you say is sent out of your home until you activate Siri with a touch, or HomePod mini hears the magic words... your request is associated with a random identifier, not your Apple Account." Siri AI: "Coming in a future software update."
- HomePod mini has **no physical mic switch** that I could find on Apple's page; muting is by voice or the Home-app setting above. UNVERIFIED that no switch exists on gen 2.
- **Apple newsroom** https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-announces-the-next-generation-of-homepod-mini/ , dated Oct 13, 2026: pre-order in "pink, green, blue, white, and midnight," availability "Friday, October 30"; "Siri AI, coming to HomePod mini 2 in a future software update." The $149 price is in the search snippet, not read in the page text I extracted.

### 3.3 Google (Google Home Speaker / Gemini for Home)

- **Manage and delete your Gemini for Home activity**, https://support.google.com/googlehome/answer/16613583 (also at /googlenest/answer/16613583), no page date (footer "©2026 Google"), read 2026-11-06. "By default, your activity saves for 18 months"; "By default, your Home History older than 18 months is auto-deleted." Auto-delete can be off or 3 or 36 months. Delete at myactivity.google.com/product/home: all activity, last hour/day, custom range, a day, or one item. "When you enable Voice Match and turn on Home History, Google stores your Gemini for Home activity in your Google Account," and it is used to improve services including training generative AI models. ### 3.4 Sonos

- **Privacy Statement**, https://www.sonos.com/en-us/legal/privacy , "Last update: September, 2026", read 2026-11-06.
  - Wake word: "the voice-enabled Sonos Product continuously buffers... locally, without transmitting or storing any information, unless it detects the wake word or phrase ('Hey Sonos' or 'Alexa')."
  - Classic Sonos Voice: "your voice command is processed on your device. No audio is saved, transmitted to Sonos, or sent to the cloud." Also "Sonos does not create, process or store transcripts of any audio recordings."
  - Sonos 27voice (optional AI upgrade): "your voice command is transmitted to Sonos cloud infrastructure for AI-assisted processing"; it collects "voice recordings... Transcripts... AI-inferred command intent." The page states no retention period for 27voice in the portion I read (UNVERIFIED; there is a retention section the extracted text did not show).
  - Alexa on Sonos: "the recording of your voice command is then sent to the voice partner you have authorized to receive such recording (for example, Amazon). Sonos does not retain a copy or transcript of your voice recording." So Alexa-on-Era-100 retention is Amazon's (3.1).
- **Era 100 mic switch** (Sonos US shop page, read 2026-11-06): "Toggle off this switch on the back of the product to disconnect all power to the microphone hardware." Mic array "uses advanced beamforming and multichannel echo cancellation for quick and accurate voice control and Trueplay tuning."
- **Era 100 SL: no mic** (Sonos blog, https://www.sonos.com/en-us/blog/guide-to-era-100-sl , read 2026-11-06): "Era 100 SL takes the same finely tuned stereo sound and rich bass... but without internal microphones." "The only real difference between these two speakers is that Era 100 SL does not contain a microphone and therefore does not have voice control capabilities." Trueplay on SL needs iOS, "as Android devices require a microphone within the speaker." (Blueprint's TechRadar/Tom's Guide quote "There are no microphones on it at all" is a reviewer quote; use the Sonos wording above for the official claim.)
- Alexa+ on Sonos Era 100: **UNVERIFIED**; no source found.
