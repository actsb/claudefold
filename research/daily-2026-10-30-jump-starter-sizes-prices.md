## 3. Warranty, registration, returns

- NOCO warranty: 1 year on the GB40 per NOCO's spec sheet snippet ("1-year hassle-free warranty") and Micro Center ("1 Year Limited") and O'Reilly (1 Year Limited). NOCO's GB150 guide also says one year from the original retail purchase date. Best Buy lists 1 year on the GB50. Not fetched from NOCO directly (403). https://no.co/gb40/specs, https://no.co/warranty-returns (both unfetchable; term per search summaries, 2026-10-30). Older forum claims of 3 years are not reliable.
- Registration: NOCO support says registering adds the product to your account and activates the limited warranty ("My Products" at no.co/support). One older NOCO manual says registration is "recommended". Treat registration as required to be safe. A third-party claim of a 30-day deadline is UNVERIFIED.
- Claims: keep proof of purchase and the serial number. Under the written warranty, the buyer pays shipping to NOCO. It covers the original purchaser only. Trustpilot reviews describe slow claims (batteryguys, Trustpilot; anecdotal).
- GOOLOO warranty: the store page says "up to 30 months" (likely 18 months plus a registration extension, UNVERIFIED). A 9to5Toys post from 2026-08-06 says 18 months is included. Returns: 30-day money-back guarantee on the GOOLOO store (fetched 2026-10-30).
- Retailer return windows (from roundups, UNVERIFIED, check each store before quoting):
  - Walmart: 90 days on most items (older roundup).
  - Target: 90 days (CBS News).
  - Home Depot and Lowe's: 90 days general, with 30 days on electronics per one roundup.
  - Best Buy: 15 days standard per one cheat sheet, 30 days per an older roundup. The holiday window is purchases from Oct 27 to Jan 2 with returns through Jan 14, 2027 per an aggregator, which makes it low-confidence.

## 5. When Amazon is not the cheapest (for the guide, no Amazon prices)

Supported by what I found:
- The NOCO US store, Walmart, Home Depot, O'Reilly, Bass Pro and Best Buy all show roughly the same maker list price ($99.95/$99.99 GB40, $149.95 GB50, $199.95/$199.99 GB70). In normal weeks, there is no price advantage to any one of them, so choose by return window and warranty ease.
- Auto-parts chains (O'Reilly, AutoZone, Advance) let you pick up same day and test the unit in store; that is the value, not price.
- NOCO's own store is the cleanest path for registration and warranty claims.
- The GOOLOO store's "$349.98" compare-at figure is inflated; the Best Buy and Walmart listings may undercut it, but those are marketplace sellers, so counterfeit risk and warranty route are worth flagging (inference).
- Best Buy's holiday return extension and Target's, if confirmed, can beat the standard 30-day window; verify first.

- Takeaway: a jump starter ($100-$200) pays back after about one or two paid roadside jumps; cables ($15-$30) are cheapest but need another vehicle.

- NOCO: no CPSC recall found. UNVERIFIED as a full database check (the cpsc.gov search page only returned page one).
- GOOLOO: no CPSC recall found. A UK notice from Electrical Safety First covers a GOOLOO/VTOMAN jump starter (model JS-378B / X7), June 2024. That is UK only, not CPSC. https://www.electricalsafetyfirst.org.uk/product-recalls/2024/06/gooloo-vtoman-jump-starter-power-bank
