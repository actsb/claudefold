## A. The seven reviewed candidates

### A1. Levoit Core 300-P
- Praise: quiet in sleep mode, compact. Best Buy listing for the plain Core 300 shows an AI summary: noticeable air-quality improvement, moderate noise, small size; concerns about filter life and coverage (Best Buy reviews page, https://bestbuy.com/site/reviews/levoit-core-300-air-purifier-white/6462880?page=6, n/d). The Walmart Core 300-RAC-P (gray) listing summary: simple controls, bedroom-suited, loudest setting is loud (https://www.walmart.com/ip/2552598681, n/d). Ratings/counts for these pages were surfaced by search but are not needed; UNVERIFIED which model they cover.
- Noise: a Target Q&A answer says very quiet but one owner could not sleep with it in the bedroom; Airpurifierfirst measured 40.7 to 64.9 dBA vs Levoit's 24-50 dB claim (https://www.target.com/p/levoit-core-300-true-hepa-air-purifier-white/-/A-81910071; https://www.airpurifierfirst.com/comparison/levoit-lv-h132-vs-levoit-core-300/; n/d).
- Failure modes (iFixit, n/d): funky filter smell, rattle from fan area, unit not turning on after a time; "HEPA" wording was dropped from the description after buyers found the filter did not meet HEPA standards (https://www.ifixit.com/Device/Levoit_Core_300-RAC).
- Filters: replace every 6-8 months per Chewy product Q&A (https://www.chewy.com/levoit-core-300-p-original/dp/355646, n/d); clean the pre-filter dry every 2-4 weeks (same). Replacement filter list prices on iFixit: $35.99 (pet/toxin) and $39.99 (smoke) (n/d; may be stale).
- Variant that matters: Core 300 vs 300-P vs 300S/300S-P are separate SKUs with different prices; Walmart sells a "Core 300-RAC-P" gray SKU. Filter part numbers differ (Core300-RF vs Core300-P-RF).
- Counterfeit/fulfilment: UNVERIFIED in this run (see 2026-09-27 brief).

### A2. O-Cedar EasyWring spin mop
- Praise (Walmart reviews as mirrored on Sam's Club, 13,073 reviews listed; some from 2026): easy to use, dry floors in about 30 minutes, helps owners with weak hands (https://www.samsclub.com/reviews/product/4178599815, accessed 2026-10-09).
- Complaints: pedal/wringing mechanism breaks (reports of failure after 3 uses, about a month, and a repeat buyer on a third unit); O-Cedar replies tell owners to contact it via the website, and one reply advises stepping the pedal up to 10 times (Sam's Club mirror pages 217, 370, 391, 32; dates n/d). Handle slides down; head flips; spinner leaves mop damp; handle short for a 5-ft owner (pages 338, 171, 192, n/d).
- One reviewer blames leaving bleach in the bucket; O-Cedar's FAQ advises against bleach (page 212 above; https://www.shopabunda.com/products/o-cedar-easywring-microfiber-spin-mop-bucket-floor-cleaning-system).
- ShopSavvy summary (secondary, UNVERIFIED): thinner plastic and less rigid pedal on newer units (https://shopsavvy.com/reviews-tldr/o-cedar-easywring-mop.md).
- Warranty: 1 year, receipt or registration required (https://www.farmandfleet.com/products/845770-o-cedar-easy-wring-spin-mop-and-bucket.html).
- Variants: EasyWring vs RinseClean; third-party pedal clutch parts found only for RinseClean version (Fruugo listing, not recommended).

### A5. Bedsure heated throw
- Safety: CPSC recall of Bedsure electric heating blankets and pads, dated March 9, 2023, controller fire/burn hazard, about 350,000 units; model BS-HB5060 (50x60 throw) is named (https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2023/Bedshe-International-Recalls-Bedsure-Electric-Heating-Blankets-and-Pads-Due-to-Fire-and-Thermal-Burn-Hazards-Recall-Alert, via earlier brief 2026-10-01; page not opened). - Controller faults: a third-party aggregator summarising reviews mentions F1/F4/FU error codes and stops heating until re-plugged (https://bestviewsreviews.com/electric-throws/bedsure-electric-heated-blanket-throw-diamond-patterned-shaggy-sherpa-fast-heating-electric-blanket-with-6-heating-levels-3h-auto-shut-off-etl-machine-washable-50x60-inches-harbor-mist-visit-the-bedsure-store/); UNVERIFIED, AI-generated summary, may derive from Amazon reviews, so do not use in the post.
- No Walmart or Target Bedsure reviews found; Bedsure appears to be sold mainly via Amazon and its own store, so non-Amazon owner evidence is thin. Alternatives sold at Walmart/Target (Biddeford, Mainstays, Black+Decker) have retailer reviews.
- 3-hour auto shut-off is listed as a feature by Bedsure; washing guidance: wash gently, an owner of a different brand reported one side died after two washes (Walmart, n/d, other brand).

## B. Added categories

### B1. Air fryer (Ninja, Cosori)
- Ninja nonstick: one Reddit-sourced quote (via a search summary, not opened, UNVERIFIED) of worn coating on a Ninja Smart pot/basket; another commenter says fine for years. Baskets are PTFE nonstick, crisper plate PTFE-free ceramic per a forum quote (search summaries; n/d). A law-firm investigation covers the PossibleCooker Pro coating, not air fryers (https://chimicles.com/ninja-possiblecooker-pro-nonstick-coating-investigation/).

- Cosori: Trustpilot reviewer got a free replacement unit after coating wore (https://at.trustpilot.com/review/cosori.co.uk, n/d); Slickdeals user went through "a couple of baskets" by 18 months on a Pro II and switched to Ninja (https://slickdeals.net/f/18026679, n/d); QVC forum user: propellant cooking spray peeled the coating, free replacement basket (https://community.qvc.com/t5/Kitchen/What-is-the-best-Air-Fryer/m-p/8516128, n/d). ### B2. Stand mixer (KitchenAid Artisan 5-qt)
- Oil/grease seepage: KitchenAid's own Best Buy Q&A answer says slight oil seepage can occur after sitting unused or tilted; heavy leaks need service (https://www.bestbuy.com/site/questions/kitchenaid-artisan-tilt-head-stand-mixer-bordeaux/6133648/question/5ac063e2-f942-3173-bf79-8a68054010b7, n/d). - Heavy dough: motor stalls/overheats, locking mechanism fails under thick dough, bowl wobble; KitchenAid says many wobble cases are bowl seating or load size and the manual limits kneading to speed 2 (Sam's Club reviews https://www.samsclub.com/reviews/product/17879361230 and /17900164733; https://www.kitchenaid.com/countertop-appliances/pinch-of-help/stand-mixer-troubleshooting; Macy's review of a replacement unit failing after about a year https://macys.com/shop/product/kitchenaid-ksm150psdr-artisan-series-5-quart-tilt-head-stand-mixer?ID=9542554; n/d).

- Variant: Artisan 5-qt tilt-head vs Classic 4.5-qt vs bowl-lift Professional; Black Friday sells Artisan in 20+ colors.
