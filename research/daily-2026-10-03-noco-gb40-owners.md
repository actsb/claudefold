## 4. Failure modes
- Self-discharge / recharge cadence. NOCO says it holds charge up to about a year; owners say that is optimistic. One summary: an owner found one red LED after 3.5 months; another says it did not hold charge beyond ~3 months after first recharge; claim that "most users" recharge monthly is a blog digest (no count) https://www.garagejournal.com/forum/threads/ls-this-noco-boost-plus-gb40-1000-amp-boost-starter-worth-having.510680/ , https://bushcraftusa.com/threads/noco-boost-jump-starter-how-long-do-they-hold-charge.386382/ [UNVERIFIED]. Practical advice in blogs: top up every 3 to 6 months https://www.batteryskills.com/how-often-should-i-charge-my-noco-gb40/ , https://automedian.com/how-often-to-charge-noco-gb40/ [UNVERIFIED; the "5% per month" figure is a blog statement, not NOCO].
- Degradation: summaries claim internal pack weakens at 12-18 months, esp. with irregular charging; one 2+ year owner who kept it charged said it later lacked power https://leadfootautomotive.com/car-maintenance/car-jump-starter/noco-boost-plus-gb40-review/ [UNVERIFIED].
- Out-of-warranty failure: a power button stopped working after warranty; NOCO reportedly replaces whole units rather than the battery; repair fees may apply https://helloportable.com/noco-gb40-problems/ , https://leadfootautomotive.com/car-maintenance/car-jump-starter/noco-jump-starter-battery-replacement/ [UNVERIFIED].
- Big engines. NOCO spec: up to 6.0L gas / 3.0L diesel; GB70 (2000A) is 8.0L gas / 6.0L diesel; GB20 is 4.0L, not for diesel [UNVERIFIED digest via https://www.automoblog.com/noco-gb40-vs-noco-gb70/ ; also https://no.co/gb40]. Owner reports: one person failed to start a light-duty pickup (1 success in 5 tries); another 5.6L V8 would not turn over, only a solenoid click, with an overheat blink (reddit/forum summary, URL not attributed; likely https://5thgenrams.com/community/threads/noco-gb40-booster-pack.53775/) [UNVERIFIED]. A Powerstroke forum summary says 6.7/6.0 diesels need far bigger packs (GB150-class) https://www.powerstroke.org/threads/noco-issues.1406941/ [UNVERIFIED]. Blog consensus: for big V8s or diesels, go to GB70.
- Cold weather: a 10F morning where the unit cycled lights and never lit the boost light https://www.garagejournal.com/forum/threads/bad-experience-with-noco-gb-40-in-cold-weather.381251/ ; a 5.7L Toyota worked but drained much of the charge; advice to warm it under a jacket https://reviewfriendly.com/noco-gb40-vs-70/ [UNVERIFIED]. Mixed overall.
- Dead-battery detection. Unit will not send current unless it sees a battery of at least 2V; below that you need Manual Override [SPEC] https://no.co/support/using-manual-override-gb40 . Override is held 3 seconds; it DISABLES spark-proof and reverse-polarity protection, so connect correctly first and power off before removing clamps [SPEC]. Owner case: a 2011 Kia Sportage 2.4 would not boost with the battery at 10.2V; commenters blamed a very drained battery rather than incompatibility https://forums.redflagdeals.com/noco-gb40-can-not-boost-2011-kia-sportage-2-4-engine-2439570/ [UNVERIFIED].
- Reverse polarity: red error LED, no output; swapping clamps clears it [SPEC] https://no.co/media/wysiwyg/downloads/User_Guides/Boost/GB40_User_Guide_03.07.2025A.pdf . Red LED blinking = internal temperature too high [SPEC, via summary].
- "Boost" button: needed only when detection fails; some vehicles may need clamps connected up to 30 seconds [SPEC via summary] https://www.etrailer.com/question-636438.html .
- Clamp cables are short; negative on engine hanger can be out of reach (a 2019 Honda Pilot example, a Porsche 718 thread). NOCO reportedly says extensions are not offered https://www.718forum.com/threads/2021-718-boxster-manual-jumping-battery-with-noco-gb40-cables-too-short.32691/ [UNVERIFIED].
- Slow recharge: USB 3 h at 2A, 6 h at 1A, 12 h at 0.5A [SPEC via summary]; no wall brick in the box; one Amazon Q&A answer reported 36 hours (exception) https://www.amazon.com/ask/questions/Tx13SRLJ4OIFL1T [UNVERIFIED].
- Count of jumps: advertised up to 20; one blog says real testing gave 10-15 (https://www.automoblog.com/noco-gb40-review/ was in that result set but the claim's exact source is unclear) [UNVERIFIED].
- Heat: NOCO says store indoors; storage spec -20C to +50C [SPEC]; blogs say a summer trunk can pass 65C so do not leave it in the car for weeks https://www.etrailer.com/question-748273.html [UNVERIFIED].
- 1-star themes (summary): clamps too short; failed to start after multiple tries while cables worked immediately; slow charging https://arcus-www.amazon.com/product-reviews/B015TKUPIC/ref=dp_prsubs_cr_2 [UNVERIFIED].
- Support sentiment: BBB and Trustpilot pages exist with complaints (not read) https://www.bbb.org/us/oh/solon/profile/battery-charging/noco-co-0312-8000611/complaints , https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/no.co [UNVERIFIED]; those mix chargers and company-wide issues.

## 5. Q&A people actually ask (from titles and snippets seen)
- How many jumps per charge? Answer: advertised up to 20 [SPEC]; real-life likely fewer with big engines/cold.
- How often to recharge? NOCO: up to a year idle; blogs say 3 to 6 months https://no.co/support/gb40 .
- Why won't my car start with it connected? Battery under ~2V, Manual Override, wait up to 30 s https://www.etrailer.com/question-636438.html .
- Can I store it in the trunk? Indoors recommended https://www.etrailer.com/question-748273.html and https://www.amazon.com/ask/questions/Tx26I82GLVIG4RQ .
- Side-post batteries (etrailer) https://www.etrailer.com/question-443590.html ; USB-C cable/charger (etrailer) https://www.etrailer.com/question-666768.html ; how long to charge https://manuals.plus/qa/6306484/How-long-does-it-take-to-fully-charge-the-NOCO-GB40-Boost-Plus-Lithium-Jump-Starter ; can it trickle-charge a battery (justanswer) https://www.justanswer.com/car/r3e1n-use-nono-boost-plus-just-battery-trickle.html .
- GB40 vs GB70 vs GB20: GB20 400A / 4.0L gas, no diesel, about $80; GB40 1000A / 6.0L gas, 3.0L diesel, about $100; GB70 2000A / 8.0L gas, 6.0L diesel, brighter light, 12V outlet [UNVERIFIED digest] https://www.automoblog.com/noco-gb40-vs-noco-gb70/ . Prices appear in 2026-era blog text and a Slickdeals thread shows sales around $69-$80 (not opened) https://slickdeals.net/f/15902383-amazon-has-noco-boost-plus-gb40-1000-amp-68-95 . Verify live.
- GB40 vs no-name "2000A" boxes: forum posters and blogs say peak amps are marketing; a 1000A peak pack is roughly 150-200 CCA-class, and big trucks need the 2000A class; the 2000A claim from cheap brands is called a joke by some https://www.tacomaworld.com/threads/the-truth-about-noco-and-other-similar-battery-jumpers.782441/ [UNVERIFIED]. Gooloo GP2000 is rated 2000A and 37 Wh vs GB40 24 Wh; blogs favor Gooloo for big V8/diesel on a budget, NOCO for build, safety track record and warranty https://redlinecargarage.com/resources/jump-starters/jump-starter-brands-compared/ , https://leadfootautomotive.com/car-maintenance/car-jump-starter/noco-vs-gooloo-jump-starter/ [UNVERIFIED]. Audew described as more entry-level [UNVERIFIED].

## 6. Counterfeit / fulfilment

## 7. Who should skip
(Derived from the above; opinion framing, mostly UNVERIFIED.)
- Owners of full-size V8 trucks, 6.2L+ gas, or any diesel bigger than 3.0L: buy GB70 or larger. Light-duty V8 (5.0-5.7L) results are mixed.
- People who live where winters sit at or below about 10-20F and park outside, unless they keep it indoors and warm it first.
- Anyone who will leave it in the glovebox/trunk year-round and never recharge it; set a 3-month reminder or choose a unit with a longer idle-hold claim.
- Anyone needing long clamp reach (cramped bays, rear/trunk batteries, opposite-side grounds).
- Buyers who want the cheapest big-engine power per dollar; no-name or Gooloo 2000A-class packs may deliver more but with different quality/safety trade-offs (UNVERIFIED).
- Anyone who expects it to revive a battery below ~2V without reading about Manual Override.
- Best fit: gas cars/crossovers/small V8s, short-reach everyday use, kept indoors and topped up.
