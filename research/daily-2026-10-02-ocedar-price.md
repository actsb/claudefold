## 7. When Amazon is not the cheapest
All based on the snippets above, not live checks:
1. Lowe's, Home Depot (HD-exclusive bundle) and Target listed $34.97 to $34.99 for the system. That is at or below Amazon's $37.20 snippet, and in line with Amazon's $34.96 from March.
2. Walmart's $34.96 rollback ties or beats Amazon's base-model list price, and its Oct 5-11 Deal Days window starts a day earlier than Big Deal Days and runs 5 days longer.
3. Costco warehouse prices on the 3-refill bundle ($24.97 to $25.97 at some warehouses) beat any Amazon price I found for a bundle with that many heads, but these are location-specific and may be short-lived (UNVERIFIED for any given warehouse).
4. BJ's at $20.99 after $5 off would be the lowest figure found, but the snippet is unclear on what is in the box. Check before relying on it.
5. Refills: Walmart 2-pack at $12.98 ($6.49 per head) vs Amazon 3-pack at $22.79 with S&S ($7.60 per head) vs generic compatible 3-packs at about $3 per head. Amazon is not the cheapest genuine-refill source in these snippets.
6. Amazon is usually cheapest for bundles with refills at the Prime event price. The $26.99 base price in Oct 2025 required a clip coupon, and the $37.99 June 2026 deal was a 2-refill bundle that was not cheaper per mop than shelf prices elsewhere.
7. Third-party marketplace and overseas look-alikes (AliExpress about $12 to $19) are cheaper up front, but do not use O-Cedar heads and have unverified quality.
