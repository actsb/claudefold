## 3. Official O-Cedar instructions (from snippets of ocedar.com pages; pages not fetched)

Official pages: Use & Care https://www.ocedar.com/use-and-care/easywring ; Mop head replacements https://www.ocedar.com/use-and-care/mop-head-replacements ; Washing guide https://www.ocedar.com/blog/o-cedar-mop-heads-washing-guide ; FAQ https://www.ocedar.com/blog/most-commonly-asked-questions and https://www.ocedar.com/blog/spin-mop-systems . Manual PDFs exist on Amazon/manualslib/manuals.plus (models TRY757, 153185, 166675, 148473; not read).
- Assembly: twist handle sections together, extend/lock handle (a manual-aggregator snippet says up to 48 in; CNN said 24 to 48; Tractor Supply listing said 51 in - discrepancy, UNVERIFIED). Align mop head with the frame and press until it clicks.
- Use: fill bucket (a manual-aggregator snippet mentions a MAX line), add cleaner if desired, dunk head, put in wringer basket, pump foot pedal 5 to 7 times (official use-and-care snippet); more pumps means drier head. Can use any floor cleaner or just water.
- Removing the head: step on it to detach so hands stay clean.
- Washing the head: official snippets say machine or hand wash in warm water, no bleach or fabric softener, lay flat or hang to dry, no high-heat tumble dry; the washing-guide snippet says machine wash up to 86 F; a manual-aggregator snippet said cold. Temperatures conflict (warm vs 86 F vs cold) - check the actual manual.
- Replacement: replace every 3 months or with the change of seasons (official Mop Head Replacements snippet). The FAQ also says a "rule of thumb" of a fresh refill after every clean (reusable heads). RinseClean heads are not interchangeable with original EasyWring heads (per third-party site).
- Surfaces: designed for sealed hard floors (hardwood, tile, vinyl, laminate); O-Cedar FAQ says safe on sealed hardwood. Warnings about unfinished, oiled, waxed or water-sensitive floors appeared for the O-Cedar PACS floor cleaner, not clearly for the mop itself; a statement that the mop must not be used on unsealed wood is inferred from "sealed floors" wording only - UNVERIFIED as an explicit manual warning.
- Storage: a snippet suggested keeping mop/bucket dry to prevent mold (not clearly official).
