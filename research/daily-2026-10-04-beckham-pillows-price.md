## When Amazon is not the cheapest
- Walmart.com reported $45.99 on one listing, but it may be a third-party seller; confirm "sold by".
- Utopia or Amazon Basics beat it on price if the shopper just wants a basic queen pillow.
- Amazon sale price around $41.96 versus eBay listings of $27.99 plus about $15 shipping: no savings. Do not recommend eBay.
- If a Walmart or BBB listing lists a price above Amazon's coupon price, Amazon wins; if coupon expired ($59.99), Walmart at $45.99 could win. Always say "check both".
