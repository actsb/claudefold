# Is That Black Friday Deal Real? Price and channels brief (researched 2026-11-20)

Research agent 3 (price and channels) for "Is That Black Friday Deal Real? How to Check a Sale Price in 2026".
Black Friday is Fri Nov 27, 2026; Cyber Monday is Mon Nov 30, 2026.

**Method and rules.** No Amazon page was opened and no Amazon price is recorded. Several articles quoted Amazon prices (MacRumors, Tom's Guide, 9to5Toys, The Drive, AndroidGuys, camelcamelcamel), and those numbers were left out on purpose. "Seen" values:
- **fetched**: I opened the store page with WebFetch on Nov 20, 2026.
- **snippet**: the price appeared only in a search-result snippet or index data, often undated.
- **deal post**: a Slickdeals or press post with its own date.

Pages that blocked the fetch on Nov 20: walmart.com (bot check), bestbuy.com (503), kitchenaid.com (503), no.co (403), kohls.com (403), homedepot.com (403). target.com, instantpot.com, bissell.com, redtigercam.com, apple.com and slickdeals.net did open.
