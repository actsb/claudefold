- Frequent Prime events with the lowest known prices ($59.99-$80).
- 30-day returns with easy shipping; Prime shipping; large review base (Amazon is NOCO's main channel; "Amazon's No. 1 jump starter" per Men's Journal).

- Caveat: third-party sellers and gray-market units; buy "ships from/sold by Amazon" or NOCO's storefront.

## When Amazon is not the cheapest
- Outside sale events, Amazon often sits at list ($99.95-$124.95); Home Depot ($91.21 reported) and Walmart (one snippet $63.99) may undercut it. Verify both.
- Auto-parts chains match list ~$99.99 and offer in-store pickup, with frequent coupons/ship-to-store (coupon amounts UNVERIFIED).
- Walmart can drop below Amazon during Black Friday (Walmart business page referenced "Black Friday Deals for 2024"); Autoblog ran "Save $35 at Walmart" (date unknown).
- Right after an event the price bounces back; waiting for Black Friday/Prime Big Deal Days historically saves $20-$40.
