## 3. FTC / deal-validity advice

- FTC Guides Against Deceptive Pricing, 16 C.F.R. Part 233 (summarized by law-firm sites such as https://revisionlegal.com/internet-law/internet/ecommerce-deceptive-pricing-legal-rules/ ; FTC primary text not fetched): a "former price" comparison is deceptive unless the higher price was a genuine price offered for a reasonably substantial period recently. FTC doesn't define the period; some states use 28 days in 90 (state-level, snippet). Fine to cite as: a "was" price only means something if it was a real recent selling price.
- FTC consumer alert, "How to avoid an online shopping scam this holiday season", Nov 2025: https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2025/11/how-avoid-online-shopping-scam-holiday-season . Supports: scam ads impersonate real brands at unusually low prices; research seller; pay by credit card; check shipping/return terms. (Nov 2025 date from URL path.)
- Older FTC tips (use price comparison sites; save order records): https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2018/11/happy-holidays-shopping-tips-ftc ; https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2015/11/ftc-offers-top-10-holiday-shopping-tips-consumers-advice-online-retailers
- Google-specific price-history/price-tracking advice: not searched successfully. UNVERIFIED; omit unless confirmed.
